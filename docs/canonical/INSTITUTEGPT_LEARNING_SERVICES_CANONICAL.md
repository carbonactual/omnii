# InstituteGPT — Learning, Skills, Certification & Lifelong Services

**Status: CANONICAL DESIGN — 2026-09-06**

## 1. Product and layer boundary

`Education Layer → InstituteGPT`

InstituteGPT is the first-class product of the Education Layer and its canonical education GPT/service. It owns the universal learner-facing education experience and composes native services for learning, skills, competencies, practice, labs, projects, assessment, recognition, credentials, CPD and lifelong progression.

There is **no separate `Advance` product**. Advancement is a native InstituteGPT service concept.

OpenEd Bot, Notebook and Exams are sibling Education Layer products/compositions. They integrate with InstituteGPT and share OMNII/Education Layer semantics without redefining them.

HAPI, Root, Actual, Evidence, Pulse, Atlas, Opportunities and other OMNII foundations/services remain shared ecosystem architecture. InstituteGPT consumes or integrates with them; it does not own or redefine them.

## 2. Curation at application and onboarding

Application and onboarding are not only account creation. InstituteGPT builds a governed learner context and continuously curates learning around the learner's interests, goals, needs and direction: what they like, what they want, what they need and what they are trying to become.

Curation may use:

`interests | goals | education stage | prior knowledge | skills | experience | career direction | subjects | preferred formats | pace | available time | language | accessibility | device/connectivity | location context | budget | provider preferences | credential goals | professional-body requirements | CPD requirements | learning-style preferences | support preferences | lab preferences | project preferences | assessment preferences`

The learner can revise these preferences at any time. Curation is adaptive, explainable and reversible. Preferences are not a license to make hidden high-impact decisions.

For children, guardian/educator controls, age suitability, safeguarding, privacy, communications and retention rules apply. Childhood exploration must not silently become an adult employment profile.

## 3. Universal learning value chain

`discover → apply/onboard → understand learner context → diagnose → advise → curate → set goal → build/revise path → select learning experience → learn → practice → lab/simulation → project → experience → demonstrate → assess → recognize → badge/certificate/credential → portfolio/record → opportunity → work/practice → CPD → renew/recertify → reskill/upskill → lifelong learning`

This is a graph: branches, loops, stacking, recognition of prior learning and direct entry are supported.

## 4. Learning Experience

The universal learning unit is a **Learning Experience**, which can contain:

`lesson | reading | media | AI dialogue | teacher instruction | tutoring | exercise | quiz | discussion | lab | simulation | project | fieldwork | mentorship | apprenticeship | internship | assessment`

Courses and programmes are compositions of learning experiences rather than the only possible form of learning.

## 5. Labs and demonstrated capability

Labs are a major InstituteGPT capability connecting knowledge to demonstrated capability.

They may be:

`coding | cloud | AI | data | cybersecurity | science | engineering | robotics | electronics | medicine | agriculture | manufacturing | finance | GIS | media | design | language | vocational | field | simulation | VR/AR | digital twin | hardware-connected | remote`

Environments may include sandboxes, browser runtimes, notebooks, containers, virtual machines, cloud workspaces, remote hardware, simulation engines and physical facilities.

Lab outputs may become governed artifacts, evidence or assessment signals. Simulation/sandbox state remains distinguishable from Actual professional practice and authorization.

## 6. Skills and competency progression

`knowledge → skill → competency → practice → evidence → demonstrated competency → achievement → qualification → professional authorization`

Skill states may include:

`self_declared | learning | practiced | assessed | demonstrated | externally_verified | expired | superseded`

A course does not automatically prove mastery. A badge does not automatically grant regulated professional authority. Evidence is not authority.

Recognition of prior learning may incorporate formal, informal, workplace, apprenticeship, internship, SIWES, portfolio, project, simulation and supervised-practice evidence where the responsible authority permits it.

## 7. Credentials, badges and certification

InstituteGPT supports:

`participation | completion | open badge | microcredential | certificate | professional certificate | diploma | degree reference | qualification | professional certification | CPD recognition`

InstituteGPT may issue configured ecosystem-native achievements and reference or verify external credentials. External issuers, educational institutions, professional bodies and regulators retain authority for their credentials and licences.

Portable targets include:

`1EdTech Open Badges | CLR | W3C Verifiable Credentials`

plus applicable national, institutional and professional frameworks.

The authority boundary is explicit:

`learning → evidence/demonstration → recognition/credential → professional/legal authorization`

A downstream state MUST NOT be inferred merely because an upstream state exists.

## 8. Learning Wallet and Skills Passport

**Learning Wallet** provides a portable collection/reference of learning records, assessments, projects, achievements, badges, certificates, credentials and evidence.

**Skills Passport** provides a provenance-aware view of declared, learned, practiced, assessed, demonstrated and externally verified skills/competencies.

Neither replaces the authority of an external issuer or regulator.

## 9. Pathway Builder

InstituteGPT can construct and revise paths from:

`goal + learner context + diagnostics + prerequisites + competencies + learning resources + labs + projects + assessments + credentials + opportunities + renewal requirements`

Paths may be native, partner, institutional, employer, professional-body, government, learner-authored or AI-generated. AI-generated paths are recommendations where human or legal authority is required.

## 10. Provider ecosystem and EdTech interoperability

InstituteGPT uses replaceable provider adapters/profiles for:

`GitHub Education | GitHub Skills | OpenAI Education/Academy | Anthropic Education | Microsoft Learn | AWS Educate | NVIDIA DLI | Google | IBM SkillsBuild | Cisco Networking Academy | Salesforce Trailhead | Coursera | Udemy | Alison | Coursiv | edX | FutureLearn | LinkedIn Learning | Khan Academy | DataCamp | Pluralsight | Codecademy | freeCodeCamp | professional bodies | universities | TVET providers | employer academies | governments | future providers`

Provider integrations may expose catalogs, enrollment/deep links, progress references, labs, assessments, achievements, badges and credential references according to actual provider interfaces, contracts and permissions.

Provider branding/source and issuer lineage remain visible.

Standards adapters may cover:

`1EdTech Open Badges | CLR | W3C Verifiable Credentials | QTI | CASE | LTI | OneRoster | Edu-API | Caliper | UNESCO OER references | ESCO | national/regional qualification frameworks | professional competency frameworks`

Standards and providers are interoperability participants, not constitutional authorities.

## 11. Child-to-lifelong coverage

InstituteGPT supports:

`early childhood | primary | secondary | youth | vocational/technical | tertiary | postgraduate | doctoral | professional entry | workplace | career transition | entrepreneurship | professional development | community learning | lifelong learning | AI/agent learning`

The semantic substrate is shared; governance, content, permissions and safety are age/context dependent.

## 12. Education capabilities kept inside InstituteGPT

These are reusable capabilities, not additional products by default:

`discovery | counselling | admissions | enrollment | curriculum | teaching | tutoring | mentoring | coaching | accessibility | learner support | assessment | marking | moderation | proctoring | invigilation | learner success | wellbeing | safeguarding | research | libraries | internships | apprenticeships | SIWES | work-integrated learning | administration | interoperability | funding | government education | AI governance | marketplace | pathway building | learning wallet | skills passport`

A capability becomes a separate first-class product only when it acquires an independently meaningful semantic, operational and ecosystem boundary.

## 13. Education product composition

`InstituteGPT = universal education, learning, skills, certification and lifelong progression GPT`

`OpenEd Bot = InstituteGPT + open/distance/flexible education composition`

`Notebook = InstituteGPT + study/research/teaching/evidence workspace`

`Exams = InstituteGPT + formal examination/testing orchestration`

NOUN Bot is an institution-specific OpenEd Bot deployment and does not create a separate universal education ontology.

## 14. Opportunity connection

Learning evidence may inform the universal Opportunities layer for:

`jobs | internships | apprenticeships | scholarships | fellowships | projects | research | contracts | entrepreneurship | promotion`

InstituteGPT supplies learning/competency context; the Opportunities layer, employer, institution or other legitimate authority makes the external opportunity decision.

## 15. AI learners

AI/agents may learn, practice, simulate, benchmark and receive governed capability state. AI learning is distinct from human qualification and does not automatically grant human or regulated authority.

The AI learning continuum can include:

`AI awareness → AI literacy → prompting → workflows → agents → tool use → MCP → retrieval → evaluation → safety/security → AI product development → AI engineering → research → governance → leadership`

AI capability state is not automatically a human credential, professional authorization or legal identity claim.

## 16. Governance

`human authority remains authoritative`

`external issuers retain credential authority`

`provider adapters are replaceable`

`AI recommendations are not automatically authoritative decisions`

`simulation and sandbox activity do not silently become Actual evidence`

`learner and child data remain subject to authorization, privacy, safeguarding and retention policy`

`HAPI/Root/Actual/Evidence/Pulse/Atlas/Opportunities remain shared OMNII architecture`

Consequential transitions use IO and the universal governed runtime:

`identity → authority → policy → capability → resource → dependency → execution → state transition → event → audit`

## 18. Architectural classification rule

Every new InstituteGPT/Education feature MUST first be classified:

`reuse shared OMNII capability → configure → compose education capabilities → extend an Education domain contract → create a genuinely reusable shared capability → create a first-class Education product only when a separate boundary exists → constitutional amendment only if foundational semantics are genuinely missing`

This prevents product proliferation, semantic duplication, provider lock-in and architectural drift.

## 19. Non-negotiable invariants

1. InstituteGPT is a product of the Education Layer.
2. InstituteGPT is the canonical education GPT/service.
3. There is no separate Advance product.
4. Learning, skills, competency, labs, practice, projects, credentials, badges, CPD, Learning Wallet, Skills Passport and lifelong advancement are native InstituteGPT services/capabilities.
5. OpenEd Bot, Notebook and Exams are sibling Education Layer products/compositions.
6. NOUN Bot is an institution-specific OpenEd Bot deployment.
7. GitHub Education and other providers are replaceable adapters/integrations.
8. HAPI, Root, Actual, Evidence, Pulse, Atlas and Opportunities remain shared OMNII architecture.
9. External issuers/regulators retain authority for external credentials and regulated licences.
10. Education-specific records use the universal OMNII object envelope and graph.
11. Simulation, future and unknown states remain distinguishable from Actual.
12. Human/legal authority cannot be replaced by AI optimization.
13. The entire layer remains modular, localizable, interoperable, reusable and vendor-neutral.
