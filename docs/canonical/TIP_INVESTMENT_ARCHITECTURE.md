# TIP — Comprehensive Investment Architecture

**Ecosystem:** Carbon Actual / OMNII  
**Product:** TIP — Trade, Investment & Value Markets  
**Status:** CANONICAL EXTENSION — approved  
**Purpose:** Define investment as the systematic deployment and stewardship of capital or value-bearing resources toward future economic, strategic, environmental or productive outcomes.

---

## 1. Investment Definition

Investment is the authorized commitment of capital, resources, capabilities, rights or capacity with an expectation of future value, income, strategic benefit, preservation, resilience, impact or other defined outcome.

Investment is distinct from trading:

```text
TRADING
= exchange / position-taking / risk-transfer / price-discovery activity

INVESTMENT
= capital allocation / ownership or exposure / stewardship / future-outcome thesis
```

The same Market Object may be traded and invested in, but the intent, time horizon, governance, measurement and lifecycle can differ.

## 33. Investment and Trading Integration

TIP must permit one object to move between investment and trading states without losing provenance.

Example:

```text
INVESTMENT THESIS
      ↓
OWNERSHIP / EXPOSURE
      ↓
MONITOR
      ↓
TRADE / HEDGE / REBALANCE
      ↓
SETTLE
      ↓
CONTINUE HOLDING
      ↓
EXIT
```

The trading engine executes permitted market actions; the investment engine maintains the longer-horizon thesis and mandate.

---

## 34. Canonical Rule

> **Investment is the disciplined allocation and stewardship of capital or productive capacity toward defined future value, income, strategic, resilience or impact outcomes; trading is one mechanism through which investment positions may be entered, adjusted, hedged or exited.**

The TIP architecture must accommodate both without collapsing them into the same workflow or data model.
