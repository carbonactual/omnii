# OMNII — Global Resource & Capacity Exchange Intelligence

**Status:** CANONICAL CAPABILITY SCOPE  
**Scope:** Global-by-default  
**Layer:** Shared ecosystem resource/capacity intelligence

## Definition

Global Resource & Capacity Exchange Intelligence is the reusable capability for discovering, representing, forecasting and coordinating available, idle, latent, reserved and future capacity across resources that can be shared, allocated, rented, leased, contracted or otherwise accessed.

## Resource universe

Factories, production lines, machinery, tools, warehouses, land, buildings, vehicles, vessels, aircraft, energy, compute, communications, facilities, processing capacity and skilled human capability.

## Capacity states

```text
unknown
available
partially available
idle
future
reserved
committed
constrained
unavailable
expired
```

Each state is time-bounded and evidence-backed.

## Exchange intelligence

Discover capacity that can be:

- shared
- rented
- leased
- booked
- pooled
- subcontracted
- fractionally allocated
- released
- aggregated

Support capacity owners, users, operators and intermediary services without assuming ownership.

## Utilization

Measure planned versus actual utilization, idle time, release windows, bottlenecks, overbooking risk and unproductive capacity. Identify opportunities for better utilization through Matching and Global Opportunity.

## Forecasting

Represent expected future capacity and likely demand, clearly separating observed schedules from forecasts and hypotheses.

## Allocation

Produce possible allocations while preserving authority, reservations, contractual priority, safety and jurisdictional constraints. Actual commitments remain governed execution.

## Interfaces

Connects to Supply & Capacity Graph, Global Sourcing, Opportunity, Matching, Network/Corridor, Market Intelligence, Capability Development, finance, TIP/IO and Pulse.

## Boundary

It does not replace the canonical Supply & Capacity Graph and does not become an exchange operator, landlord, employer, carrier, power market or equipment owner.

## Canonical invariant

> **Capacity becomes economically visible when its availability, timing, condition and access rights are represented accurately enough to be discovered and coordinated.**
