# OMNII — Global Opportunity Capability

**Status:** CANONICAL CAPABILITY SCOPE  
**Scope:** Global-by-default  
**Layer:** Shared ecosystem capability; consumed by Desk, TIP, HAPI World, institutional systems and future products

## 1. Constitutional definition

**Global Opportunity** is the reusable ecosystem capability for detecting, describing, qualifying, composing, exposing and advancing a potentially valuable possibility arising from demand, supply, capability, assets, gaps, timing, relationships, information, infrastructure, capital, technology or changes in conditions.

Opportunity answers:

> **“What could become possible or valuable here, for whom, under what conditions, and what would it take to make it real?”**

Opportunity is distinct from Sourcing. Sourcing begins with a requirement and seeks a source or route to satisfy it. Opportunity begins with a signal, possibility, surplus, capability, mismatch, gap, demand, asset or changing condition and determines what can be created, connected, developed, exchanged or unlocked.

## 2. Global-by-default rule

Opportunities are not bounded to the jurisdiction in which they are discovered.

The opportunity graph may discover value across:

```text
local
→ territorial
→ national
→ regional
→ continental
→ global
```

Jurisdiction is a condition on execution, not a limit on discovery.

## 7. Hidden opportunity detection

The opportunity engine should identify latent possibilities such as:

### Idle capacity

A producer is operating below economic capacity. Demand can be found for the unused capacity.

### Fragmented demand

Many small buyers need compatible goods/services. Aggregation can create a commercially meaningful order.

### Fragmented supply

Many small providers jointly satisfy a larger requirement.

### Capability gaps

Demand exists without adequate local capability. The opportunity may be import, local production, licensing, training, shared capacity, refurbishment or investment.

### Price/value asymmetry

Material differences exist across markets after accounting for freight, duties, finance, quality and risk.

### Asset underutilization

Land, machines, buildings, vehicles, warehouses, energy or compute are underused.

### Backhaul

A route has unused return capacity that could carry compatible goods or services.

### Service substitution

Users buy expensive assets when what they actually need is access to the output/service.

### Product substitution

An unavailable product may be replaced by an acceptable equivalent, alternate process or local manufacture.

## 13. Opportunity marketplace vs Opportunity layer

Global Opportunity is not merely a public listings marketplace.

It includes:

- private opportunities
- permissioned institutional opportunities
- participant-only opportunities
- system-generated opportunities
- public opportunities
- tender/procurement opportunities
- strategic development opportunities
- latent opportunities not yet offered to a market

Presentation and permissions are product/channel concerns; opportunity semantics remain shared.

## 23. Governance and authority

Opportunity discovery may be automated.

Opportunity commitment may not be silently automated where human authority, regulated action, financial commitment, legal acceptance or material consequences are involved.

ABBA may discover, prioritize, explain and orchestrate proposed actions within its authority boundary. Human Authority/Seal and jurisdictional permissions remain decisive wherever required.

## 26. Non-goals

Global Opportunity does not become:

- a compulsory marketplace
- a bank/investment adviser
- a regulator
- an employer of record
- a legal authority
- a replacement for sourcing
- a replacement for TIP

It is the ecosystem's **discover-what-could-be capability**.

## 27. Canonical invariant

> **Opportunity is possibility with a value pathway: it identifies what could become possible or valuable from signals, assets, capabilities, needs, gaps and relationships. It is distinct from sourcing a known requirement.**
