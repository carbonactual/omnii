# GOVERNANCE

Governance is the constitutional authority layer governing rules, rights, responsibilities, decisions, constraints, accountability, and dispute resolution across OMNII.

## Purpose
Ensure that power, authority, and collective decision-making remain explicit, bounded, auditable, and accountable.

## Canonical Concerns
- Authority
- Rights and responsibilities
- Policy
- Decision rights
- Delegation
- Accountability
- Dispute resolution
- Constitutional change

## Invariant
No actor, agent, product, or institution may exercise authority beyond its defined scope without explicit escalation or delegation.
