# HAPi World — Canonical Operating Model

HAPi World is the whole operating universe of the Carbon Actual ecosystem. `CANON.md` is its supreme law.

## World model

HAPi World maintains a 1:1 inspected mirror of Earth using Earth's clock and geography as its reference frame. Digital entities do not float outside the world model: humans, organizations, AI entities, agents, assets, liabilities, services, locations, events, and value movements are represented as governed ecosystem objects with identity and provenance.

## Know before use

The world is closed to unknown operational input. An object may be discovered before it is trusted, but it cannot be used as trusted state until its identity, provenance, verification state, authority context, and relevant constraints are known.

Discovery therefore precedes admission, and admission precedes operational use.

## One object, many representations

A canonical object is not duplicated because it appears in another product. Views, registries, ledgers, tokens, fractions, APIs, dashboards, indexes, and specialized products reference the same underlying object.

A representation can add context, rights, precision, accessibility, or presentation. It cannot silently become a competing source of truth.

## Living entities inside the world

Organizations may mint AI entities. HAPi/NAIRE may mint AI entities and NGIN may operate organizational/territorial AI capabilities. Agents are ecosystem participants. All of them remain subordinate to SealGrant authority and the constitutional boundary established by the human administrator.

## Financial spine

HAPi Bank provides the financial operating substrate for the world through one coherent economic system:

`banking infrastructure → value/IOLedger → economic intelligence`

Value may be represented internally, settled through governed pathways, and converted at an explicit real-world boundary. Conversion does not collapse the distinction between internal value and external money.

## No waste

Pulse observes value signals. Ash captures non-productive residue and recoverable waste intelligence. Phoenix handles malicious or prohibited input according to security policy. Each outcome retains lineage to the originating action or canonical object.

The purpose is not to erase information; it is to prevent hidden waste, hidden duplication, and unaccountable resource consumption.

## Human–AI balance

AI operates the computational and orchestration middle layer. Humans retain constitutional administration, authority, curation, consent, and ultimate control. Automation expands capability without transferring sovereignty from the human administrator.

## Execution

The standard operating sequence is:

`know → verify → contextualize → establish capability → establish authority → assess mission → obtain required consent → execute → observe → record → reconcile`

Any lower-level interface that bypasses a required higher-level gate is non-conformant.
