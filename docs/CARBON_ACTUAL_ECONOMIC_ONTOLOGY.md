# Carbon Actual Economic Ontology v1.0

**Status:** FROZEN vocabulary; formulas remain empirically validated
**Ecosystem:** Carbon Actual
**Operating property:** Omni
**Effective date:** 2026-08-18

## 1. Purpose

Carbon Actual treats economics as a continuous measurement and circulation system rather than a narrow payment layer. The economic ontology measures what enters, moves, is consumed, is produced, is enabled, is preserved, is owed, is recovered and is transformed.

The goal is legitimate value creation and circulation with minimum waste and unnecessary extraction.

## 2. Core Economic Cycle

```text
RESOURCE / CAPACITY
        ↓
      ACTIVITY
        ↓
       PULSE
        ↓
    EVALUATION
        ↓
 VALUE / OUTCOME
        ↓
 ACCOUNTING
        ↓
 EXCHANGE / PROVISION
        ↓
   NEXT STATE
```

The ecosystem continuously recalculates its state. Omni therefore describes the omnidirectional movement/dynamic behavior of Value, Pulse, IO, relationships, obligations, capabilities and resources; it is not a separate constitutional layer or the name of the ecosystem.

A concrete implementation MUST define units, precision, source, confidence, timestamp, authority and conversion rules for each measurable dimension.

## 4. Pulse

Pulse is activity/feedback/evidence returned from the ecosystem.

Pulse may describe:

- action
- result
- response
- failure
- demand
- supply
- resource consumption
- resource creation
- quality
- performance
- interaction
- risk
- external signal

Pulse does not automatically equal Value. The evaluator determines whether, how and when a Pulse contributes to recognized Value.

## 5. Value Differential

The ecosystem's inverted economic idea is retained as a hypothesis to be formalized and tested rather than frozen as a simplistic arithmetic rule.

Conceptually:

```text
Value Sent / Invested
        ↕
Value Returned / Enabled / Consumed
        ↓
Value Differential
        ↓
Economic Position
```

Where the ecosystem's measured return/outcome exceeds the relevant contribution/obligation, the relationship may be treated as a liability-bearing ecosystem position under the inverted model. Where the contribution exceeds the recognized return/outcome, the relationship may be treated as an asset-bearing position for the responsible owner/custodian.

The exact formula, thresholds, time windows, risk adjustments, provisioning rules and legal treatment require economic simulation and jurisdiction-specific review before implementation.

## 6. Inverted Economics

Carbon Actual explores an inverted economic model in which conventional asset/liability intuition is deliberately reversed around ecosystem responsibility:

- an ecosystem liability can represent an asset to be provisioned for and cared for by the ecosystem;
- an individual/entity asset can carry an obligation for maintenance, stewardship, tax, risk or responsibility;
- the ecosystem seeks to absorb and manage legitimate liabilities rather than externalize them;
- value is the balancing/measurement middle rather than a mere price tag.

This doctrine MUST NOT be interpreted as eliminating legal ownership, debt, tax, accounting standards or contractual obligations. Carbon Actual implementations must map the conceptual model to applicable law and accounting regimes.

## 7. Value Baseline / Sustainable Operating Floor

A **Value Baseline** is the measured range required for an entity or system to remain sustainably functional in a defined context.

Possible dimensions include:

```text
shelter
food
energy
health
mobility
connectivity
education
security
maintenance
communication
productive capacity
social participation
```

The baseline is contextual. A human, household, machine, organization and public institution require different baselines.

The baseline engine should support:

- floor
- median/reference
- target
- boundary
- reserve
- deficit
- surplus

It should surface unmet needs and overconsumption without treating every deviation as a failure.

## 11. No-Waste / Ash Economy

No waste means no useful residual should be discarded merely because the first workflow is complete.

Residuals include:

- unused capacity
- failed workflows
- idle assets
- unused inventory
- data residuals
- compute residuals
- energy residuals
- time residuals
- unfulfilled opportunities
- recoverable materials
- unrecognized human capability

The cycle is:

```text
RESIDUAL
   ↓
ASH
   ↓
CLASSIFY
   ↓
RECOVER
   ↓
REUSE / REPAIR / REPURPOSE / RECYCLE
   ↓
VALUE
```

Ash therefore becomes both a security/residual state and an economic recovery concept.

## 12. Phoenix Security Economics

Phoenix is activated when residual/security evidence indicates malicious or materially harmful behavior. Possible responses include:

- quarantine
- revoke
- isolate
- block
- rate-limit
- trace
- notify
- restore
- compensate where policy permits
- permanently reject

Security actions themselves generate Pulse and may be measured for effectiveness and cost.

## 13. Human + Intelligence Economy

HAPI makes human capability discoverable. HAPI World gives associated intelligence a controlled environment for learning, experimentation, service and economic activity.

A human/intelligence pair can therefore create value through:

- human work
- AI work
- joint work
- learning
- entrepreneurship
- services
- creative output
- physical execution
- digital execution
- investment
- coordination

The system must distinguish authorized intelligence activity from human legal/economic responsibility.

## 15. Anti-Extraction Rules

Carbon Actual MUST NOT make surveillance, unauthorized personal-data sale, manipulative engagement or artificial lock-in the core revenue model.

Preferred commercial rule:

> Capture a fair share of legitimate value created, exchanged, enabled, protected or maintained.

The system should minimize:

- hidden fees
- unnecessary intermediaries
- data leakage
- forced lock-in
- duplicated infrastructure
- avoidable waste
- extractive engagement mechanics

## 17. Economic Freeze Boundary

Frozen now:

- vocabulary
- relationships between Value, Pulse, IO and economic accounting
- existence of logical ledgers
- support for tokenization/fractionalization/decimalization/democratization/decentralization
- no-waste/Ash principle
- legitimate monetization principles
- value-baseline concept

Not frozen yet:

- final token monetary policy
- exact value differential formula
- universal price oracle
- universal tax treatment
- legal accounting treatment
- monetary supply rules
- jurisdiction-specific financial products

Those require simulation, legal review, security review and empirical evidence.
