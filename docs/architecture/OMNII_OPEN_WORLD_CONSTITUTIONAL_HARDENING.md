# OMNII Open-World Constitutional Hardening

**Status: CANONICAL ARCHITECTURAL HARDENING**

## Goal
Ensure OMNII can represent any present or future scenario without enumerating scenarios, while preserving the distinction between reality, representation, observation, claims, inference, plans, decisions, actions, outcomes and evidence.

## Constitutional boundaries

1. **Reality ≠ representation.** A record is not the thing it represents.
2. **Observation ≠ claim ≠ inference.** Evidence provenance and confidence attach to knowledge assertions, not to reality itself.
3. **Plan ≠ execution.** Proposal, prediction, simulation, decision, action and outcome are distinct states/objects.
4. **State is transitional.** State changes preserve predecessor, successor, actor, authority, conditions, evidence, time and reversibility.
5. **Causality is typed.** Causes, contributors, enablers, preventers, triggers, correlations, hypotheses and disputed causes are distinct.
6. **Time is multi-dimensional.** Occurrence, observation, recording, effectiveness and supersession are distinct timestamps.
7. **Unknown is valid.** Unknown or unclassified objects are representable without forced classification.
8. **No silent loss.** Important state/value/resource transitions are traceable, reconcilable and recoverable where recovery is possible.
9. **Authority is explicit.** Capability never implies authority; automation cannot silently expand authority.
10. **Privacy is contextual.** Connectivity does not imply visibility or disclosure.
11. **Ontology evolves without destroying history.** Concepts, schemas and mappings are versioned and superseded rather than silently rewritten.
12. **Products are compositions.** Products, services and workflows select capabilities from the shelf; they do not redefine the constitutional substrate.

## Scenario grammar

A scenario is represented by composable primitives:

`Entity → Context → Condition → Intent → Decision → Action → Event → State Transition → Observation → Claim/Inference → Effect/Outcome → Feedback → Recovery/Continuity`

The graph may branch, merge, loop, reverse, fail, recover, or remain hypothetical. Counterfactual and simulated branches must be marked as non-actual.

## Knowledge and epistemic model

Knowledge records carry:
- subject/object references;
- assertion type;
- source/provenance;
- observed/recorded/effective times;
- confidence;
- verification state;
- dispute state;
- supersession state.

## Causality model

Canonical relation types include:
`causes`, `contributes_to`, `enables`, `prevents`, `requires`, `triggers`, `correlates_with`, `intended_to`, `suspected_cause`, `inferred_cause`, `disputed_cause`, `unknown_cause`.

Causal claims require provenance and confidence and must remain distinguishable from observed sequence.

## Quality and completeness

Completion is evaluated across independent dimensions:
- structural;
- evidence;
- logical/consistency;
- operational;
- regulatory/standards;
- safety/risk;
- economic/value;
- continuity/recovery.

A result may be usable while incomplete. The system must expose gaps instead of manufacturing completeness.

## No-loss semantics

“No loss” means **no silent loss**, not an impossible guarantee that reality cannot destroy resources. Significant transitions carry origin, destination, quantity/state, custodian/actor, expected outcome, actual outcome, evidence, reconciliation status and exception/recovery information.

## Open-world extension

Unknown objects and capabilities enter through a provisional extension path:
`Unknown → Observed Properties → Provisional Classification → Evidence/Hypotheses → Candidate Capability/Object → Constitutional Review → Canonicalization or Rejection`.

No future product or domain should require a new foundational ontology merely because its instances were not anticipated.
