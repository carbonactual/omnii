# OMNII — Global Economic Outcome Intelligence

**Status:** CANONICAL CAPABILITY SCOPE  
**Scope:** Global-by-default  
**Layer:** Shared economic outcome, variance and learning intelligence

## Definition

Global Economic Outcome Intelligence is the reusable capability for measuring what actually happened after a sourcing, opportunity, procurement, financing, logistics, capability-development or other economic pathway, and converting results into reusable learning.

## Outcome universe

Measure:

```text
cost
value
revenue
savings
time
quality
utilization
capacity
risk
failure
loss
recovery
market access
employment/capability effects
participant benefit
resilience
environmental effects where measured
```

## Expected vs actual

Compare recommendation/forecast/commitment with observed outcome, preserving assumptions and attribution limits.

## Leakage detection

Identify avoidable loss from excess intermediaries, poor quality, delay, idle capacity, route failure, financing cost, returns, disputes, fraud indicators and other measurable variance.

## Performance learning

Build context-specific performance history for providers, routes, products, capacity, financing paths and workflows without reducing the result to a single opaque reputation number.

## Outcome lifecycle

```text
expected → committed → executing → observed → accepted → settled → analyzed → learned
```

Failed, partial and inconclusive outcomes remain first-class states.

## Pulse and Value

Outcome intelligence contributes evidence to Pulse, Value/VCE, transaction reputation, Sourcing, Opportunity, Matching, Market Intelligence and Resilience. It does not redefine those canonical layers.

## Counterfactual discipline

Distinguish observed outcome from estimated avoided loss or hypothetical alternative. Do not claim causal impact when the evidence only supports correlation or comparison.

## Interfaces

Connects to TIP/IO, Evidence/Chain-of-Custody, Sourcing, Opportunity, Matching, Market Intelligence, Resource/Capacity, Resilience, Value/VCE and Pulse.

## Boundary

Not a financial auditor, regulator, accounting authority, rating agency or universal causal inference engine.

## Canonical invariant

> **Outcome Intelligence closes the loop between what the ecosystem expected, what actually occurred, what value was realized or lost, and what should be learned next.**
