# NAIRE + NGIN Operating Environment Composition

**Status:** Canonical composition doctrine
**Purpose:** Reconcile legacy Carbon Actual architecture with current OMNII constitutional semantics.

## Position

NAIRE and NGIN are not new constitutional kernels. They are reusable operating-environment compositions built on the OMNII Floor/Common Layer.

- **NAIRE** = Human Operating Environment.
- **NGIN** = Organization / Government / Entity Operating Environment.

They are concurrent and complementary. They may be independently authoritative within their legitimate domain while remaining subject to OMNII constitutional contracts, applicable law, institutional authority, provenance, security and evidence requirements.

## Inheritance

Both environments inherit:

`Identity + Relationship + Intent + Capability + Resource + Authority + Authorization + Workflow + Execution + State + Event + Evidence + Value + Continuity + Integration`

Neither environment creates a competing universal graph, alternate authority hierarchy, alternate economic constitution, or independent constitutional ledger.

## NAIRE

NAIRE composes human-side capabilities such as identity/context, personal continuity, human agency, communication, relationships, learning, work, economic participation, service, movement, health and other lawful personal domains.

Where a product requires human-side data, it consumes authorized NAIRE contracts rather than copying the human's canonical identity or continuity records.

## NGIN

NGIN composes organization, institution, government, business, association, territorial and entity-side capabilities such as registry, governance, personnel, assets, services, forms, workflows, permits, compliance, finance, infrastructure and operational management.

Products consume authorized NGIN contracts rather than creating parallel institutional truths.

## Interaction

A product may use:

- NAIRE only;
- NGIN only;
- both; or
- neither, where its domain is independently bounded.

Cross-environment interaction is represented through explicit relationships, authority, consent, context, evidence and I/O. Neither side is silently allowed to infer authority from a relationship alone.

## Canonical boundary

`NAIRE / NGIN → Common Layer → Product / Institutional Composition`

is a composition relationship, not a constitutional sequence.

The environments remain replaceable implementations beneath stable contracts. Their internal storage, deployment topology, vendors and service providers are not constitutional semantics.
