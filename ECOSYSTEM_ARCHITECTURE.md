## Why this document was superseded

The earlier architecture treated Carbon Actual too much like a conventional software/agent stack. The frozen architecture is broader and more compressed: Carbon Actual is the ecosystem; Omni is a dynamic operating property; products are compositions of reusable capabilities; humans, intelligence, physical systems and digital systems are first-class; Value and Pulse are central operating/economic primitives; and Integration is a core value.

The old document remains useful as historical evidence of earlier thinking, but future implementation decisions must use the frozen architecture package.
