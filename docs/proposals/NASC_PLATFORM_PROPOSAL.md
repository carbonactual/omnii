# Proposal: NASC Institutional Digital Platform

## Executive proposition

Carbon Actual proposes to deploy a National Agricultural Seeds Council institutional operating platform composed from the reusable OMNII institutional architecture. The platform is designed to make NASC services, records, regulatory workflows, evidence, field operations, certification, traceability, public verification and reporting connected, auditable and handoff-ready.

## Outcomes

- single institutional operating fabric for regulated seed-sector journeys
- structured producer/operator and seed-sector registries
- digital forms and governed workflows replacing fragmented intake and handoffs
- traceable inspection, laboratory, certification and lot evidence
- licensing, renewal, suspension and enforcement lifecycle visibility
- controlled public verification
- measurable service performance and compliance visibility
- portable deployment with explicit operations, security and exit documentation

## Reference service journey

`application → verification → evidence → inspection → testing → review → authorized decision → licence/certificate → public verification → renewal`

## Reusable architecture

The deployment reuses OMNII capabilities for registries, forms, workflow, cases/tasks, evidence, authority, audit, controls, reconciliation, analytics, notifications, integrations and continuity. NASC-specific policy and statutory configuration remain under NASC authority.

## Implementation approach

Phase 1 establishes the NASC composition and reference data contracts. Phase 2 configures priority service journeys and operational roles. Phase 3 integrates institutional services and conducts UAT. Phase 4 hardens, accepts and hands over the deployment.

## Governance

NASC remains the decision-making institution. Software records, routes, validates and surfaces information according to delegated policy. No person, vendor or AI agent is treated as a substitute for lawful institutional authority.

## Commercial/procurement boundary

Final scope, pricing, procurement route, service levels and data-processing terms should be agreed through NASC's applicable procurement and governance procedures.
