# OMNII Ecosystem Capability & Completeness Contract

## Purpose

OMNII is a composable ecosystem. Products are reusable ingredients, capabilities are the shelf, workflows are recipes, artifacts are outputs, and Pulse/reconciliation closes the loop.

## Capability Shelf

The canonical shelf accommodates recurring work such as:

- data analysis and research;
- documentation, modeling and design;
- planning, consulting and strategy;
- audit, assessment and verification;
- forecasting and feasibility studies;
- standardization, certification and compliance;
- workflow composition and delivery.

Additional capabilities may be registered without changing the constitutional runtime.

## Composition

A request may select existing capabilities and products together. The ecosystem creates a typed workflow recipe with declared inputs, outputs and validation rules. A new product is not required merely because a new combination is requested.

## Completeness

Before an output is considered complete, OMNII evaluates:

```text
required capabilities
required artifacts
required evidence
required standards
required dependencies
```

Missing components remain explicit. A low completeness score is not represented as a completed outcome.

## Artifact Contract

Artifacts are governed objects, including reports, analyses, dashboards, documents, slide decks, proposals, policies, SOPs, business cases, feasibility studies, SWOT analyses, risk assessments, financial models, implementation plans, standards, specifications, audit reports, assessments, certificates, compliance packs, contracts and decision records.

Each artifact carries provenance, version, status and evidence references.

## Standards and Compliance

Standards are represented as versioned objects with requirements and optional assessment/certification paths. OMNII does not equate a named standard with certification; certification remains an evidence-backed assessment outcome.

## Pulse and Inverted Economics

Pulse records outcomes and value dimensions independently of monetary price. Value may be created, preserved, destroyed or transferred. Monetary amount is one economic field, not the constitutional definition of value.

## No-Loss Principle

Every material request or action must have a place for its intent, requirements, dependencies, evidence, outcome, exception, recovery path and continuity record. Failures become recoverable states rather than disappearing events.

## Output Rule

**Nothing leaves the ecosystem as "complete" merely because an artifact was generated.**

Completion requires the declared requirements to be satisfied, evidence to be available at the required level, applicable standards to be addressed, dependencies to be resolved, and the resulting outcome to be reconciled.

The model remains open-world: new capabilities, artifact types, standards and compositions can be registered without changing the foundational ecosystem grammar.
