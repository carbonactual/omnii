# BUNK Property Value Chain

BUNK covers the complete property lifecycle, including the people, services, capital, evidence, risk and intelligence attached to each stage.

## Lifecycle

1. Discovery and demand
2. Identification and geospatial mapping
3. Registry/cadastre and address
4. Tenure, title and property rights
5. Evidence, due diligence and searches
6. Valuation and feasibility
7. Planning, zoning and approvals
8. Acquisition, assembly and land banking
9. Capital, financing and investment
10. Architecture, design and engineering
11. Procurement, vendors and supply chain
12. Construction and artisan delivery
13. Inspection, certification and commissioning
14. Listing, brokerage and marketing
15. Matching, negotiation and contracting
16. Sale, lease, rent, reservation and settlement
17. Occupancy and use
18. Property and facilities operations
19. Maintenance, repair and improvement
20. Insurance, tax, compliance and risk management
21. Measurement, intelligence and portfolio optimization
22. Refinancing, recapitalization and reinvestment
23. Fractionalization, pooling, decimalization and secondary exposure
24. Transfer, inheritance and succession
25. Redevelopment, conversion and adaptation
26. Distress, recovery, reuse, repurpose and recycling
27. Retirement, preservation and archival continuity

## Participant economy

BUNK composes OMNII identities and capabilities for owners, buyers, tenants, investors, communities, governments and institutions; agents, brokers, agencies and property managers; surveyors, valuers, lawyers and planners; architects, engineers and quantity surveyors; developers, contractors, builders and artisans; material/equipment vendors and suppliers; inspectors, certifiers and facility operators; financiers, insurers, tax/compliance professionals and technology providers.

A participant's capability, availability, evidence and reputation remain OMNII concerns. BUNK provides the property context and workflow; TIP provides economic market/settlement semantics.

## Product opportunity forms

BUNK supports listings and wanted requests for sale, rent, lease, accommodation, land, development, off-plan, auction, tender, distressed/recovery, joint venture, land banking, co-investment, pooled/fractional exposure, property finance, property services, maintenance and capacity/usage.

## Economic compositions

Property may be represented through direct ownership, co-ownership, pooled structures, funds, REIT exposure, debt, equity, revenue interests, collateral, property-backed finance, fractional/decimalized interests and tokenized representations where legally and operationally enabled. Such representations do not override underlying legal rights.

## Frontier continuity

The value chain remains extensible to autonomous property operations, floating/offshore facilities, underground habitats, orbital infrastructure, lunar/space facilities, extraterrestrial resources and unknown-origin property candidates. Frontier classification is deliberately separated from ownership and marketability.
