# CHARTER — Composability and Capability Handoff

**Status:** Canonical Charter composition supplement  
**Domain:** Universal transport / movement  
**Economic boundary:** IO owns value movement.

## Principle

> Build canonical capabilities once, compose them many ways, and hand off coherent compositions without forking the source of truth.

Charter is therefore a capability-rich transport/movement domain, not a monolithic application and not a closed catalogue of vehicles.

## Capability layers

Charter composes existing OMNII primitives and capabilities for:

- movement and journeys;
- transport objects and equipment;
- passengers, cargo and animals;
- routes, waypoints and networks;
- infrastructure and interchange;
- availability, capacity and capability;
- condition, inspection, maintenance and repair;
- fleets, dispatch and assignment;
- operators, crews and specialists;
- incidents, recovery and emergency movement;
- access, pickup, handoff and delivery;
- events, sport, pilgrimage, migration, tourism and expeditions;
- historical/provenance relationships;
- autonomous, emerging, future and unknown movement systems.

The list is descriptive, not a closed constitutional enumeration.

## Product composition

A product is a projection/composition of canonical capabilities.

Examples:

```text
DOOR-TO-DOOR MOBILITY
= intent + journey + discovery + availability + route + transport + operator + tracking + exception handling

FLEET OPERATIONS
= fleet + asset + condition + availability + assignment + maintenance + movement

ROADSIDE RECOVERY
= incident + location + recovery vehicle + specialist + capability + availability + handoff

PRIVATE / EXECUTIVE MOVEMENT
= journey + premium capability + chauffeur/crew + access + availability + movement

MULTIMODAL JOURNEY
= multiple movement legs + interchange + timing + handoffs + destination
```

These are compositions, not new universal primitives.

## Handoff contract

A coherent capability bundle may be packaged or handed off when it has:

1. a defined purpose;
2. explicit canonical inputs and outputs;
3. dependencies identified;
4. authority boundaries identified;
5. evidence/provenance requirements identified;
6. lifecycle/state requirements identified;
7. event and relationship requirements identified;
8. clear interface/workflow boundaries;
9. no duplicated canonical source of truth.

Handoff does not mean copying the canonical ontology. The receiving product or service references the canonical objects/capabilities and owns only its permitted composition or implementation boundary.

## Composition rules

### Rule 1 — Source first

Before creating a capability, identify its canonical source in OMNII.

### Rule 2 — Compose before creating

If an existing capability can satisfy the requirement, compose it rather than creating a Charter-specific duplicate.

### Rule 3 — Ownership follows source

Using a capability does not transfer canonical ownership of that capability to Charter.

### Rule 4 — Products remain projections

A Charter product may be broad and capability-rich while remaining a composition over the shared ecosystem graph.

### Rule 5 — Value stays in IO

Any value/economic movement is represented and executed through IO's canonical architecture. Charter does not create a parallel money, payment, settlement or economic primitive.

### Rule 6 — Identity remains canonical

Persons, organizations, vehicles, places, infrastructure and other objects use the canonical OMNII object/identity model.

### Rule 7 — Authority remains canonical

Government, regulatory, operational and delegated authority use OMNII authority semantics. Charter may apply authority constraints to movement but does not manufacture jurisdiction.

### Rule 8 — Evidence remains explicit

Verified, reported, inferred, unknown, hypothetical and future movement states must remain distinguishable through existing evidence/provenance semantics.

## Extraction / handoff pattern

```text
OMNII CANONICAL CAPABILITY
          │
          ▼
   CHARTER COMPOSITION
          │
    ┌─────┼─────┐
    ▼     ▼     ▼
 Product  Workflow  Service
    │     │     │
    └─────┼─────┘
          ▼
     HANDOFF PACKAGE
          │
          ├── contract
          ├── interfaces
          ├── dependencies
          ├── authority boundaries
          ├── evidence requirements
          └── canonical references
```

The handoff package can be implemented by another product, team, organization or service without creating a competing canonical model.

## Door-to-door composition

Charter can compose any number of movement legs:

```text
ORIGIN
 → ACCESS
 → PICKUP
 → LOCAL MOVEMENT
 → INTERCHANGE
 → PRIMARY MODE
 → INTERCHANGE
 → SECONDARY MODE
 → LAST MILE
 → DESTINATION
```

Each leg may use a different mode, operator, owner, infrastructure resource or authority context.

## Government / private / individual composition

Charter may coordinate lawful combinations of:

- government assets and infrastructure;
- public transport;
- commercial operators;
- private owners;
- individual providers;
- community resources;
- fleets;
- specialist operators;
- emergency/humanitarian resources;
- autonomous systems.

The source organization retains ownership and authority. Charter coordinates the movement context.

## Future openness

The architecture is intentionally open to movement capabilities not currently known or commercially available, including future autonomous systems, novel terrestrial/marine/air systems, commercial space transportation and hypothetical/extraterrestrial systems. Such objects remain subject to explicit evidence and authority status.

## Architectural outcome

Charter can therefore be extremely broad without becoming cramped, because breadth comes from **composition**, while architectural integrity comes from **canonical ownership and non-duplication**.

The reusable unit is the capability. The product is the composition. The handoff is the contract. The canonical source remains OMNII.
