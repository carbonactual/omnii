# TIP — Investment Completeness Extensions

**Ecosystem:** Carbon Actual / OMNII  
**Product:** TIP — Trade, Investment & Value Markets  
**Status:** CANONICAL EXTENSION — approved  
**Purpose:** Close second-order investment taxonomy gaps across institutional, specialty, insurance-linked, participatory, structured, retirement and lifecycle capital.

---

## 1. Retirement, Pension and Longevity Capital

TIP investment architecture must support:

- defined-contribution pensions
- defined-benefit pension assets
- retirement portfolios
- target-date funds
- lifecycle funds
- annuity accumulation
- annuity payout
- longevity-linked investments
- liability-matching portfolios
- duration matching
- cash-flow matching
- pension de-risking
- pension buy-ins/buy-outs where applicable
- deferred income structures
- retirement reserve management

The system must separate the investment portfolio from the retirement obligation it is intended to satisfy.

---

## 2. Insurance-Linked Investment

Investment and risk capital can connect through:

- insurance-linked securities
- catastrophe bonds
- catastrophe-linked notes
- collateralized reinsurance structures
- insurance-linked funds
- longevity-risk transfer
- mortality-risk transfer where lawful
- weather-risk transfer
- parametric-risk-linked capital

TIP should represent the underlying insured risk, trigger, collateral, payout logic and investor exposure separately.

- Murabaha

## 27. Investment + Trading Convergence

Investment and trading share infrastructure but retain separate semantics:

```text
INVESTMENT THESIS
      ↓
PORTFOLIO POSITION
      ↓
TRADE / HEDGE / FINANCE
      ↓
SETTLEMENT
      ↓
MONITOR
      ↓
REBALANCE
      ↓
EXIT / HOLD / FOLLOW-ON
```

Trading changes positions. Investment explains why the position exists and what outcome it is intended to produce.

---

## 28. Canonical Completeness Rule

> **No investment taxonomy is complete if it only lists securities. TIP must represent the capital, vehicle, claim, cash flow, governance, risk, liquidity, purpose, evidence, obligation, outcome and jurisdiction associated with an investment.**
