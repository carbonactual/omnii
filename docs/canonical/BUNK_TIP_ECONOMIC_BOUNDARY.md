# BUNK ↔ TIP Economic Boundary

BUNK is the property product. TIP is OMNII's foundation tool for trade, investment, financing and value markets.

## BUNK owns

- Property context and lifecycle
- Property/unit/development representations
- Listing and wanted-request experiences
- Property-specific workflows
- Property participant and service composition
- Property intelligence and operational views
- Property evidence/verification presentation
- Property portfolio experiences

## TIP owns

- Canonical market and economic semantics
- Trade/investment instruments and mechanisms
- Financing and capital-market semantics
- Pricing, order, matching, clearing and settlement semantics
- Collateral and risk-market semantics
- Fractionalization/decimalization and tokenization mechanisms
- Secondary-market and transfer semantics

## Required routing

A BUNK economic action must resolve to a TIP capability reference, explicit authority, jurisdiction/policy context and provenance. BUNK may not create a parallel economic ledger, settlement model, market mechanism or universal value ontology.

## Examples

- A building is an OMNII property object; BUNK exposes its sale workflow; TIP executes the relevant market/settlement path.
- A housing development is BUNK context; pooled capital/fractional participation is composed through TIP.
- A property service or maintenance job is BUNK workflow context and a TIP service-market action when economic exchange is required.
- A cemetery plot may be a BUNK property/right context; any transferable or reservable economic route is jurisdiction- and authority-controlled and uses TIP rather than a BUNK-specific financial engine.

## Safety boundary

Tokenization is representation/mechanism, not automatic ownership. Fractionalization is an economic structure, not automatic title. AI/agents cannot use the economic route to bypass human authority, consent, jurisdiction, risk or compliance requirements.
