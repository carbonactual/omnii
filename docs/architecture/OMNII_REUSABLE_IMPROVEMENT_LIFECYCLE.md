# OMNII Reusable Improvement Lifecycle

**Status:** CANONICAL OPERATIONAL GUIDANCE — 2026-09-16

A product improvement is not automatically product-private. Every material improvement is evaluated for reusable value and preserved with provenance.

```text
PRODUCT USE
→ OBSERVATION
→ PROBLEM / OPPORTUNITY
→ CHANGE
→ EVIDENCE
→ GENERALIZATION
→ COMPATIBILITY CHECK
→ REUSABLE CANDIDATE
→ SHARED
→ CANONICAL
```

## Required record

Use `config/ecosystem-improvement.schema.json` for material capability improvements. The record must identify the originating product, canonical capability, problem, change, evidence, affected consumers, compatibility impact, maturity transition and provenance.

## Promotion rules

- A product may remain the first/current context without becoming permanent semantic owner.
- Multiple-domain adoption strengthens promotion evidence but is not mandatory when the contract is clearly universal.
- Promotion does not erase product history or domain extensions.
- Compatibility identifiers remain until consuming products migrate or an explicit governance decision retires them.
- Existing consumers must be identified before a shared/canonical change is released.
- A rollback path must preserve prior versions and reconstructable provenance.

## Reusable dimensions

Promotion can apply to semantics, schemas, events, workflows, policies, forms, UI components where appropriate, APIs, agent/tool interfaces, adapters, tests, conformance checks, knowledge, prompts/instructions, human procedures, deployment packages, security controls, observability and recovery procedures.

## Relationship to Pulse

Pulse and outcome observations can provide evidence for improvement but do not by themselves authorize promotion, establish truth or redefine canonical Value.

## Relationship to product boundaries

A product remains responsible for its experience, domain configuration, local law/policy and deployment context. The underlying reusable capability may still be strengthened once and consumed by many products.
