# CHARTER — HAPI, Fleet & Logistico Composition Boundary

## Canonical rule

Charter does not own humans, crews, or fleet organizations. It composes them.

```text
OMNII CANONICAL LAYER
        │
 ┌──────┼─────────┐
 │      │         │
HAPI   FLEET   CHARTER
 │      │         │
Human  Asset &   Movement
Agent  Fleet Ops Execution
 │      │         │
 └──────┼─────────┘
        │
    LOGISTICO
   Goods Workflow
        │
        ▼
       IO
  Value Movement
```

## HAPI

HAPI represents the human/agent side of movement. Charter may require HAPI capabilities such as:

- driver;
- pilot;
- captain;
- chauffeur;
- engineer;
- mechanic;
- attendant;
- dispatcher;
- specialist;
- instructor;
- rescue/recovery operator.

Qualifications, training, identity, experience, availability and progression remain HAPI concerns.

## Fleet

Fleet represents organizational management of transport assets:

- fleet composition;
- asset assignment;
- deployment;
- availability;
- utilization;
- readiness;
- maintenance coordination;
- inspection coordination;
- dispatch structure;
- asset lifecycle management;
- replacement planning.

Fleet may reference HAPI people/agents but does not become the canonical home of their identity or human progression.

## Charter

Charter composes:

`HAPI capability + Fleet asset capability + infrastructure + route + movement requirements → executable journey`

Charter owns the movement context, journey composition, leg execution, handoffs, exceptions and recovery composition.

## Logistico

Logistico composes Charter movement around goods:

`goods intent + custody + cargo requirements + Charter movement → logistics workflow`

Logistico owns the goods-movement workflow context, not the canonical movement primitive.

## Example

A fleet operator needs a qualified driver to move a refrigerated vehicle from a depot to a customer.

- **HAPI:** supplies the qualified human/agent.
- **Fleet:** supplies and manages the vehicle and fleet assignment.
- **Charter:** composes driver + vehicle + route + infrastructure + journey execution.
- **Logistico:** manages the cargo movement and delivery workflow.
- **IO:** handles any value movement required by the workflow.

## Non-overlap rules

1. Charter must not create a second human/crew ontology.
2. Fleet must not create a second human identity/qualification ontology.
3. HAPI must not become a fleet-management system.
4. Logistico must not create another transport capability registry.
5. No product may duplicate OMNII canonical identity, authority, lifecycle or relationship primitives.
6. Economic/value operations remain in IO.

## Extraction rule

Any of HAPI, Fleet, Logistico or Charter can be handed off as a product composition because their boundaries are explicit and their dependencies are references/contracts rather than copied canonical structures.

## Invariant

> **HAPI is the human/agent side. Fleet is organizational asset management. Charter is movement execution. Logistico is goods-flow orchestration. IO is value movement.**
