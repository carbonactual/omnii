# RELATIONSHIP

**Status: CANONICAL CONSTITUTIONAL KERNEL**

## Purpose
Relationship gives explicit semantic form to connections between every governed object in OMNII. It makes connection, membership, ownership, commitment, delegation, dependency, communication and network participation first-class and auditable.

## Canonical Objects
- Relationship
- Connection
- Association
- Membership
- Commitment
- Contract
- Ownership
- Delegation
- Trust relationship
- Dependency
- Communication channel
- Network

## Invariants
1. A relationship has source, target, type, authority context and lifecycle.
2. Relationship semantics are typed and registered; products cannot redefine universal relationship meaning.
3. A relationship never erases the identity or authority boundary of its participants.
4. Consequential relationships are attributable, auditable and provenance-preserving.
5. Termination preserves required historical provenance.
6. Relationship state cannot silently grant authority absent an explicit grant.

## Inputs
Object identities, relationship type, intent, authority, evidence, constraints and temporal context.

## Outputs
A typed relationship, lifecycle state, derived obligations/permissions where authorized, provenance and auditable events.

## Lifecycle
`Proposed → Authorized → Active → Suspended/Constrained → Modified → Terminated → Archived`

## Dependencies and Relationships
Relationship connects Being and Identity to Knowledge, Motion, Value and Trust and provides the connective substrate for Intent, Execution, Capability, Resource, Continuity and Composition.

## Composition Rules
Relationships may be composed into higher-order structures. Composite relationships must retain constituent relationships, authority boundaries, provenance and failure/termination semantics.

## Implementation Contract
The runtime representation must expose stable relationship identity, typed endpoints, relationship type/version, authority, lifecycle, temporal validity, provenance/evidence and correlation/event references. Graph storage is an implementation detail beneath this contract.

## ABBA
ABBA may discover, evaluate, coordinate, propose, establish, monitor or terminate relationships only within delegated authority. ABBA must preserve provenance and escalate authority-sensitive decisions.
