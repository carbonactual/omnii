# TIP — Comprehensive Trading Taxonomy

**Ecosystem:** Carbon Actual / OMNII  
**Product:** TIP — Trade, Investment & Value Markets  
**Status:** CANONICAL EXTENSION — approved  
**Purpose:** Establish a comprehensive trading taxonomy covering legacy, current, emerging and future forms of exchange, position-taking, financing, arbitrage, hedging and market execution.

---

## 1. Trading Definition

Trading is the authorized exchange, transfer, creation, modification, hedging, financing, or position-taking around a Market Object, Capability, Right, Flow, Contract, Obligation or Opportunity.

Trading is broader than buying and selling securities.

A TIP trade may involve:

- ownership transfer
- temporary access
- possession/custody transfer
- delivery now or later
- a future claim
- a service
- capacity
- financing
- risk transfer
- price exposure
- spread exposure
- arbitrage
- collateralized funding
- a contractual position
- a digital representation
- a recurring entitlement
- a measurable outcome

## 25. Trading AI and Risk Constitution

The General Market Intelligence Engine may optimize trading strategies, but it cannot rewrite constitutional limits.

Required controls include:

- maximum per-trade exposure/risk
- maximum portfolio concentration
- leverage limits
- drawdown limits
- liquidity limits
- reserve requirements
- counterparty limits
- market eligibility
- jurisdiction restrictions
- strategy kill switch
- account kill switch
- daily loss controls
- abnormal-volatility controls
- model-drift controls

The configured 10% per-trade ceiling and 100% daily-profit stop remain policy-controlled parameters, not a universal promise of safe returns.

---

## 26. Progression / Regression

### Progression

Validated performance and control stability may permit greater permitted opportunity access or deployment within the unchanged constitutional ceiling.

### Regression

Losses, drawdown, model drift, unstable execution, liquidity deterioration, control violations or market-regime changes can trigger:

**reduce → restrict → paper-only → manual approval → suspend → quarantine**.

---

## 27. Canonical Rule

> **TIP must be able to represent every legitimate trading pattern without confusing the economic object with the mechanism, position, contract, right, financing relationship, or execution method used to trade it.**

A new trading form should normally require a new taxonomy/configuration entry, not a new foundational architecture.
