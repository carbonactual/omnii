# Carbon Actual Ecological Build Method

**Status:** Canonical OMNII build guideline, harvested from `carbonactual/Carbon-Actual-`.
**Source:** `docs/ECOLOGICAL_BUILD_METHOD.md` (source commit `446d6a8059bda2bdc744478ecb45e41008ff978c`).

## Core principle

**The build reveals the being and the habitat that gives the being meaning.**

Do not begin with a generic template and force the product into it. First understand the product's identity, habitat, relationships, current state, functions, interactions, and Becoming. Then select ecological and plate references whose characteristics naturally fit what has been discovered.

The final build must become its own Plate: distinct, native to its own reality, and recognizable as part of the Carbon Actual Book without being visually uniform with other builds.

## Audubon reference model

Audubon's *Birds of America* is a useful reference because the complete folio contains 435 plates and 1,065 life-size bird figures, and the plates were designed to show birds true to life in size, detail, and action. The scale of the work demonstrates a collection in which individual subjects retain individuality while the complete work has a recognizable authorial whole.

The reference is **not** to copy Audubon's artwork. It is to study how character, habitat, composition, observation, action, scale, color, and context can be composed into a coherent work.

## Build workflow

### 1. Discover the life of the build

Before choosing a design reference, establish:

- Identity (#)
- Root
- Actual
- Relationships
- Interactions
- Becoming
- Atlas purpose
- users and observers
- functions and capabilities
- habitat and operating environment
- scope and boundaries
- information density
- desired outcomes
- behavioral qualities
- emotional and experiential qualities

### 2. Determine the build's character

Describe the build in terms of qualities rather than industry labels.

Examples include:

- migratory
- rooted
- communal
- territorial
- adaptive
- protective
- precise
- expansive
- resilient
- investigative
- elegant
- dense
- open
- quiet
- active
- networked
- cyclical
- transformative

A product may have multiple character references.

### 3. Select bird references

Choose one or more birds whose characteristics correspond to the discovered character, behavior, scale, relationships, or movement of the build.

A bird is a **character archetype**, not a visual skin.

Do not select a bird merely because it resembles the industry. Select it because its observable qualities help explain how the product should feel, behave, relate, or move.

### 4. Study the bird's habitat

Never separate the bird from its habitat.

Study:

- where it lives
- what surrounds it
- what it depends on
- what it interacts with
- how it moves through its environment
- what environmental conditions affect it
- what relationships sustain it
- how its habitat contributes to its identity

The habitat becomes a reference for the product's own ecology.

### 5. Select Plate references

Choose one or more plates that best express the required qualities of the build.

A Plate may become a reference for:

- composition
- scale
- spatial hierarchy
- subject placement
- negative space
- density
- framing
- color relationships
- layering
- visual tension
- movement
- environmental context
- storytelling
- relationship visibility

Multiple Plates may be combined where a single reference is insufficient.

### 6. Extract design intelligence

Do not copy the image.

Extract the principle behind it.

Ask:

> Why does this composition work for this subject in this habitat?

Then translate the answer into the product's own reality.

### 7. Extract behavioral intelligence

The reference is also a guide for behavior, not just appearance.

Study how the subject appears to:

- enter and leave context
- attract attention
- reveal and conceal information
- establish proximity
- respond to surroundings
- change position
- coexist with other elements
- move through space
- establish hierarchy

Translate these observations into interaction, navigation, motion, transitions, state changes, and responsive behavior.

### 8. Build the product's own habitat

The build must express the actual ecology of the product.

This can include:

- people
- places
- territory
- communities
- institutions
- resources
- assets
- services
- events
- flows
- authority
- obligations
- agreements
- opportunities
- history
- evidence
- networks
- other connected entities

Habitat is not decoration. Where it is identity-forming, it belongs in the structure and function of the experience.

### 9. Create the Plate

Now compose the product experience.

The Plate should be:

- naturally fitted
- comfortable
- context-aware
- purposeful
- responsive
- expressive
- distinct
- free from unnecessary rules

It may be a website, dashboard, marketplace, map, workflow, conversation, financial surface, institutional portal, spatial interface, mobile experience, or another form appropriate to the reality.

### 10. Test against the whole

The final build must satisfy two conditions:

**Individuality:**

> This could only have been designed for this product and its habitat.

**Continuity:**

> This unmistakably belongs to the Carbon Actual Book.

The second condition must come from shared philosophy and craft, not forced visual uniformity.

## Framer application

Framer is an implementation medium, not the source of the design logic. Use structured/dynamic content and components to preserve natural variation rather than creating a rigid template system.

## Reference library model

The ecosystem may maintain a reference library containing:

### Bird Archetypes
Character, behavior, movement, sociality, resilience, scale, territory, and other observable qualities.

### Habitat Archetypes
Environmental conditions, spatial patterns, relationships, resources, dependencies, density, boundaries, and context.

### Plate References
Composition, color, scale, framing, hierarchy, negative space, movement, environmental integration, and visual narrative.

### Behavioral References
Interaction patterns, response patterns, transition logic, discovery, movement, attention, and state change.

### Translation Guides
Rules for converting reference characteristics into the product's own architecture, texture, function, UX, motion, and visual language.

These are **reference systems**, not fixed templates.

## Non-negotiable rule

**Understand the life first. Find the birds. Study their habitats. Select the plates. Extract their design and behavioral intelligence. Then let the product become its own Plate.**

## Thriving objective

The purpose of the method is to produce experiences that help the entity and the people interacting with it to understand context, navigate relationships, act naturally, continue Becoming, and thrive within their real habitat.

**Many birds. Many habitats. Many plates. One living Book.**
