## Global Constraints
- No new constitutional kernel solely because a new domain, technology, intelligence class or civilization scale exists.
- `ABBA ≠ AUTHORITY ISSUER`.
- `CAPABILITY ≠ AUTHORITY`.
- Products remain downstream compositions.
- Future, simulated and unknown states must not silently become actual states.
- Unknown/alien entities receive no inferred authority or trust.
- Provider-specific infrastructure remains replaceable beneath canonical contracts.
