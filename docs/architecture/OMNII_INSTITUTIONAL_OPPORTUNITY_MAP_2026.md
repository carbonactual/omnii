# OMNII Institutional Opportunity Map 2026

**Status: CANONICAL MARKET/COMPOSITION MAP**
**Purpose:** Identify repeatable institutional problems that can be solved by assembling OMNII capabilities across government, private enterprise, nonprofit, research and community organisations.

## 1. Opportunity thesis

The opportunity is not to sell unrelated applications. It is to identify institutional operating patterns, solve the common machinery once, and compose deployments around local mandates and domain rules.

Current public-sector evidence strongly supports this direction. OECD 2026 describes shared digital infrastructure, core registries, digital identity, payments, notifications, data interoperability and reusable building blocks as foundations for joined-up services. World Bank GTMI 2025 covers core government systems, online public services, citizen engagement and enablers including public financial management, tax, customs, HR/payroll, e-procurement and public investment. GOV.UK explicitly operates common Forms, Notify and Pay platforms and promotes reuse. NIST and ISO provide common risk, privacy, supply-chain and compliance patterns applicable across organisations.

## 2. Government opportunity families

### Ministries and departments

Pain: fragmented records, paper workflows, approvals, reporting, inter-agency handoffs.

Bundle: institution registry + people/role/authority + forms + workflow + case management + document/evidence + notifications + dashboard + audit + interoperability.

First pilot: one high-volume service journey from application to decision.

Expansion: all services, records, departmental registries and cross-ministry data exchange.

### Regulatory agencies

Pain: licences, inspections, renewals, complaints, enforcement and fragmented operator data.

Bundle: regulated-entity registry + licensing + inspection + evidence + certification + compliance + case management + public verification + analytics.

First pilot: one licence class and its renewal/inspection lifecycle.

Expansion: all regulated sectors and risk-based supervision.

### Tax, revenue and collections

Pain: leakage, duplicate records, offline cash handling, reconciliation and weak visibility.

Bundle: taxpayer/business registry + assessment + collection + receipt + reconciliation + field collections + payment adapters + audit + dashboards + exception cases.

First pilot: one revenue stream or territory.

Expansion: multi-channel government revenue and reconciliation.

### Public procurement and supply

Pain: disconnected demand, bids, awards, contracts, deliveries, invoices and supplier performance.

Bundle: supplier registry + requirements + sourcing + bid intake + evaluation + approval + contracting + purchase orders + delivery + invoice matching + supplier risk + audit.

First pilot: one procurement category.

Expansion: enterprise e-procurement and contract intelligence.

### Public financial management

Pain: budget fragmentation, slow approvals, weak commitment tracking and reconciliation.

Bundle: budget + allocation + commitment + expenditure + procurement + payment + reconciliation + audit + reporting + scenario planning.

First pilot: one programme or cost centre.

Expansion: programme-wide financial operations.

### Human resources and payroll

Pain: employee records, onboarding, approvals, leave, attendance, training, payroll interfaces and succession gaps.

Bundle: workforce registry + roles + credentials + onboarding + workflow + scheduling + performance + payroll adapter + training + succession + access lifecycle.

First pilot: onboarding-to-access-to-first-pay workflow.

Expansion: employee lifecycle management.

### Agriculture and commodity institutions

Pain: producer records, inputs, inspection, certification, traceability, market information and programme distribution.

Bundle: producer registry + commodity/variety registry + licensing + field inspection + testing/evidence + certification + lot traceability + market intelligence + payments + notifications.

First pilot: one commodity and one operational region.

Expansion: national agricultural intelligence.

### Health institutions and public health agencies

Pain: fragmented patient/provider/facility records, referrals, inspections, stock, reporting and emergency coordination.

Bundle: person/provider/facility registry + appointments + case management + referrals + inspection + laboratory evidence + inventory + notifications + dashboards + continuity.

First pilot: one service journey such as referral or facility inspection.

Expansion: network-wide service coordination.

### Education ministries and institutions

Pain: admissions, student records, staffing, examinations, credentials, attendance and infrastructure data.

Bundle: institution registry + learner registry + workforce + admissions + forms + workflow + timetable + assessment + credentials + facility/assets + analytics.

First pilot: admissions or credential lifecycle.

Expansion: education ecosystem management.

### Justice and dispute-resolution institutions

Pain: case intake, evidence, hearings, deadlines, orders, notices and records.

Bundle: case registry + parties + forms + evidence + workflow + scheduling + notices + decision records + secure archive + audit.

First pilot: administrative case type rather than judicial decision automation.

Expansion: broader case-management interoperability.

### Land, property and planning authorities

Pain: property records, permits, inspections, maps, applications and fragmented ownership/usage information.

Bundle: property/land registry + territory/geo + permits + inspection + documents + workflow + payments + public verification + analytics.

First pilot: planning permit or facility registration.

Expansion: property and territorial operations.

### Transport authorities

Pain: vehicle/operator licensing, inspection, routes, incidents, terminals and fee collection.

Bundle: person/operator/vehicle registry + licensing + inspection + route/terminal registry + incidents + payments + enforcement evidence + analytics.

First pilot: operator/vehicle lifecycle.

Expansion: multimodal transport intelligence.

### Infrastructure and public works

Pain: projects, contractors, assets, inspections, maintenance and payments are disconnected.

Bundle: project + supplier + asset + work order + inspection + evidence + schedule + payment + risk + maintenance + geo.

First pilot: one capital project or asset class.

Expansion: portfolio-wide infrastructure management.

### Environment and natural resources

Pain: permits, field inspections, monitoring, assets, incidents and enforcement.

Bundle: resource registry + territory/geo + permits + inspections + telemetry adapters + evidence + cases + compliance + alerts + reporting.

First pilot: one permit/regulatory workflow.

Expansion: monitoring and compliance network.

### Social protection and public programmes

Pain: beneficiary registration, eligibility, case management, payments and programme leakage.

Bundle: beneficiary registry + eligibility + application + case + verification + payment adapter + appeals + audit + analytics.

First pilot: one benefit/programme.

Expansion: multi-programme service delivery.

### Emergency management and response

Pain: fragmented incidents, dispatch, resources, shelters, communications and after-action data.

Bundle: incident registry + alerting + dispatch + resource/capacity + geo + communication + field execution + evidence + recovery + analytics.

First pilot: one incident type.

Expansion: multi-agency response coordination.

### Cultural, religious and civic institutions

Pain: events, membership, donations/value, facilities, communications, protocol and archives.

Bundle: membership + event + facility + booking + communication + volunteer/workforce + documents + value + archive + continuity.

First pilot: one recurring event or service.

Expansion: full institutional continuity platform.

## 3. Private and nonprofit opportunity families

### Banks, fintechs and regulated financial institutions

Bundle: customer/entity registry + onboarding/KYC workflows + case management + documents/evidence + approvals + risk + compliance + service + payments + analytics.

Constraint: integrate with regulated core systems and applicable regulatory controls; do not pretend OMNII itself is a banking licence or regulated ledger.

### Insurance

Bundle: customer/policy/asset registry + quote/application + underwriting case + claims + inspection + evidence + approvals + payments + fraud/risk + renewals.

### Manufacturing

Bundle: workforce + supplier + product/asset + inventory + procurement + production workflows + quality inspection + maintenance + traceability + logistics + finance.

### Logistics and distribution

Bundle: customer + vehicle + driver/operator + warehouse + inventory + order + route + dispatch + proof + maintenance + billing + exception management.

### Retail and commerce

Bundle: customer + supplier + product + inventory + order + fulfilment + payments + returns + customer service + loyalty + marketing + analytics.

### Construction and real estate

Bundle: project + property + contractor + supplier + permits + documents + inspections + work orders + inventory + payments + tenancy/occupancy + maintenance.

### Hospitality

Bundle: property/facility + rooms/resources + reservations + guest identity + workforce + service cases + payments + maintenance + events + feedback.

### Healthcare providers

Bundle: patient/provider/facility registry + booking + intake forms + cases + referrals + lab evidence + billing adapters + inventory + compliance + analytics.

### Schools, universities and training institutions

Bundle: learner + staff + course + admissions + timetable + assessment + credentials + payments + facilities + research + alumni/continuity.

### Professional services firms

Bundle: client + matter/case + engagements + contracts + tasks + documents + approvals + billing + workforce + knowledge + compliance.

### NGOs, foundations and development organisations

Bundle: beneficiary + programme + grant + partner + field activity + monitoring/evaluation + evidence + procurement + finance + reporting + donor communications.

### Energy and utilities

Bundle: customer/site/asset registry + meter/device adapters + service requests + work orders + outage incidents + field dispatch + billing + maintenance + compliance + forecasting.

### Telecom and digital infrastructure operators

Bundle: customer + site + network asset + service order + field work + incident + maintenance + capacity + billing + supplier + compliance.

### Media and content organisations

Bundle: talent/contributor + content + rights + production + editorial workflow + approvals + publication + audience + sponsorship + archive + analytics.

### Sports organisations

Bundle: athlete + team + competition + venue + official + registration + scheduling + ticketing + sponsorship + medical/fitness evidence + media + finance.

### Research, laboratories and science organisations

Bundle: researcher + project + sample/specimen + chain-of-custody + instrument + method + test + result + evidence + grant/procurement + publication + compliance.

### Cooperatives, associations and chambers

Bundle: member registry + governance + dues/value + meetings + elections where appropriate + documents + services + benefits + communication + continuity.

### Franchises and multi-site enterprises

Bundle: organization hierarchy + site registry + workforce + asset + inventory + standard workflows + local configurations + compliance + dashboards + central/local authority separation.

## 4. Cross-selling pattern

`Registry → Forms → Workflow → Notifications → Payments → Documents → Case Management → Compliance → Analytics → Automation → AI/Agents → Marketplace/Service Delivery`

### Suggested first pilot

A controlled producer/operator lifecycle:

`application → identity/organization verification → evidence → inspection → review → approval → licence/certificate → renewal → public verification → compliance/incident → analytics`

## 7. Opportunity selection score

Prioritize an institutional opportunity using:

`strategic fit + pain/volume + repeatability + measurable outcome + data readiness + authority clarity + procurement feasibility + implementation risk + reusable capability yield`

A high-value opportunity is one where a small pilot exercises many shared capabilities and can become a reference implementation.

## 8. Procurement and relationship boundary

Institutional access is not a substitute for lawful procurement. A personal or executive relationship may facilitate discovery, sponsorship, demonstrations and problem identification, but contractual award must follow the applicable institution's procurement, conflict-of-interest, data-protection and governance requirements.

The commercial objective is to become the provider of a strong, reusable capability—not to make the architecture dependent on an individual office holder.

## 9. Strategic principle

The market-facing OMNII loop is:

`institutional problem → reusable capability design → reference deployment → evidence → stronger shared module → next institution → lower marginal build cost → wider capability coverage`

The institution pays for the value of its deployment; Carbon Actual retains a growing capability fabric that makes future deployments faster, safer and more interoperable.

## 10. Research basis

Public sector: OECD Digital Government Outlook 2026; World Bank GovTech Maturity Index 2025; GOV.UK common-platform and reuse guidance.

Enterprise/private sector: Oracle Fusion Cloud Applications publicly groups finance, procurement, supply chain/manufacturing, HR, sales, service and marketing into integrated workflows; Salesforce publicly groups sales, service, marketing, commerce and IT around shared customer/data/workflow capabilities. These confirm recurring enterprise operating functions, while OMNII deliberately models them as vendor-neutral reusable capabilities rather than reproducing a vendor suite.

Risk/compliance: NIST RMF and recent SP 800-18r2 cover security, privacy and cybersecurity supply-chain planning across systems/organizations; ISO 37301 defines compliance-management requirements applicable to public, private and not-for-profit organizations.
