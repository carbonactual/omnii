# NAB — Charter Registry, Biography & Operational Intelligence Constitution

**Status:** Canonical Charter branch
**Role:** Registry, biography, provenance, operational state, compliance, documentation and evidence layer for movement.

## Purpose

NAB is the authoritative knowledge and registry substrate that enables Charter functions. NAB does not replace movement execution; it supplies the trusted information required for movement decisions and records continuity over time.

NAB answers:

> What is this, who/what is associated with it, where is it, what can it do, what state is it in, what is permitted, what happened before, and what evidence supports the answer?

## Scope

NAB may register and maintain movement-related records for:

- people and passengers through references to HAPI;
- vehicles and mobility assets;
- aircraft and aerial systems;
- vessels and maritime systems;
- rail assets;
- drones and autonomous systems;
- heavy, industrial, agricultural and construction equipment;
- roads, stations, terminals, ports, airports and other infrastructure;
- routes, corridors, waypoints and network resources;
- operators, organizations, authorities and institutions;
- licenses, permits, registrations and certifications;
- travel and border documentation references;
- compliance and enforcement records;
- movement journeys and operational events;
- evidence and provenance.

## Core functions

### Registry

Provide canonical references and discoverability for movement-relevant entities and resources.

### Biography

Maintain longitudinal history across creation, registration, assignment, operation, inspection, maintenance, modification, incident, recovery, transfer, retirement and other material events.

### Operational state

Maintain current and recent state such as availability, readiness, location, occupancy, capacity, restrictions, disruption and service condition.

### Compliance and documentation

Maintain references to applicable permissions, certifications, registrations, travel documents, inspections, violations, restrictions and regulatory evidence. NAB references authoritative issuers; it does not invent jurisdiction.

### Evidence and provenance

Link records to authoritative evidence and preserve confidence/status distinctions such as verified, reported, inferred, disputed, unknown and hypothetical.

## Boundary

Charter executes movement and journeys.

NAB provides the registry, biography, state, compliance, documentation, provenance and evidence needed by Charter.

Fleet manages organizational asset operations.

HAPI owns human/agent identity, qualifications and progression.

Logistico owns goods-flow workflow.

IO owns value movement.

## Real-time principle

NAB may ingest or receive real-time or near-real-time state from approved sources, but the source, timestamp, freshness and confidence of each state assertion must remain explicit.

A current state is therefore treated as a time-bounded assertion, not an eternal fact.

## Automation principle

NAB should enable automated eligibility, readiness, compliance, route-context, documentation and exception workflows without silently making authority decisions outside the responsible domain.

## Invariant

> **Charter moves. NAB knows and records the movement world.**
