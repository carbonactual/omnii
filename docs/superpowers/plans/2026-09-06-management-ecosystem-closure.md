# Universal Management Ecosystem Closure Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Extend the canonical OMNII ecosystem rather than creating product-specific silos.

**Goal:** Define and register Management as a universal reusable capability that directs resources, people, intelligence, processes, obligations and change toward intended outcomes across any entity or composition.

**Architecture:** Management is a capability family branch within the existing constitutional families, not a new top-level constitutional family. It composes governance, intelligence, capability, knowledge/data, composition, exchange and continuum primitives and binds directly to the Universal Production Fabric.

**Tech Stack:** Canonical Markdown architecture and registry documents; no runtime implementation in this pass.

**Spec:** `docs/canonical/OMNII_MANAGEMENT_FULL_SCOPE.md`

## Global Constraints

- Management is global-by-default; geography, jurisdiction, sector and institutional context are configuration layers.
- Management does not create constitutional authority; authority comes from existing governance and human/legal controls.
- Management does not imply employment, ownership, custody, legal representation, professional licensure or autonomous authority.
- Human authority remains explicit for consequential actions.
- ABBA may coordinate and recommend; agent permissions remain bounded, auditable and revocable.
- Unknowns, conflicts and incomplete information remain visible rather than silently resolved.
- Management consumes canonical ecosystem capabilities before creating new ones.
- Products expose Management through appropriate experience surfaces but do not fork its semantics.
