# CAUSALITY

**Status: CANONICAL CROSS-CUTTING CONSTITUTIONAL CONTRACT**

Causality describes typed relationships between occurrences without assuming that sequence proves cause.

## Canonical relations
`causes`, `contributes_to`, `enables`, `prevents`, `requires`, `triggers`, `correlates_with`, `intended_to`, `suspected_cause`, `inferred_cause`, `disputed_cause`, `unknown_cause`.

## Invariants
- Causal assertions carry provenance and confidence.
- Correlation is not causation.
- Intent is not outcome.
- A suspected or inferred cause cannot silently become a verified cause.
- Causes may be multiple, conditional, circular through feedback, delayed, or disputed.
- Counterfactual and simulated causal chains are explicitly non-actual.
