# OMNII Intelligence & Optimization Layer

**Status:** CANONICAL COMPOSITION LAYER — 2026-09-07  
**Authority:** subordinate to Constitution, governance, human authority, policy and runtime controls

## Purpose

Provide the intelligence loop above the observable OMNII substrate: understand, predict, simulate, recommend, coordinate, learn and optimize without becoming self-authorizing.

## Intelligence stack

`Observation → Context → Knowledge → Reasoning → Simulation → Prediction → Recommendation → Decision → Authorization → Execution → Outcome → Learning`

The layers are separable so prediction cannot be confused with observation and recommendation cannot be confused with authorization.

## ABBA role

ABBA is the master intelligence/orchestration layer. It can interpret, reason, plan, discover, match, route, compose, monitor, learn and escalate across domains within declared authority.

ABBA cannot:

- change constitutional law
- grant itself authority
- silently convert simulation into Actual
- manufacture evidence
- override required human/legal decisions

## Specialized intelligence

The layer supports domain-specific intelligence nodes for economics, education, trade, transport, health, governance, media, property, infrastructure, security and other domains. These are composable capabilities, not competing masters.

## World model

The world model distinguishes:

`observed/current state | historical state | intended state | simulated state | predicted state | proposed state | unknown state`

Simulated and predicted values cannot silently become Actual.

## Scenario and simulation

Simulation can test:

`policy + market + infrastructure + resource + operational + emergency + institutional + ecosystem`

Scenarios carry assumptions, model/version, inputs, uncertainty, horizon and provenance. Outputs are recommendations/evidence, not facts.

## Optimization objective

Optimization is multi-objective and context-specific. Candidate objectives may include:

`value realized, cost, time, resilience, fairness, safety, sustainability, reliability, capacity, quality, human preference and institutional goals`

No global optimizer is permitted to override constitutional or jurisdictional constraints.

## Pulse

Pulse is the feedback mechanism for observed performance and realized value. It consumes outcomes rather than hallucinated intent and may generate signals such as:

`value gain/loss, waste, friction, risk, reliability, capacity pressure, quality, externality and learning opportunity`

Pulse feeds optimization while preserving historical observations.

## Learning loop

`Experience → Evidence → Knowledge → Evaluation → Learning → Adaptation`

Learned behavior must be versioned, attributable, evaluable and rollback-capable where feasible. Learning is not law.

## Recommendations

Recommendations include:

`candidate action + rationale + evidence + assumptions + expected outcome + uncertainty + affected objects + required authority + reversible/irreversible classification`

Decision makers can accept, reject, modify or defer recommendations.

## Coordination

The intelligence layer may coordinate multiple agents and systems by assigning work, sequencing dependencies, resolving conflicts and monitoring outcomes. Coordination remains bounded by authority and policy.

## Self-audit and self-healing

It supports governed diagnostics:

`detect → diagnose → propose → authorize → repair → validate → learn`

Repairs to infrastructure/runtime may be automated under policy. Constitutional changes require explicit governance authorization and provenance.

## Safety and uncertainty

Every consequential recommendation should expose uncertainty and the source quality behind it. Unknown/alien objects remain provisional and cannot acquire rights or authority merely through model confidence.

## Integration contract

The intelligence layer consumes data from Identity/Trust, Value/IO, Settlement, Workflow, Knowledge and Actual. It emits recommendations, plans, classifications, alerts, simulations and learning artifacts. Authorized workflows are the only route from recommendation to consequential action.

## Conformance invariant

**Intelligence can increase capability. It cannot manufacture authority.**
