# OMNII — Value, Pulse & Progression Framework

**Status:** Canonical economics implementation supplement
**Depends on:** `OMNII_CONSTITUTIONAL_ECONOMICS.md`, `CARBON_ACTUAL_VALUE_PULSE_ENGINE.md`

## 1. Measurement chain

```text
ACTIVITY
  ↓
OBSERVATION / EVIDENCE
  ↓
PULSE
  ↓
VALUE EVALUATION
  ↓
CONTRIBUTION / OUTCOME / DIFFERENTIAL
  ↓
CURATION
  ↓
PROGRESSION
```

## 2. Pulse is feedback

Pulse records observable response to contribution or activity. It can be positive, neutral, negative, uncertain or disputed.

Pulse must retain its provenance and context. A single Pulse signal cannot, by itself, determine a person's worth or economic rank.

## 3. Value is multidimensional

Value may include:

- knowledge;
- teaching;
- capability created;
- time;
- labor;
- intelligence;
- energy;
- data;
- attention;
- access;
- reliability;
- quality;
- outcome;
- opportunity enabled;
- harm prevented;
- social/community effect;
- environmental effect;
- continuity preserved.

Different dimensions may be measured separately and combined only where the context justifies combination.

## 4. Contribution graph

A contribution can propagate through other actors:

```text
TEACHER
  ↓
STUDENT CAPABILITY
  ↓
STUDENT WORK
  ↓
OUTCOME
  ↓
DOWNSTREAM VALUE
```

The graph should preserve attribution without assigning all downstream outcomes entirely to one contributor.

## 5. Progression

Progression is an ecosystem recognition process, not a simple salary or popularity ranking.

Inputs can include:

- verified contribution;
- quality;
- reliability;
- demonstrated capability;
- learning;
- teaching/enabling others;
- downstream outcomes;
- stewardship;
- trust/evidence;
- sustained positive Pulse;
- responsible handling of obligations.

## 6. Teacher / mentor value

Teaching and mentorship may receive elevated recognition where they demonstrably multiply capability.

A teacher may create value through:

```text
KNOWLEDGE
 + CAPABILITY
 + PEOPLE ENABLED
 + DOWNSTREAM OUTCOMES
 + FUTURE CAPABILITY
```

The model must avoid double counting and distinguish correlation, contribution and causation.

## 7. Progression safeguards

Progression systems must not become arbitrary social credit systems. They require:

- transparent factors;
- contextual evaluation;
- evidence quality;
- dispute mechanisms;
- correction paths;
- privacy controls;
- anti-gaming detection;
- separation of personal dignity from performance scoring.

## 8. Economic position

Pulse and Value may inform economic position but do not directly determine legal ownership or financial accounting status.

Economic settlement belongs to IO.

## 9. No single scoreboard

The ecosystem should prefer a multidimensional profile over one universal rank.

Examples:

```text
Knowledge
Capability
Reliability
Teaching
Creation
Stewardship
Network contribution
Outcome
Continuity
Value enabled
```

A contextual evaluator may summarize these dimensions for a specific decision.

## 10. Becoming and Atlas

Progression connects Actual to Becoming and eventually Atlas:

```text
ACTUAL
 ↓
SIGNALS / CONTRIBUTIONS
 ↓
PULSE
 ↓
CURATION
 ↓
BECOMING
 ↓
DEMONSTRATED CAPABILITY / OUTCOME
 ↓
ATLAS
```

Atlas should present the evidence-backed result, while preserving the underlying continuity and provenance.

## 11. Core rule

> **Progression follows demonstrated value contribution and responsible stewardship, with Pulse as evidence of ecosystem response—not as a substitute for judgment, dignity or truth.**
