# OMNII UNIVERSAL LEDGER CONTRACT

## Purpose
Provide a universal auditable record of value, ownership, obligations, allocations, transactions, and consequential state changes.

## Ledger Entry
Each entry identifies entry ID, subject, source, target, type, amount/state, unit, timestamp, authority, provenance, correlation, and status.

## Invariants
Ledger history is append-oriented. Entries are attributable and reconciliable. Corrections reference prior entries rather than silently deleting economic history.

## Scope
The ledger may represent financial value, labor, resource allocation, obligations, credits, rights, contributions, and other governed value states.

## ABBA
ABBA may prepare, reconcile, classify, and route ledger operations but requires appropriate authority for commitment or settlement.
