# OMNII — Global Resilience & Disruption Intelligence

**Status:** CANONICAL CAPABILITY SCOPE  
**Scope:** Global-by-default  
**Layer:** Shared resilience and continuity intelligence

## Definition

Global Resilience & Disruption Intelligence is the reusable capability for detecting conditions that threaten economic or institutional pathways, understanding dependencies, modelling consequences and identifying alternative routes before or during disruption.

## Disruption universe

Supplier failure, factory shutdown, capacity loss, route interruption, port/airport disruption, border friction, regulatory shock, price shock, demand shock, technology obsolescence, infrastructure failure, energy shortage and concentration risk.

## Dependency model

Represent critical dependencies, concentration, substitution availability, single points of failure, recovery options and affected participants.

## Detection

Combine live/permitted operational signals, market intelligence, network conditions, supplier performance, regulatory change and historical outcomes. Distinguish observed events from inferred risks.

## Scenario analysis

Produce:

```text
baseline
likely disruption
severe disruption
alternative recovery path
multi-source resilience path
```

Keep assumptions, time horizon, confidence and impact scope visible.

## Continuity pathways

Possible actions include alternate supplier, alternate route, substitute product/process, inventory strategy, capacity pooling, financing, local production, repair/refurbishment and staged recovery.

## Alerts

Alerts should identify affected objects, reason, evidence, confidence, severity, expected timing and recommended next checks. Consequential changes require the appropriate authority.

## Interfaces

Connects to Network/Corridor, Sourcing, Opportunity, Matching, Market Intelligence, Verification/Trust, Resource/Capacity, Trade/Regulatory, Finance/Funding and Outcome Intelligence.

## Boundary

Not an autonomous continuity authority, insurer, regulator or emergency command system. It provides resilience intelligence and orchestration support within policy and authority boundaries.

## Canonical invariant

> **Resilience Intelligence makes dependencies and disruption pathways visible so participants can prepare, adapt and recover without surrendering decision authority.**
