# OMNII Ecosystem Conformance

**Status: CANONICAL — ecosystem composition and completeness standard**

## Purpose

This document defines the conformance test for any Carbon Actual ecosystem composition, including Emirati.

An ecosystem is conformant when arbitrary real-world situations can be represented, governed, operated, evidenced, measured, reported and evolved using the universal OMNII substrate without creating a competing constitutional graph or foundational kernel.

## Required coverage

Every ecosystem composition SHOULD be able to represent, as applicable:

1. **Objects** — people, families, organizations, institutions, places, assets, resources, services, products, agents and other identifiable participants.
2. **Relationships** — membership, ownership/custody, representation, delegation, trust, participation, hosting, sponsorship, dependency, communication and governance.
3. **Authority** — jurisdiction, mandate, role, delegation, permission, approval, revocation and escalation.
4. **Identity and provenance** — attributable identity, origin, version, lifecycle and source evidence.
5. **Situations** — planned, recurring, spontaneous, conditional, exceptional and emergency contexts.
6. **Time** — dates, intervals, recurring cycles, calendars, deadlines, temporal validity and availability windows.
7. **Data collection** — schemas, forms, validation, consent, attachments, versioning and audit.
8. **Process** — workflows, tasks, dependencies, approvals, retries, alternatives, emergency paths and human override.
9. **Rules** — policy, eligibility, etiquette, protocol, safety, access and routing constraints.
10. **Decisions** — accountable determinations, effective dates, conditions and review paths.
11. **Cases** — complaints, conflicts, investigations, incidents and managed exceptions.
12. **Evidence** — proof, sources, verification, confidence, provenance and audit trails.
13. **Resources** — people, equipment, animals, vehicles, venues, rooms, supplies and services.
14. **Capacity** — available, reserved, allocated, utilized and remaining capacity.
15. **Availability** — reservation, booking, rental, leasing and conditional access.
16. **Commitments** — promises, obligations, deliverables, due dates, fulfillment and breach.
17. **Commerce/value** — price, cost, contribution, transaction, obligation, value, economic position and reporting.
18. **Movement** — people, animals, vehicles, goods, services, routes and temporal movement.
19. **Communication** — notifications, announcements, invitations, acknowledgements and escalation.
20. **Safety/health/security** — readiness, incidents, response, medical/security resources and after-action evidence.
21. **Hospitality** — hosts, guests, accommodations, capacity, matching and continuity.
22. **Culture/protocol** — local rules, etiquette, ceremony sequence, heritage and language variation.
23. **Marketplace** — discovery, listing, matching, purchase, rental, reservation, fulfilment and reputation.
24. **Reporting** — operational, economic, safety, participation, compliance, sponsor, after-action and lessons-learned reporting.
25. **Simulation** — what-if scenarios, capacity tests, alternative plans and risk analysis.
26. **Localization** — local authority, language, calendar, custom, terminology and process configuration.
27. **Unknown handling** — discovery, classification, temporary composition, authority, evidence, review and learning for novel situations.
28. **Continuity** — preservation of identity, knowledge, relationships, assets, obligations, decisions and institutional memory across cycles.

## Conformance rule

An implementation that lacks one of these capabilities MAY still be a valid limited product, but it is not a complete ecosystem composition until the omitted capability is either:

- provided by OMNII universally; or
- explicitly declared out of domain for that ecosystem with an authoritative boundary.

## Anti-gap rule

Before adding a new primitive, ask:

`Is this genuinely new constitutional meaning, or can it be represented by Object + Relationship + Authority + State + Event + Capability + Resource + Composition + Continuity + Integration and the universal composition contracts?`

Default answer: compose.

## Emirati acceptance test

Emirati must be able to express, without a new constitutional object:

- Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha;
- Sallah/Durbar and horse participation;
- weddings and marriage-related ceremonies;
- funerals and condolence processes;
- trade fairs and cultural markets;
- family visits and spontaneous arrivals;
- council and institutional meetings;
- tourism and hospitality;
- youth programmes;
- sponsorships and partnerships;
- emergencies and incident response;
- horse/equipment rental and marketplace activity;
- accommodation and hosting;
- transportation and routing;
- forms, dates, permissions, reports and evidence;
- unforeseen or novel situations.

If a new situation cannot be represented, the missing element MUST first be analyzed as a schema, rule, workflow, resource, relationship, composition or other universal contract before proposing a constitutional change.

## Vendor independence

No paid/proprietary coordinator, workflow service, event engine or vendor platform is a constitutional dependency. Equivalent capabilities MUST be implementable with open standards, ordinary application code, self-hosted infrastructure or replaceable providers.

A paid service may be an optional implementation adapter only when its removal does not invalidate constitutional semantics or ecosystem correctness.
