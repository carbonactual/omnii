# EPISTEMIC

**Status: CANONICAL CROSS-CUTTING CONSTITUTIONAL CONTRACT**

EPISTEMIC governs what OMNII knows, how it knows it, and how certainty is represented.

## Invariants
- Reality is distinct from its representation.
- Observation, claim, inference, hypothesis and simulation are distinct.
- Provenance is mandatory for consequential assertions.
- Confidence belongs to the assertion, never to reality itself.
- Unknown, disputed and unverified are valid states.
- Occurrence time, observation time, recording time, effective time and supersession time are distinct where applicable.
- Supersession preserves historical assertions.

## ABBA
ABBA may infer and recommend, but inference must remain distinguishable from observation and verified fact.
