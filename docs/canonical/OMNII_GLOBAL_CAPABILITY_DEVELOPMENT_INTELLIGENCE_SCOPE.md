# OMNII — Global Capability Development Intelligence

**Status:** CANONICAL CAPABILITY SCOPE  
**Scope:** Global-by-default  
**Layer:** Shared capability-gap and development intelligence

## Definition

Global Capability Development Intelligence is the reusable capability for diagnosing missing capabilities and determining what must be learned, built, acquired, adapted, financed or partnered to make a desired outcome possible.

## Gap universe

Identify gaps in:

```text
skills
people
machinery
production
materials
technology
standards
certification
infrastructure
capital
management
market access
logistics
information
```

## Development pathways

Evaluate alternatives including training, apprenticeships, certification, equipment acquisition, used/refurbished equipment, shared facilities, contract manufacturing, technology transfer, licensing, local fabrication, partnerships, joint ventures, industrial clusters and external sourcing.

## Maturity

Track:

```text
identified → designed → resourced → pilot → tested → qualified → repeatable → scalable
```

Capability maturity is separate from legal licensing and does not imply market eligibility.

## Local and global development

The engine compares local, regional, continental and global ways to close a gap. Local development may be preferred where it creates durable value, but global capability remains available.

## Development packages

A package can combine people, equipment, training, standards, finance, facility, technology, buyer demand and operational support into a staged pathway.

## Interfaces

Connects to Opportunity, Sourcing, Matching, Resource/Capacity Intelligence, Market Intelligence, Research, Verification/Trust, Finance/Funding, Trade/Regulatory and Outcome Intelligence.

## Boundary

It identifies and composes development pathways; it does not become an accreditation authority, training regulator, lender, manufacturer or government planning authority.

## Canonical invariant

> **Capability Development Intelligence turns a capability gap into a set of evidence-backed pathways for closing it.**
