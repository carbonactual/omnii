# OMNII Scope Taxonomy

**Status: CANONICAL HARDENING RECORD — 2026-09-05**

OMNII remains one constitutional substrate capable of representing reality across time, scale, intelligence class and technological novelty.

## Horizons

| Horizon | Meaning | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| OLD | Historical reality, legacy institutions, records, practices and systems | Preserve as evidence and lineage; never inherit obsolete semantics as constitutional truth |
| NOW | Current human, institutional, territorial, economic, digital and physical reality | Operational reality governed by the universal runtime |
| FUTURE | Planned, designed, forecast or intended reality | Represent as plan, commitment, scenario or possible/probable state; never present as actual without evidence |
| EMERGING | New technologies, institutions, markets, social forms and intelligence patterns | Admit through versioned contracts and contain provisional semantics |
| UNKNOWN_ALIEN | Unidentified, non-human, synthetic, extraterrestrial or otherwise not-yet-modeled phenomena/actors | Represent provisionally with provenance and uncertainty; no inferred authority, trust, intent or consent |

## Universal subject classes

`HUMAN | FAMILY | COMMUNITY | ORGANIZATION | INSTITUTION | TERRITORY | ECOSYSTEM | SPECIES | ANIMAL | PLANT | MACHINE | VEHICLE | ROBOT | SOFTWARE | DATASET | AI | AGENT | MULTI_AGENT_SYSTEM | SWARM | SYNTHETIC_ENTITY | UNKNOWN_ENTITY`

## Value-bearing and obligation-bearing classes

`MONEY | CURRENCY | TOKEN | SECURITY | EQUITY | DEBT | CREDIT | ROYALTY | LICENSE | PERMIT | CERTIFICATE | CREDENTIAL | CONTRACT | LEASE | SUBSCRIPTION | MEMBERSHIP | FRANCHISE | MANDATE | COLLATERAL | PROPERTY | LAND | RESOURCE_RIGHT | DATA_RIGHT | AI_RIGHT | ACCESS_RIGHT | CARBON_RIGHT | WATER_RIGHT | ENERGY_RIGHT | ATTENTION | REPUTATION | POSITION | RANK | CLEARANCE | SKILL | TIME | CAPACITY | OPPORTUNITY`

All economic/value objects remain subject to identity, provenance, authority, custody/ownership semantics as applicable, transfer conditions, settlement state and feedback. Tokenization alone does not prove ownership or authority.

## Reusable operational classes

`EVENT | STATE | COMMAND | ACTION | EXECUTION | WORKFLOW | CASE | DECISION | POLICY | RULE | FORM | APPLICATION | APPOINTMENT | RESERVATION | CAPACITY | INVENTORY | ASSET | LIABILITY | SERVICE | PRODUCT | MARKET | ROUTE | LOCATION | JOURNEY | COMMUNICATION | MESSAGE | DOCUMENT | RECORD | EVIDENCE | PROOF | OBSERVATION | TELEMETRY | INCIDENT | RISK | ISSUE | OPPORTUNITY | SCENARIO | SIMULATION | FORECAST | PLAN | COMMITMENT | OBLIGATION`

These are composition types, not additional constitutional kernels.

## Civilizational scale

`PERSON → FAMILY → COMMUNITY → CITY → TERRITORY → EMIRATE/KINGDOM/GOVERNED CULTURAL-TERRITORIAL UNIT → NATION → REGION → FEDERATION → PLANETARY SYSTEM → INTERPLANETARY SYSTEM → INTERSTELLAR/MULTI-WORLD SYSTEM → CIVILIZATION`

Scale never creates authority automatically.

## Intelligence scale

`INDIVIDUAL_INTELLIGENCE → AI → AGENT → AGENT_CREW → MULTI_AGENT_SYSTEM → ORGANIZATIONAL_INTELLIGENCE → INSTITUTIONAL_INTELLIGENCE → COLLECTIVE_INTELLIGENCE → MACHINE_COLLECTIVE → UNKNOWN_INTELLIGENCE`

Intelligence can interpret, reason, recommend, plan, simulate and execute only within explicit authority and capability bounds.

## Knowledge/evidence progression

`DATA → INFORMATION → KNOWLEDGE → MODEL → THEORY → UNDERSTANDING → INSIGHT → WISDOM → FORECAST → FORESIGHT`

Claims can link to source, provenance, confidence, evidence and temporal validity. A forecast is not an observation; a simulation is not proof of existence.

## Reality/time states

`ACTUAL | HISTORICAL | OBSERVED | PLANNED | COMMITTED | SIMULATED | POSSIBLE | PROBABLE | PREFERRED | REJECTED | UNKNOWN`

State is always distinct from the event or command that caused or described it. Promotion between states is governed and evidenced.

## Extra-domain readiness

The taxonomy intentionally accommodates physical and digital infrastructure, biology, ecology, culture, religion, governance, transport, energy, finance, property, health, education, agriculture, media, communications, robotics, autonomous vehicles, satellites, space systems, synthetic entities and future domains without adding a kernel for each.

## Conformance

Every addition declares:

`horizon + architectural class + canonical type + lifecycle + provenance + authority context`

and, where applicable:

`relationships + dependencies + uncertainty + temporal validity + capabilities + resources + execution + state + event + audit + Pulse`

Required architectural class:

`constitutional/core | reusable capability | domain module | product | institutional configuration | integration adapter | presentation | reference/speculative`

Universal rule:

`reuse → configure → compose → extend domain contract → create shared capability → constitutional amendment`

The final step requires demonstrated foundational insufficiency.
