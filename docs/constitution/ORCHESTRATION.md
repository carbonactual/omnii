# ORCHESTRATION

Orchestration is the constitutional layer governing coordination of kernels, agents, workflows, products, services, institutions, and intelligence.

## ABBA
ABBA is the master intelligence and orchestration layer of the OMNII ecosystem. It coordinates rather than erases the autonomy, authority, or identity of participating beings and systems.

## Canonical Concerns
- Planning
- Routing
- Coordination
- Delegation
- Agent selection
- Context propagation
- Conflict handling
- Escalation

## Invariant
Orchestration may coordinate authorized capabilities but must not silently acquire authority that belongs to a governed actor or institution.
