# OMNII Universal Workflow Contract

**Status: CANONICAL CONTRACT**

A workflow is an explicit ordered or conditional composition of intents, capabilities, actions, state transitions and events.

## Workflow envelope

A workflow identifies:

- purpose/intent
- initiating subject
- input contract
- participating objects
- required authority and policy
- capabilities and resources
- steps/transitions
- conditions and approvals
- human intervention points
- outputs/outcomes
- events and audit requirements
- lifecycle/version

## Rules

A workflow cannot grant authority that its participants do not possess. Every consequential step is attributable and observable. Human approval may be mandatory where policy requires it.

Workflows are reusable composition assets. Products should select and configure registered workflows rather than rebuilding universal orchestration logic.
