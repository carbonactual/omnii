# OMNII Constitutional Architecture

**Status: CANONICAL — strengthened Phase 1–40 architecture**

This document is the single authoritative architecture description for OMNII. Supporting documents may explain, specialize, or implement a component, but may not redefine its canonical semantics.

> **OMNII is a constitutional operating environment for human–AI civilization-scale composition. It is a Being → Becoming architecture, not merely a software or application architecture.**

## 0. Constitutional Frame

OMNII is organized around a closed constitutional feedback model:

```text
BEING
  ↓
INTELLIGENCE
  ↓
IDENTITY + AUTHORITY
  ↓
FOUNDATION
  ↓
DOMAINS
  ↓
RUNTIME
  ↓
FLOOR
  ↓
PULSE
  ↓
EVIDENCE / LEARNING
  ↓
BECOMING
  ↓
BEING
```

This is a framing model, not a replacement for the canonical object graph or foundational kernel sequence below. It describes how constitutional meaning, intelligence, identity, authority, canonical objects, domains, execution, lived reality, feedback and evolution relate.

The loop is closed in the sense that operational reality produces evidence and change that can inform learning and Becoming. It is **not** closed in the sense of self-authorizing governance: capability may expand, but authority remains explicit.

The governing trajectory is:

`Constitution → Runtime → Ecosystem → Civilization → Learning → Evolution → Becoming`

## 1. Being

**Being** is the highest-order constitutional frame: the source of principles, laws, values, continuity, economic assumptions, governance constraints and the conditions under which systems may become.

Being is not a database, repository, application, model or ordinary runtime service. It is the constitutional meaning layer from which the rest of OMNII derives its permitted semantics.

Being may evolve through authorized evidence, governance and Becoming. Evolution does not permit arbitrary runtime components or agents to silently rewrite constitutional law.

## 2. Foundational Kernel Sequence

The canonical OMNII kernel sequence remains:

`BEING → IDENTITY → KNOWLEDGE → MOTION → VALUE → TRUST → RELATIONSHIP → INTENT → EXECUTION → CAPABILITY → RESOURCE → COMPOSITION → CONTINUITY → INTEGRATION`

These are foundational semantic kernels, not UI screens, repositories or application modules.

Governance, security, economics, ABBA/orchestration, audit, observability and resilience constrain and operate across the sequence; they are not additional foundational kernels.

The Being→Becoming frame organizes the architecture at a higher level without replacing this semantic sequence.

## 3. Intelligence — ABBA and Actual AI

**ABBA** is OMNII's master interpretive and orchestration intelligence.

ABBA may:

- perceive and interpret;
- reason and plan;
- curate and classify;
- route and coordinate;
- compose capabilities;
- delegate to agents;
- monitor execution;
- learn from evidence;
- escalate uncertainty or authority decisions.

ABBA is not constitutional authority. It cannot self-authorize, redefine canonical semantics, bypass policy, manufacture consent, or convert capability into authority.

**Actual AI** refers to AI capabilities operating through the interpreted, governed environment. AI can feed observations, analysis, predictions, proposed actions and learned artifacts upward into the system; governance and human authority determine what becomes authorized action.

The fundamental boundary is:

`Interpretation ≠ Authority`

## 4. Identity and Authority — # and SEAL

Every governed object uses the universal OMNII object envelope:

`identity, type, version, lifecycle, provenance, authority context, attributes, relationships, dependencies, capabilities, resources, timestamps and extensions.`

### # — Identity Geometry

`#` is the canonical identity/provenance geometry concept for representing an object's identity, continuity, proof, consent context, relationships and lifecycle across the ecosystem.

It may identify humans, families, organizations, products, assets, agents, property, contracts, vehicles, events, institutions and other governed objects.

`#` does not replace the universal object envelope. It is a higher-order identity abstraction expressed through that envelope and the canonical graph.

### SEAL — Authority and Consent

**SEAL** represents explicit human or otherwise legally/constitutionally valid authority, consent, approval and release boundaries where required.

SEAL does not replace authentication, authorization, policy or governance. Instead:

`Authentication establishes identity evidence → Authorization evaluates authority/policy → SEAL records/expresses required approval or consent → Execution proceeds within delegated boundaries.`

No system may infer human consent merely from capability, convenience, optimization, silence or prior activity where explicit consent is required.

## 5. Canonical Object Foundation

The Foundation is the canonical object/function family through which OMNII organizes identity, records, visibility, protected reality, active composition and continuity.

### TERMINAL

The controlled intake and routing boundary for governed inputs and requests. Terminal classifies and directs work according to identity, authority, policy, capability and route. It must not become an unconditional bypass-prevention slogan that contradicts an explicit integration contract; boundaries are defined by canonical contracts.

### ROOT

The continuity and origin context of an object: lineage, heritage, formation, relationships and other durable origin semantics where applicable.

### INDEX

The canonical indexing/collective-information function for histories, events, activities, receipts, logs, proof references and other discoverable records. Indexes do not silently become alternate systems of record for domains whose canonical authority resides elsewhere.

### ATLAS

The governed public/discoverable representation of canonical objects and their authorized public identity, value, proof and reputation. Atlas exposes only what policy and authority permit.

### VAULT

The protected reality/custody boundary for contracts, documents, treasury records, protected proofs, custody material and other sensitive records. Vault security does not itself establish ownership or authority.

### ACTUAL

The curated operational-reality layer where authorized opportunities, connections, capabilities, relationships, activation and current-state compositions become actionable.

Actual must distinguish observed facts, authorized actions, proposals, predictions and simulations.

### BECOMING

The durable evolution/continuity record through which states, learning, adaptations, transitions and authorized future development are preserved without erasing provenance.

Becoming is not an excuse for unbounded mutation. Evolution remains attributable, governed and auditable.

## 6. Universal Graph

The universal graph remains:

`Object --[typed Relationship]--> Object`

Consequential edges carry source, target, type, authority, provenance, lifecycle/status and temporal validity where required. Dependency edges are semantically distinct from ordinary relationships.

The Foundation objects, people, institutions, products, territories, agents and ecosystems all participate in this graph through the same canonical relationship semantics.

No product-specific graph may silently become an alternate universal graph.

## 7. Domains and Pillars

OMNII supports domain classification for civilization-scale composition. The eight-pillar model is retained as a useful high-level domain lens:

1. Finance
2. Religion
3. Creed
4. Education
5. Governance
6. Trade
7. Community
8. Infrastructure

These are **domains, not constitutional kernels**. They do not replace the universal graph, object envelope, legal/jurisdictional boundaries or the foundational kernel sequence.

Every product, service, registry, workflow or institution may map to one or more applicable domains, while cross-domain systems remain first-class compositions.

The pillar list is extensible where evidence and constitutional governance require additional domains. The existence of eight pillars does not prohibit other legitimate domain classifications.

## 8. Events, Pulse and State

An **event** records an occurrence or observation and preserves attribution, provenance, time, correlation and outcome where applicable. Events are not state, commands, queries, actions, executions or workflows.

**State** is the modeled condition of an object or system. State transitions are governed operations and should produce observable events. State is not an event log.

**Pulse** is the ecosystem-level feedback abstraction over meaningful activity and change. Activity, movement, communication, relationships, transactions, proofs, events and state transitions may generate Pulse where the applicable domain contract defines them as consequential.

Pulse therefore connects:

`Floor → Evidence → Learning → Becoming → Being`

Pulse is not a currency, permission, authority token or substitute for an event schema. It is a cross-system feedback concept implemented through canonical events, state transitions, telemetry and governed evidence.

## 9. Registries

Registries index canonical objects, relationships, dependencies, capabilities, resources, products, services and agents.

A registry is a system of record/index for one semantic domain; duplicate registries must not create alternate definitions.

Registry design must preserve:

`identity + provenance + authority + lifecycle + ownership/custody + relationships + auditability.`

## 10. Runtime

The universal runtime is the implementation boundary that resolves:

`identity → authority → policy → capability → resource → dependency → execution → state transition → event → audit`

The runtime implements constitutional contracts. It does not define constitutional authority merely because it executes it.

Persistence, transport, storage, databases and provider implementations remain replaceable infrastructure beneath the contracts.

### IO

IO is the interaction boundary through which governed inputs, outputs, signals and exchanges enter or leave a system.

### HAPI

HAPI is the human connection and continuity boundary: human onboarding, identity connection, trust, participation, incubation and interaction with the governed environment.

HAPI is not merely OAuth, middleware or a UI. It represents the human relationship to OMNII while still using standard identity, authentication, authorization and integration technologies underneath.

### ECC

ECC — Execution Coordination Center — coordinates authorized agents, tasks, workflows, repositories, communications, integrations, products, services and human work.

ECC is an orchestration/execution function, not a constitutional authority. It executes within the same identity, authority, policy, capability, resource and audit boundaries as every other runtime participant.

## 11. Agents

Agents are runtime participants governed by an agent contract. They plan, reason, coordinate and execute only within delegated authority and capability/resource constraints.

Agents are not constitutional dependencies.

ABBA may coordinate agents, but neither ABBA nor an agent may acquire constitutional authority through scale, optimization, consensus, learning or autonomy.

## 12. Governance

Governance answers:

- who may act;
- what they may do;
- why they may do it;
- under what authority;
- for how long;
- against which object or domain;
- with which resources and capabilities;
- subject to which jurisdiction and policy;
- who can revoke, review or audit the action.

Governance is cross-cutting. It does not become a hidden product layer.

## 13. Security and Trust

Authentication establishes identity evidence.

Authorization evaluates authority and policy.

Permissions are concrete allowed operations.

Secrets and data protection secure implementation state.

Trust is a separate modeled confidence/assurance concept.

Consent is distinct from authentication, authorization and trust. Where human consent is required, SEAL and the applicable governance contract preserve that distinction.

## 14. Value and Economy

Value, money, asset, resource, ownership, contribution, price, cost, obligation, right, ledger entry and transaction remain separate concepts.

Economic implementations must remain auditable, attributable and policy-governed.

No value system may silently become a substitute for authority, identity or constitutional governance.

## 15. Composition

Products, services, workflows, organizations, institutions, territories and ecosystems compose canonical objects and capabilities.

Composition cannot redefine canonical primitives.

A product is a manifestation of OMNII, not the architecture itself.

A product may therefore be represented through:

`# + identity + route + authority + value + applicable domain/pillar + HAPI relationship + Pulse + Becoming`

without creating a separate constitutional model.

## 16. Human Layer

People are canonical object participants with identity, agency, relationships, consent, authority and continuity.

Human authority cannot be silently substituted by agent optimization.

Human continuity may span life, family, relationships, work, assets, knowledge, identity, legacy and future state while preserving privacy, consent, provenance, legal context and agency.

## 17. Organization and Institution Layer

Organizations and institutions are governed object compositions with mandates, membership, roles, resources, policies, services, accountability and lifecycle.

Schools, hospitals, businesses, governments, courts, associations, communities and financial institutions can therefore become native OMNII compositions without receiving special constitutional primitives merely because of their category.

## 18. Territory Layer

Territories compose:

`people + institutions + infrastructure + resources + services + economy + events + governance`

Territorial models remain subordinate to applicable law, jurisdiction and constitutional authority.

## 19. Ecosystem / World Layer

Ecosystems and worlds are higher-order compositions of people, organizations, products, communities, territories, institutions, infrastructure, agents, services and resources.

Phase 21–40 architecture belongs here. Ecosystems use the canonical graph and object envelope rather than defining competing ones.

Local autonomy remains real. Shared substrate does not imply shared authority.

## 20. The Floor — Operational Reality

The **Floor** is the operational reality in which constitutional objects and capabilities are actually exercised.

It includes, depending on domain:

- banking and finance;
- education;
- government;
- property;
- agriculture;
- healthcare;
- elections;
- procurement;
- logistics;
- employment;
- commerce;
- infrastructure;
- community activity;
- institutional services;
- physical-world operations.

The Floor is not a lower-value layer. It is where OMNII's constitutional commitments encounter reality.

Every consequential Floor action must remain traceable to identity, authority, policy, capability, resource, state transition and evidence as applicable.

The constitutional feedback loop and object lifecycle route are distinct but compatible.

### High-level constitutional loop

```text
  ↓
FOUNDATION
  ↓
DOMAINS
  ↓
RUNTIME
  ↓

  ↓

  ↓
```

### Typical governed object route

```text
      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

```

This is a reference route, not a universal requirement that every object traverse every Foundation function. Contracts determine which boundaries apply.

## 22. Operating Law

A cross-cutting design heuristic for OMNII is:

`ROLE + ROUTE + RETURN + BOUNDARY + STATE`

Every consequential component or composition should make these explicit:

- **ROLE** — what the object/function is responsible for;
- **ROUTE** — how authority, data, capability and work move;
- **RETURN** — what feedback, result, evidence or value returns;
- **BOUNDARY** — where authority, jurisdiction, privacy, trust and capability stop;
- **STATE** — what condition exists before and after consequential change.

This is a design law/heuristic, not a replacement for canonical schemas and contracts.

## 23. Infrastructure Boundary

Technical infrastructure implements OMNII; it does not constitute OMNII's constitutional meaning.

Infrastructure may include identity systems, DNS/HNS/ENS/DID/VC mechanisms, authentication, passkeys, consent systems, policy engines, secrets, encryption, agent runtimes, tool gateways, vector memory, knowledge graphs, event buses, workflow engines, communication routers, ledgers, payment rails, governance tooling, analytics, search, physical proof, marketplaces and redress systems.

These are implementation capabilities that may evolve, be replaced or be composed differently while the constitutional contracts remain stable.

Repository names, database schemas, Vercel routes, framework choices and provider-specific services are therefore **implementation references, not constitutional layers**.

## 24. Products and Services

Products are manifestations of OMNII's constitutional and runtime capabilities.

Examples may include BUNK and other domain products such as SPARE, NGIN, Charter, PabloPay, Open Polling or InstituteGPT.

Their names, repositories and implementation boundaries may change. Their canonical relationship to OMNII is what matters.

Products must not redefine Being, identity, authority, the universal graph, the object envelope or constitutional governance.

## 25. Phase 21–40 Architecture

The phases remain layered as:

```text
FOUNDATIONAL CONSTITUTION
        ↓
UNIVERSAL RUNTIME
        ↓
ECOSYSTEM RUNTIME (21–25)
        ↓
GOVERNED SELF-AUDIT / EVOLUTION (26–30)
        ↓
HUMAN / INSTITUTION / TERRITORY / CIVIC / PLANETARY COMPOSITION (31–35)
        ↓
INTER-CIVILIZATIONAL / MULTI-INTELLIGENCE / MULTI-WORLD HORIZONS (36–40)
```

Phase 26 self-audit produces evidence and findings; it does not silently mutate constitutional law.

Phase 27 evolution proposals remain non-canonical until applicable authority approves them and compatibility, safety, provenance and migration requirements are satisfied.

Phase 28 self-healing may repair implementation and recoverable runtime faults but may not silently rewrite constitutional authority or durable governance decisions.

Phases 31–35 remain conditional on demonstrated multi-ecosystem reliability, interoperability, simulation, security, resilience and institutional governance.

Phases 36–40 remain conditional architectural horizons. Their future authority models and implementation details must emerge from evidence rather than being prematurely frozen.

Phase 27 is currently an evidence gap: no Phase 27 package or commit is present in the repository. It is not treated as implemented merely because the roadmap names it.

Phase 40's `CivilizationNode`/`CivilizationRelation` model is an implementation-specific view/adapter candidate, not a second canonical graph.

## 26. Canonical / Implementation / Extension / Future

### CANONICAL

Constitutional frame, foundational kernels, universal object envelope, graph, authority boundaries, core semantic contracts and the distinctions between identity, state, event, capability, resource, value, trust, relationship and execution.

### IMPLEMENTATION

Concrete databases, APIs, runtimes, adapters, providers, infrastructure, executable packages, UI routes and deployment systems.

### EXTENSION

Domain objects, products, registries, workflows, institutions, territories, ecosystems and compositions that use canonical contracts without redefining them.

### FUTURE

Phase 31–40 capabilities whose production implementation is not proven by current repository evidence.

No implementation detail becomes constitutional merely because it is widely deployed.

## 27. Constitutional Invariants

1. **Capability can expand. Authority must remain explicit.**
2. **Interpretation does not equal authority.**
3. **Human consent cannot be inferred where explicit consent is required.**
4. **Products compose the architecture; they do not redefine it.**
5. **Agents execute under delegated authority; they are not constitutional authorities.**
6. **Events are not state; state is not an event log.**
7. **Registries do not create alternate definitions of canonical objects.**
8. **External providers are replaceable beneath stable contracts.**
9. **Jurisdiction and local autonomy remain explicit.**
10. **Evidence may inform evolution, but evidence alone does not authorize constitutional change.**
11. **No phase is considered implemented solely because a roadmap names it.**
12. **No future horizon may silently redefine the canonical substrate.**

## 28. Core Dependency Rule

No constitutional kernel depends on a product, specific agent, provider, implementation framework or future horizon.

ABBA coordinates the substrate; governance constrains it; SEAL expresses required approval/consent; HAPI connects human participation; ECC coordinates authorized execution; products, institutions, territories and ecosystems compose canonical capabilities.

The architecture therefore remains capable of evolving without becoming constitutionally self-authorizing.
