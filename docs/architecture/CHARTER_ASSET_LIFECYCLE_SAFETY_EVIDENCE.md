# CHARTER — Asset Lifecycle, Safety & Evidence Layer

**Status:** Canonical Charter build specification
**Domain:** Universal transport / movement
**Boundary:** Charter owns movement context; canonical lifecycle, authority, identity and evidence semantics remain reusable OMNII capabilities; IO owns value movement.

## Asset lifecycle

Transport assets and equipment can be composed across:

```text
CONCEPT
 → DESIGN
 → BUILD
 → IDENTIFY
 → CERTIFY / APPROVE
 → DEPLOY
 → OPERATE
 → INSPECT
 → MAINTAIN
 → REPAIR
 → MODIFY / CONVERT
 → REFURBISH
 → TRANSFER / HANDOFF
 → RETIRE
 → RECYCLE / PRESERVE
```

Lifecycle stages are contextual and use existing OMNII lifecycle semantics.

## Safety

Charter safety workflows can represent:

- hazard identification;
- operational constraints;
- inspection;
- testing;
- certification status;
- crew/operator qualification;
- maintenance readiness;
- incident detection;
- emergency response;
- evacuation/rescue;
- recovery;
- corrective action;
- return-to-service evidence.

Charter does not replace jurisdictional safety regulation or create a universal regulatory authority.

## Evidence

Transport evidence can include:

- specifications;
- inspection records;
- maintenance records;
- diagnostic results;
- test results;
- telemetry;
- journey events;
- location history;
- incident records;
- recorder/black-box outputs;
- photographs/video/media;
- provenance;
- certification evidence;
- operator credentials.

Evidence states must distinguish verified, reported, inferred, disputed, unknown, simulated and hypothetical information.

## Investigation

Where an incident occurs:

```text
INCIDENT
 → PRESERVE
 → COLLECT
 → CONTROL ACCESS
 → ANALYZE
 → DETERMINE FINDINGS
 → CORRECT
 → VERIFY
 → RETURN / RESTRICT / RETIRE
```

Access to sensitive evidence remains subject to canonical authority and privacy/security controls.

## Customization and modification

Vehicles, vessels, aircraft and equipment may be customized, restored, wrapped, repainted, converted, tuned or otherwise modified. Charter records the movement-relevant configuration, provenance and operational consequences while canonical asset identity/lifecycle remains elsewhere.

## Cultural and historical layer

Collections, museums, garages, exhibitions, racing history, film/television appearances, creators, restoration projects and memorabilia can be represented as relationships to canonical assets, people, organizations, places and media.

This supports collector/enthusiast workflows without turning Charter into a separate media or collectibles ontology.

## Invariant

> **Every operational claim should have an appropriate state and evidence context; every safety decision must respect authority and provenance.**
