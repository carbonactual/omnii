# OMNII Post-Phase-20 Roadmap

**Status:** Architectural roadmap; phases 21–40 are not implementation requirements until their prerequisites are validated.

## Purpose

Phase 20 establishes the Universal Operating Environment. From this point, OMNII shifts from primarily constructing shared platform primitives toward operating, composing, governing, learning, and evolving systems built on those primitives.

The roadmap deliberately separates near-term implementation from increasingly conditional architectural horizons.

## Epoch III — Universal Ecosystem

### Phase 21 — Universal Ecosystem Runtime

OMNII can host and operate multiple independent ecosystems on the same constitutional substrate.

Canonical composition:

`Person → Organization → Product → Community → Territory → Ecosystem`

Requirements include ecosystem identity, boundaries, local governance, scoped registries, lifecycle, tenancy/isolation, resource domains, ecosystem-level runtime context, and controlled composition with the shared constitutional layer.

Local autonomy must remain real. A shared substrate cannot imply shared authority.

### Phase 22 — Cross-Ecosystem Interoperability

Independent OMNII ecosystems can communicate and exchange governed objects or capabilities.

`Ecosystem A ↔ Ecosystem B ↔ External Systems`

Portability may cover identity, trust, value, resources, relationships, agents, services, knowledge, and capability references while preserving ownership, consent, authority, provenance, and ecosystem boundaries.

### Phase 23 — Universal Coordination Layer

ABBA evolves from ecosystem orchestrator to multi-ecosystem coordination intelligence.

It may coordinate people, organizations, agents, markets, infrastructure, institutions, territories, and ecosystems within declared authority and policy boundaries.

ABBA does not acquire constitutional authority through scale, optimization, consensus, or learned behavior.

### Phase 24 — Universal Simulation & World Modeling

OMNII adds a world-modeling substrate representing:

`current state → possible state → intended state → transition paths`

The model supports simulation of policies, markets, infrastructure, organizations, emergencies, resource allocation, and complex system interactions before consequential execution.

Simulation remains distinguishable from fact, prediction from observation, and proposed futures from authorized plans.

### Phase 25 — Universal Planning & Foresight

Planning integrates observation, knowledge, simulation, prediction, scenario generation, decision, authorization, and execution.

`Observation → Knowledge → Simulation → Prediction → Scenario → Decision → Execution`

ABBA gains strategic foresight capabilities without bypassing governance or reserved human decisions.

## Epoch IV — Self-Evolving OMNII

### Phase 26 — Constitutional Self-Audit

OMNII continuously evaluates itself for contradictions, broken dependencies, authority violations, capability drift, security failures, schema drift, economic inconsistency, and architectural decay.

Self-audit produces evidence and findings; it does not silently mutate constitutional law.

### Phase 27 — Constitutional Evolution

OMNII may generate architectural improvement proposals from validated evidence.

The invariant is:

**ABBA may propose. Systems may simulate. Governance must authorize.**

A proposed evolution remains non-canonical until the applicable authority approves and the change passes compatibility, safety, provenance, and migration requirements.

### Phase 28 — Self-Healing Infrastructure

Runtime infrastructure gains governed recovery loops:

`detect → isolate → repair → recover → validate → learn`

Self-healing may repair implementation state, infrastructure, and recoverable runtime faults but may not silently rewrite constitutional law, authority, or durable governance decisions.

### Phase 29 — Universal Learning Loop

The ecosystem becomes an explicit learning system:

`Experience → Evidence → Knowledge → Evaluation → Learning → Adaptation`

Learning artifacts are versioned, evaluated, attributable, reversible where feasible, and separated from constitutional authority.

### Phase 30 — OMNII Meta-Architecture

OMNII can derive new system architectures from canonical primitives and validated composition patterns.

A new product, institution, service, territory system, agent network, or economic system becomes primarily a governed composition problem rather than a greenfield architecture problem.

## Epoch V — Civilization Infrastructure

### Phase 31 — Universal Human Continuity Layer

Represent long-term continuity across:

`life → family → relationships → work → assets → knowledge → identity → legacy`

Continuity must preserve consent, privacy, authority, legal context, provenance, and human agency.

### Phase 32 — Universal Institutional Operating Layer

Institutions become first-class constitutional/runtime participants, including schools, hospitals, businesses, governments, courts, associations, communities, and financial institutions.

Institutional mandates, offices, authority, records, resources, procedures, services, accountability, and continuity become composable through shared primitives.

### Phase 33 — Universal Territorial Operating Layer

A territory becomes a living governed graph of:

`people + institutions + infrastructure + resources + economy + services + events + governance`

Territorial systems remain subordinate to applicable constitutional and legal authority.

### Phase 34 — Universal Civic & Governance Network

Connect institutions and territories into interoperable governance structures while preserving jurisdiction, authority, local autonomy, public accountability, and explicit cross-boundary agreements.

### Phase 35 — Planetary Systems Layer

OMNII can represent and coordinate planetary-scale systems such as energy, food, water, transport, climate, health, communication, resources, trade, population, and infrastructure.

This phase is conditional on validated multi-territory governance, simulation, interoperability, resilience, and observability primitives.

## Epoch VI — Beyond a Single Civilization

### Phase 36 — Inter-Civilizational Protocol

Define interoperability between radically different societies, institutions, governance systems, and intelligence systems without requiring a single cultural or political model.

### Phase 37 — Multi-Intelligence Constitution

Represent governed relationships between humans, AI agents, autonomous systems, and future forms of intelligence.

Different participants may have different capacities, rights, responsibilities, authority models, and safety constraints.

### Phase 38 — Multi-World / Multi-Territory Architecture

Extend the universal graph to represent many worlds, territories, large-scale environments, simulations, or civilization-scale domains simultaneously.

### Phase 39 — Universal Civilization Exchange Layer

Enable governed exchange of identity, knowledge, value, capability, resources, and relationships across large organizational and territorial boundaries.

Exchange never implies unrestricted portability or authority transfer.

### Phase 40 — OMNII Universal Civilization Operating Environment

The next major architectural endpoint: OMNII as a constitutional operating environment for intelligent civilization-scale systems, rather than merely an operating system for applications.

The objective is not centralized control. It is interoperable composition under explicit constitutional, jurisdictional, human, institutional, and machine-governed boundaries.

## Epoch VII — Becoming

Phase 40 is not a mathematical terminal state.

Once OMNII can model, compose, govern, learn, and evolve, subsequent architecture becomes open-ended.

The governing trajectory is:

`Constitution → Runtime → Ecosystem → Civilization → Learning → Evolution → Becoming`

This is intentionally not specified as a finite implementation backlog. Later architecture must emerge from evidence produced by earlier phases.

## Phase-gating doctrine

### Phases 21–25

Specify precisely enough to build against validated Phase 1–20 primitives.

### Phases 26–30

Treat as governed evolution capabilities. No self-modification may bypass authorization, review, provenance, or rollback requirements.

### Phases 31–35

Treat as civilization-scale expansion capabilities. Their implementation should be conditioned on demonstrated multi-ecosystem reliability, interoperability, simulation, security, and institutional governance.

### Phases 36–40

Treat as conditional architectural horizons. Do not freeze implementation details, authority models, or assumptions about future intelligence until earlier layers demonstrate the need and provide evidence.

## Non-negotiable invariant

OMNII may become more capable without becoming constitutionally self-authorizing.

**Capability can expand. Authority must remain explicit.**
