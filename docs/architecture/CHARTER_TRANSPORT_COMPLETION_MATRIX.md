# CHARTER — Universal Transport Completion Matrix

## Purpose

Close the transport/movement architecture at the source without creating competing product primitives.

## Canonical layers

| Layer | Function |
|---|---|
| OMNII | canonical object, identity, lifecycle, authority, relationship and evidence substrate |
| HAPI | human/agent identity, capability, qualification, training and progression |
| Fleet | organizational management of transport assets and operations |
| Charter | universal movement capability, journey composition, routing, dispatch, execution and recovery |
| Logistico | goods/cargo movement workflow |
| NAB | automobile biography/registry government handoff |
| Pilgrim | pilgrimage movement experience/context |
| Hitch | hitchhiking movement experience/context |
| ABBA | ecosystem intelligence and orchestration |
| IO | value movement only |

## Transport coverage

Charter's movement capability model remains open to:

- walking, running and human-powered movement;
- bicycles, tricycles, motorcycles and scooters;
- cars, taxis, ride-hailing, carpooling and chauffeured vehicles;
- buses, coaches, school transport and public transport;
- trucks, trailers, tow trucks, recovery vehicles and heavy haulage;
- ambulances, fire and emergency vehicles;
- agricultural, construction, mining and specialist machines where movement is the operational function;
- trains, trams, metros and rail systems;
- ships, ferries, cargo vessels, fishing vessels, yachts and other marine craft;
- submarines and underwater vehicles;
- aircraft, helicopters, balloons and airships;
- drones and autonomous movement systems;
- spacecraft and commercial space transportation;
- animals and animal-powered transport including horses, camels, donkeys and sled animals;
- future and currently unknown movement modes represented through extensible capabilities and evidence.

## Journey lifecycle

`intent → discovery → eligibility → availability → reservation → assignment → preparation → pickup/boarding → active movement → handoff → exception/recovery → completion → evidence`

Cancellation, delay, refusal, breakdown, route obstruction, safety incident and service mismatch are explicit execution states, not silent failures.

## Service integrity

A confirmed movement must preserve its accepted operating terms. Any legitimate change is explicit, attributable and recoverable. The system must detect and provide escalation paths for:

- post-acceptance renegotiation;
- vehicle/specification mismatch;
- unsafe or unfit vehicle condition;
- identity mismatch;
- unauthorized substitution;
- abandonment;
- coercive or non-transport exchange demands;
- route or destination deviation;
- unavailable capability;
- delay and cancellation;
- breakdown and recovery requirement.

## Operational evidence

Transport state may reference:

- position and location;
- time and timeline;
- capacity and load;
- vehicle/asset condition;
- operator qualification;
- infrastructure status;
- route and waypoint state;
- inspection and maintenance state;
- incident and recovery state;
- service commitments;
- custody/handoff state;
- registry provenance.

## Fleet/HAPI separation

A vehicle is an asset operationally managed by Fleet. A person operating it is a HAPI entity/agent. Charter composes both into movement and never creates a second crew or asset ontology.

## Product extraction

Products are compositions, not new primitives. A ride-hailing product, luxury Charter product, public transport product, pilgrimage transport, hitchhiking service, fleet product or Logistico product can be extracted from the same substrate.

## Economic boundary

Charter may express eligibility, obligations, reservations, ownership-program references and value requirements. It does not implement money, payment, settlement, savings ledgers or financing primitives. Those belong to IO.

## Completion invariant

> **Everything that moves, everything required to move it, and every movement state can be represented as a composable Charter capability or a reference to the canonical domain that owns it.**
