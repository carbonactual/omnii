# SPOTIST — Canonical Seek / Discovery Capability

**Status:** CANONICAL ECOSYSTEM CAPABILITY
**Domain:** Discovery, seeking, matching, locating, tracing and opportunity discovery
**Authority:** OMNII constitutional/meta-ontology and applicable ecosystem authority, privacy, safety, evidence and consent rules

## 1. Purpose

SPOTIST is the ecosystem-wide capability for **seeking**: discovering, locating, identifying, matching, tracing, qualifying, verifying, connecting, assembling or monitoring whatever is legitimately being sought.

SPOTIST is deliberately **not limited to products, services, procurement, suppliers, markets or commerce**.

A seek may concern a:

- person
- family or relationship
- identity or identity lead
- animal or pet
- object or property
- vehicle
- home, land or place
- rare, unique, obsolete or hard-to-find item
- book, artwork, collectible or historical object
- information or record
- document or evidence lead
- capability, skill or expertise
- human service
- supplier or producer
- production or logistical capacity
- opportunity, deal, promotion or unusual offer
- event, activity or experience
- adventure or composed experience
- resource
- relationship or connection
- missing/lost item or connection
- any other legitimately represented object of intent that the ecosystem can lawfully and safely seek.

The list is open-world and non-exhaustive.

## 2. Canonical abstraction

The universal primitive is **SEEK**.

```text
SEEK
  ↓
INTERPRET INTENT
  ↓
DEFINE / DECOMPOSE CRITERIA
  ↓
DISCOVER
  ↓
MATCH / TRACE / LOCATE
  ↓
QUALIFY
  ↓
VERIFY / EVIDENCE
  ↓
CONNECT / ALERT / ASSEMBLE
  ↓
HAND OFF TO THE APPROPRIATE CAPABILITY
  ↓
OPTIONALLY ACT UNDER AUTHORITY
```

SPOTIST is therefore a capability domain, not a fixed application category.

## 3. Natural-language first

The human should not need to know the internal category before seeking.

Examples include:

> "Find me a 1970s first edition of this book."

> "Find an actor with these characteristics for this role."

> "Find someone who can repair this machine."

> "Help me find my mother's family from these details."

> "Find this particular car under this condition and budget."

> "Find an exceptional offer that fits what I normally look for."

> "Find me an unusual weekend adventure within these constraints."

> "Find the person who can actually do this."

The input may be precise, vague, incomplete, unusual, compound or exploratory. SPOTIST adapts the seek model to the expressed intent rather than forcing the user into a predetermined category.

## 4. Tailored seeking

SPOTIST may tailor discovery using authorized context such as:

- the seeker's explicit criteria;
- preferences and constraints;
- budget or economic boundary;
- geography and travel range;
- timing and urgency;
- prior saved or rejected seeks;
- relevant capabilities and relationships;
- accessibility requirements;
- known permissions and authority;
- historical outcomes and evidence;
- recurring or standing requests.

Personalization must remain transparent, bounded by applicable permissions, and subject to privacy and authority constraints.

## 5. Discovery is not certainty

SPOTIST MUST preserve distinctions between:

- exact match;
- strong match;
- possible match;
- substitute or alternative;
- lead;
- unverified lead;
- evidence-backed finding;
- conflicting information;
- historical information;
- inferred relationship;
- unresolved seek.

A discovery result MUST NOT be represented as established fact merely because a search produced it.

Evidence, provenance, confidence, contradiction and verification status remain explicit.

## 6. Compound seeks and missions

A seek may contain multiple dependencies.

Example:

```text
"Create an unforgettable birthday experience"

→ venue
→ activity
→ participants
→ transport
→ food
→ timing
→ equipment
→ permissions
→ providers
```

SPOTIST may decompose the mission into related seeks, discover compatible components, assemble candidates and hand the resulting chain to ABBA or another authorized orchestrator.

SPOTIST does not thereby become the authority for consequential action.

## 7. Serendipity and opportunity discovery

SPOTIST may discover relevant things the user did not explicitly name when those discoveries are materially related to the expressed seek.

Examples:

- a better substitute;
- an unusual opportunity;
- a scarce item;
- an exceptional offer;
- a newly available capacity;
- a person who may solve the problem;
- a related opportunity revealed by the seeker's constraints.

Such discoveries MUST be distinguished from direct matches and SHOULD explain the relevance basis.

## 8. Two-way seeking

SPOTIST may operate in both directions:

```text
SEEKER → searches for something

and

WORLD / NETWORK → exposes something that may satisfy a seek
```

The capability therefore supports both:

- "I am looking for X";
- "I have X / can do X / know X / can provide X".

Matching may connect seekers, holders, providers, capabilities, opportunities and relationships.

## 9. Monitoring / radar

A seek may remain active after the initial search.

The ecosystem may monitor authorized sources for:

- newly available matches;
- price or condition changes;
- new leads;
- expiring offers;
- relevant events;
- newly available people or capabilities;
- changing availability;
- new evidence;
- newly created opportunities.

Standing seeks must retain their scope, authority, privacy conditions, expiry and notification rules.

## 10. Human services and people discovery

People may be sought by role, capability, availability, qualification, language, experience, geography, representation or other legitimate criteria.

SPOTIST may support discovery of roles such as:

- performer/actor;
- tutor;
- specialist;
- consultant;
- artisan;
- caregiver;
- guide;
- translator;
- expert;
- service provider;
- intermediary;
- other legitimate human capability.

Sensitive people-related seeking MUST be subject to identity, consent, privacy, safeguarding, legal and authority constraints. SPOTIST must not become unrestricted surveillance, stalking, doxxing or unauthorized disclosure infrastructure.

## 11. Lost, missing and recovery seeks

SPOTIST may support lawful discovery and recovery of:

- lost property;
- pets;
- documents;
- records;
- missing connections;
- genealogical/family relationships;
- historical or provenance leads;
- other missing objects or relationships.

These workflows MUST preserve evidence and uncertainty rather than presenting a speculative lead as a confirmed identification.

## 12. Opportunity seeks

SPOTIST may search for:

- exceptional offers;
- promotions;
- bargains;
- surplus or underused resources;
- contracts;
- grants;
- partnerships;
- available capacity;
- investment or economic opportunities where the relevant economic product and authority applies;
- time-sensitive openings;
- unusual combinations of resources or capabilities.

SPOTIST does not own the economic semantics of every opportunity. It discovers and matches; the relevant economic domain, such as OMNI/I/O, governs transaction and value movement.

## 13. Adventures, experiences and actions

A seek may target an experience rather than a pre-existing product.

SPOTIST may discover or compose:

- adventures;
- outings;
- educational experiences;
- cultural experiences;
- events;
- group activities;
- themed or constraint-driven experiences.

The capability may assemble participants, places, providers, resources and timing, then hand off execution to the appropriate event, economic, communication or orchestration capability.

## 14. Product boundary

SPOTIST is intentionally reusable.

It MAY be exposed as:

- a standalone product;
- a product module;
- a dashboard capability;
- an API/service;
- an agent capability;
- an internal ecosystem service;
- a workflow;
- an embedded seek surface;
- another form appropriate to context.

It MUST NOT require a dedicated product shell merely to exist.

## 15. Composition boundaries

```text
OMNII   → constitutional/runtime substrate
ABBA    → intelligence, reasoning and orchestration
SPOTIST → seeking, discovery, matching, tracing and finding
DESK    → participant workspace/dashboard that may expose SPOTIST
OMNI    → broader economic participation/trade/market/investment surfaces
I/O     → value circulation, ledger and settlement infrastructure
HAPI    → human identity/authority/continuity interfaces
```

A product may consume SPOTIST without becoming SPOTIST or duplicating its semantics.

## 16. Existing sourcing capability remains valid

Sourcing, procurement, supplier discovery, manufacturing discovery, demand aggregation, capacity discovery, quotation, qualification, negotiation and trade-related discovery are **valid SPOTIST applications**, but they do not define the whole capability.

Historical Desk/Spotist sourcing implementations MUST be interpreted as existing domain compositions of the broader SEEK capability unless a more specific canonical boundary states otherwise.

## 17. Relationship to ABBA

ABBA may:

- interpret a user's request;
- decompose a mission;
- invoke SPOTIST;
- compare discovered results;
- coordinate follow-on capabilities;
- monitor an active seek;
- ask for human clarification or approval.

SPOTIST supplies discovery capability. ABBA remains the ecosystem's orchestration intelligence and does not become a second SPOTIST-specific authority layer.

## 18. Authority, safety and privacy

SPOTIST discovery does not itself grant authority.

Any action involving:

- sensitive personal information;
- identity disclosure;
- financial or economic commitment;
- regulated services;
- physical intervention;
- law-enforcement-sensitive matters;
- access to private records;
- binding agreements;
- movement of value;
- consequential communications;

must use the relevant authority, consent, policy, safeguarding, evidence and governance mechanisms.

## 19. No forced taxonomy

SPOTIST MUST remain open-world.

A new type of seek does not require rewriting the constitutional core merely because the ecosystem has not previously encountered it.

The seek may initially be represented as an unknown/provisional object and progressively classified through evidence and governed extension under the Universal Meta-Ontology.

## 20. Canonical invariant

> **SPOTIST is the ecosystem's universal capability for finding what is legitimately being sought — whatever that may be — and for turning a natural-language seek into discoverable, evidence-aware, qualified and appropriately hand-offable results.**
