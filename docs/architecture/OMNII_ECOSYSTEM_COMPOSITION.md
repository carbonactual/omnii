# OMNII Ecosystem Composition Constitution

## Status

Canonical architectural rule for ecosystem composition and execution.

## Principle

OMNII is a living ecosystem, not a fixed catalog of products. Products, services, institutions, workflows and experiences are composable expressions of shared constitutional capabilities.

A new idea should first attempt composition from existing capabilities and products. A new constitutional primitive is introduced only when the recurring capability cannot be represented by the existing grammar.

## Shelf Model

The ecosystem exposes reusable ingredients:

- constitutional primitives;
- capabilities;
- resources;
- services;
- products;
- workflows;
- registries;
- rules;
- evidence sources;
- human and machine actors.

Examples include Charter, Pilgrim, NAB, NGIN and STOS. These are reusable ingredients, not permanent boundaries around what OMNII can express.

## Composition

A request is resolved as:

`intent -> ingredients -> composition -> conformance -> authorization -> workflow -> execution -> evidence -> reconciliation -> Pulse -> continuity`

The composition must preserve provenance for every selected ingredient and every generated relationship.

## Product Non-Exclusivity

A product may be selected whole, selected for one capability, combined with other products, partially reused through a capability, replaced during execution, or extended by a newly introduced capability.

Products must not create parallel foundational primitives when the ecosystem already has a canonical one.

## Conformance

Every action must be expressible in the constitutional grammar and pass applicable authority, capability, resource, dependency, rule and evidence checks before execution.

Conformance does not require uniform behavior. It requires that behavior remains explainable, traceable and governable inside the ecosystem.

## No-Loss Execution

Every value-bearing or state-changing action must have:

- a unique action identity;
- an idempotency key;
- before-state and after-state references where applicable;
- actor and authority context;
- provenance;
- explicit outcome;
- recoverable exception state when execution does not complete;
- reconciliation status.

A failed step becomes an exception or recovery state, not an orphaned mutation.

## Automation

Automation may execute only within declared authority and capability bounds. Workflows may be automatic, semi-automatic or human-gated.

Human intervention is a valid workflow state, not a failure of automation.

## Open World

The composition model is open-world. New domains, entities, environments, technologies and unknown classifications can be represented without redesigning the ecosystem grammar.

Uncertainty must remain explicit. Unknown is a valid state; unsupported certainty is not.

## Economic Boundary

Checkout is the economic interpretation point, not the beginning of the ecosystem. Value is observed from outcomes and Pulse; money is one possible representation of exchange.

Economic records must remain reconcilable with the underlying action, resource, contribution and outcome.

## Continuity

The ecosystem treats interruption as a state, not an implicit deletion. A composition can be paused, resumed, retried, compensated, rerouted, reassigned, reversed where lawful and reversible, or escalated.

## Architectural Lock

**Every product is an ingredient. Every action is a governed workflow. Every workflow leaves evidence. Every outcome feeds continuity and Pulse.**
