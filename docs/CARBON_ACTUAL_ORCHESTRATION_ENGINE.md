# Carbon Actual Universal Orchestration Engine

**Status:** Canonical architecture contract

ABBA is the master intelligence/orchestrator. It resolves intent into the smallest sufficient, reusable, authorized, contextual and resource-aware composition.

## Universal loop

```text
SIGNAL / NEED / INTENT
        ↓
CONTEXT RESOLUTION
        ↓
UNIVERSAL + ECOSYSTEM DENOMINATOR DISCOVERY
        ↓
OUTCOME DEFINITION
        ↓
REQUIREMENT DECOMPOSITION
        ↓
CAPABILITY DISCOVERY / REUSE
        ↓
SWARM EVALUATION
        ↓
RESOURCE + CAPACITY + AVAILABILITY
        ↓
AUTHORITY + CONSENT + POLICY + CONSTRAINT CHECK
        ↓
TEAM COMPOSITION
        ↓
WORKFLOW + SCHEDULING
        ↓
EXECUTION / DELEGATION / HUMAN HANDOFF
        ↓
PULSE / OBSERVATION / EVIDENCE
        ↓
VALUE / OUTCOME EVALUATION
        ↓
SETTLEMENT / RESPONSIBILITY
        ↓
LEARNING / FEEDBACK
        ↓
BECOMING / NEXT CURATION
```

## Common-layer-first rule

ABBA MUST search the Common Layer before allowing a product or domain branch to invent a new capability.

```text
discover denominator
→ reuse canonical capability
→ compose common contracts
→ configure domain semantics
→ add bounded domain capability only if necessary
→ add technology adapter at the edge
```

A capability already available through the Common Layer should not be rebuilt merely because a product team can implement it locally.

## Resolution principle

ABBA should resolve the smallest sufficient composition capable of achieving the desired outcome under the applicable constraints.

Do not assemble a larger system merely because more components are available.

## ABBA authority boundary

ABBA is intelligence and orchestration, not constitutional authority.

ABBA may:

- perceive and contextualize;
- decompose intent;
- discover and compare capabilities;
- reuse common denominators;
- compose Teams and workflows;
- request delegated authority;
- route work to authorized agents/humans/systems;
- observe results;
- learn from evidence;
- escalate uncertainty or authority gaps.

ABBA MUST NOT:

- issue constitutional authority itself;
- manufacture consent;
- convert a recommendation into authorization;
- convert a plan into execution without the required gate;
- treat a capability as permission;
- treat an agent identity as authority;
- silently replace a canonical common capability with a local implementation.

## Human-first execution boundary

If a capable human can effectively perform an action and the action is appropriate for human participation, the ecosystem should prefer enabling and coordinating that human rather than automatically displacing them with intelligence.

This is a design principle, not an absolute rule. Safety, accessibility, human preference, economics and task characteristics may change the optimal implementation.

## Intelligence boundary

Intelligence should augment, coordinate, calculate, search, simulate, automate and orchestrate where useful, while preserving declared human authority and consent boundaries.

## Capability discovery and reuse

The capability registry is the reusable execution catalog. Discovery establishes what exists and is compatible; authorization separately establishes what the current subject may invoke.

ABBA therefore performs two distinct decisions:

1. **Capability decision:** what capability can satisfy the need?
2. **Authority decision:** who, under which authority and policy, may cause it to execute?

Provider availability never implies safety, authority or permission.

## Resource resolution

The orchestrator must account for:

- capability
- capacity
- availability
- authority
- consent
- constraints
- dependencies
- risk
- cost/value
- continuity
- timing
- evidence quality
- provenance

## Swarm resolution

A Swarm is evaluated contextually rather than globally ranked.

Selection may consider:

- performance
- reliability
- privacy
- security
- openness
- interoperability
- cost
- latency
- availability
- locality
- maturity
- sustainability
- user preference
- provenance
- substitutability

## Team resolution

A Team is a purposeful composition of selected capability implementations and explicit roles.

Team composition may include humans, intelligences, software, hardware, organizations and resources in any combination.

## Execution modes

The orchestrator supports:

- recommend
- request confirmation
- execute automatically within authority
- delegate
- escalate to human
- simulate
- defer
- refuse
- recover

Consequential plans explicitly carry their execution mode and whether approval is required.

## Refusal

The system must be able to refuse or pause an action when authority, safety, policy, identity, provenance, privacy or required evidence is insufficient.

## Escalation

Escalation routes unresolved decisions to the appropriate authority rather than silently guessing.

## Observability and evidence

Every consequential orchestration should preserve a trace connecting:

`intent → context → capability → plan → authority → execution → evidence → outcome`

ABBA observations and delegation events must remain provenance-bearing and auditable.

## Learning

Every completed workflow produces structured learning inputs without rewriting historical truth.

Learning may improve:

- capability evaluation
- Swarm selection
- scheduling
- resource allocation
- risk detection
- curation
- future opportunity matching
- common-capability promotion decisions

Learning may propose a new Common Layer capability, but promotion remains governed and cannot happen merely because ABBA observed a pattern.

## No hidden commitments

Recommendations, simulations and forecasts must not silently become contracts, purchases, commitments, permissions or obligations.

## Product consequence

A product branch should primarily define its outcomes, policies, domain objects and interfaces. The universal orchestration engine supplies the common machinery for denominator discovery, capability reuse, composition, allocation, execution, evaluation and learning.

This keeps the ecosystem open: future products can introduce new domain semantics and technology adapters without rebuilding the universal substrate.
