**Status:** FROZEN

**Scope:** ecosystem architecture, universal capability ontology, economic ontology and product composition

## 1. What Is Being Frozen

This freeze establishes the architectural constitution for building Carbon Actual products and integrations.

It freezes:

- Carbon Actual as the ecosystem name.
- Omni as an operating property/state describing omnidirectional, continuously changing movement and configuration through Value, Pulse, IO, relationships, capabilities and activity.
- Being as the ecosystem ground.
- ABBA as master intelligence/orchestration intelligence.
- Value and Pulse as central operating/economic primitives.
- IO as movement/access/exchange/flow.
- Union and Communication as relationship/connection primitives.
- Continuum and Curation/Becoming as lifecycle and progression primitives.
- Integration as a core value.
- HASH, SEAL, Terminal, Root, Index, Vault, Actual and Atlas as identity/authority/routing/knowledge roles.
- HAPI as Human API and HAPI World as intelligence/agent environment.
- Ash and Phoenix as residual/security-response concepts.
- The ten compressed architectural families.
- The universal object model.
- The reusable capability and product-composition model.
- The logical-ledger model and ledger/blockchain agnosticism.
- Support for tokenization, fractionalization, decimalization, democratization and decentralization.
- Human + intelligence balance as a design requirement.
- Digital + physical + human + machine execution as first-class.
- No-waste/recovery as a system principle.

## 3. Constitutional Source Frameworks

Carbon Actual may draw useful principles, patterns and curation methods from historical, mathematical, philosophical, governance and organizational frameworks. These are **sources of design inspiration and analytical tools, not imported religions, authorities or literal constitutional dependencies**.

Relevant source families include:

- Hermetic principles — especially balance, correspondence, polarity, rhythm, cause/effect and the discipline of avoiding simplistic binary interpretation.
- Kabbalistic/tree-based structural ideas — adopted as a source for ordered progression, relationship and staged development; Carbon Actual's identity construct remains HASH rather than a literal Kabbalistic structure.
- 22-step/tree/path concepts — treated as a curation/continuum inspiration where useful, not as a mandatory product layer.
- 33-step/Masonic organizational progression concepts — treated as a source for staged development, discipline and organizational maturity, not as a literal membership or governance structure.
- Law of Continuum / variation-continuum concepts — used to model continuous state change, transition and variation rather than rigid binary states.
- Mathematics — the formal mechanism for measurement, balancing, optimization, probability, modelling and validation.

The source frameworks must be compressed into operational principles. They must never be allowed to create architectural congestion.

## 5. Stateless/Formless Clarification

Carbon Actual is **not stateless**.

It is **dynamic and formless at the ecosystem-state level**: the ecosystem's current configuration is continuously recalculated from events, Pulse, Value, obligations, relationships, capabilities, time, context and external conditions.

The underlying identity, provenance, authority, records and state required for continuity remain persistent.

Therefore:

> **Persistent identity + continuous state transition = dynamic ecosystem.**

## 6. Freeze vs Evolution

### Frozen

The architecture, vocabulary, boundaries, principles and composition rules in this document and its companion specifications.

### Not Frozen

The following remain empirical/implementation domains:

- exact economic formulas
- token monetary policy
- token supply
- financial regulatory implementation
- jurisdiction-specific accounting
- final blockchain choices
- final AI model/provider choices
- final storage/database choices
- final cryptographic algorithms where standards evolve
- product UI
- individual product workflows
- pricing levels
- market assumptions
- agent autonomy limits beyond constitutional authority/safety rules

Freezing the architecture does not freeze technology selection or prevent improvement.

## 7. Change Control

A proposed change must first be classified:

1. **Implementation change** — does not affect the architecture.
2. **Capability extension** — adds a reusable capability under an existing family.
3. **Domain branch** — creates a product/domain composition without changing the constitution.
4. **Constitutional amendment** — changes a frozen concept, boundary, dependency or top-level family.

Only category 4 requires an architecture freeze amendment.

## 9. Integration Rule

Integration is a core value.

Carbon Actual SHOULD prefer an existing compatible capability over rebuilding it. External components should be connected through explicit adapters/contracts and should not receive unnecessary user or ecosystem data.

Open source, open standards, free/open alternatives, white-label systems, SDKs, AI agents, MCP/A2A-compatible systems, decentralized networks, ledgers and blockchains are eligible integration targets subject to security, license, quality, privacy and sustainability review.

## 10. Security Freeze

The system is designed for resilience, not an impossible guarantee of being literally unbreakable.

The security posture includes:

- explicit authority
- least privilege
- compartmentalization
- provenance
- encryption
- selective disclosure
- revocation
- anomaly detection
- isolation
- recovery
- tamper evidence
- post-quantum migration readiness

Ash is residual/security residue. Phoenix is the active response mechanism.

## 11. Economic Freeze

The economic architecture is based on:

```text
RESOURCE → ACTIVITY → PULSE → EVALUATION → VALUE → EXCHANGE/PROVISION → NEXT STATE
```

The ecosystem supports inverted economics as a formal research/implementation doctrine, with Value as the balancing measurement medium and with ecosystem responsibility for legitimate liabilities.

Exact formulas are deliberately not frozen until validated through mathematical modelling, simulation, legal analysis and empirical evidence.

## 12. Human/Intelligence Freeze

HASH is the identity construct formed through a balanced human + associated intelligence relationship.

HAPI represents the human capability interface.

HAPI World represents the intelligence/agent environment.

The ecosystem's mission is to secure humanity's future by making intelligence and humans mutually enabling rather than structurally replacing or overpowering one another.

Meaningful human physical, social and real-world activity remains a first-class outcome.

## 13. No-Waste Freeze

No useful value should be discarded merely because it has left its original workflow.

Residuals enter Ash, are classified, and are considered for recovery, reuse, repair, repurposing or recycling.

The principle applies to:

- material
- money/value
- time
- energy
- data
- compute
- infrastructure
- human capability
- machine capacity
- knowledge
- opportunity
