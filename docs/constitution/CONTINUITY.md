# CONTINUITY

Continuity is the constitutional kernel governing persistence of Being, Identity, Knowledge, Relationships, Value, Trust, and institutional state through time.

## Purpose
Preserve what must persist while allowing legitimate change.

## Canonical Concerns
- State persistence
- History and provenance
- Lifecycle
- Succession
- Recovery
- Legacy
- Handoff

## Invariant
No transition should destroy required continuity without explicit authority and recorded consequence.
