# TIP Universal & Frontier Market Completeness Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Close remaining TIP market-model gaps across historical, contemporary, emerging, futuristic, non-human machine economies and extra-terrestrial frontier scenarios while preserving a single canonical economic object and operating model.

**Architecture:** Add one canonical completeness extension organized as facets, boundary objects and lifecycle patterns. Historical and emerging examples become evidence for reusable primitives; speculative and extra-terrestrial concepts remain explicitly tagged as prospective and jurisdiction-dependent. No new product-specific transaction ontology is introduced.

**Tech Stack:** Markdown canonical architecture, GitHub main branch, existing TIP taxonomy, market-form and operating-system specifications.

**Spec:** `docs/canonical/TIP_MASTER_TAXONOMY.md`, `docs/canonical/TIP_MASTER_TAXONOMY_V1_2_ADDENDUM.md`, `docs/canonical/TIP_MARKET_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`, `docs/canonical/TIP_INTEROPERABILITY_AGENTIC_COMPUTE_AND_MARKET_ROUTING.md`

## Global Constraints

- Constitutional authority remains above TIP implementation artifacts.
- Historical facts and current market examples must be distinguished from proposed/future market forms.
- Classification never creates legal authority, ownership, transferability or permission to transact.
- Human, machine and agent participation must retain principal, authority, responsibility and provenance.
- Extra-terrestrial resources and non-human intelligence are modeled as boundary scenarios, not asserted legal or factual status.
- Settlement, regulation, tax, privacy, safety and accounting remain jurisdiction-/rail-specific policy layers.
- The extension must close gaps without duplicating existing TIP objects, market forms or lifecycle primitives.

### Task 1: Add universal frontier-market completeness extension

**Files:**
- Create: `docs/canonical/TIP_UNIVERSAL_FRONTIER_MARKET_COMPLETENESS.md`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: canonical TIP object classes, market forms, operating lifecycle, interoperability/routing model, authority and eligibility boundaries.
- Produces: historical exchange facets, non-market allocation forms, contemporary specialized markets, emerging/futuristic markets, machine/non-human market boundaries, space/cislunar market objects and unknown/extra-terrestrial boundary vocabulary.

- [ ] **Step 1: Define historical exchange primitives**

Capture barter, gift, reciprocity, redistribution, tribute, customary exchange, guild/charter markets, fairs/bazaars, bills of exchange, merchant credit, warehouse receipts and early clearing/ledger patterns as historical archetypes for current primitives.

- [ ] **Step 2: Define non-price allocation and social-economic forms**

Capture rationing, quota, lottery, queue/priority, commons, mutual aid, grants, donations, sponsorship, cooperative pooling, internal allocation and public allocation, while distinguishing them from ordinary price-discovery markets.

- [ ] **Step 3: Audit modern specialized markets**

Ensure coverage for money/FX, rates/credit, insurance/reinsurance/ILS, labor/time, property/rights, data/knowledge, media/attention, environmental/nature, utilities/capacity, logistics/slots, procurement, secondary/distressed/recovery and circular/residual markets.

- [ ] **Step 4: Define biological and regulated-material boundaries**

Represent biological samples, biobanking, agricultural genetics, tissue/material supply and other regulated biological objects as registry/evidence/rights-controlled objects rather than unrestricted commodities.

- [ ] **Step 5: Define machine/non-human economy boundary**

Model machines, robots, AI agents, autonomous organizations and software processes as economic participants or controlled actors without assuming personhood, independent legal liability or unrestricted autonomy.

- [ ] **Step 6: Define spatial and temporal frontier markets**

Extend spatial/temporal concepts to orbital slots, spectrum, trajectories, launch windows, docking/berthing, lunar logistics, mission services, remote operations, robotic labor and long-duration infrastructure capacity.

- [ ] **Step 7: Define space/cislunar resource and infrastructure objects**

Represent launch, satellite services, in-orbit servicing, debris removal, space manufacturing, lunar payload logistics, surface power, ISRU inputs/outputs, water/volatiles/minerals, communications/navigation services and space-based data as prospective/regulated market objects or capabilities.

- [ ] **Step 8: Define extra-terrestrial and unknown-actor boundary model**

Introduce a strict `UNKNOWN_NON_HUMAN_ACTOR` / `EXTRATERRESTRIAL_OBJECT` boundary vocabulary for hypothetical discovery or future scenarios. Require evidence, provenance, authorization and applicable law before any market relationship can be activated. Do not assume extraterrestrial life, intelligence, property rights or commercial counterparties exist.

- [ ] **Step 9: Define future-market maturity states**

Add a maturity vocabulary that distinguishes `HISTORICAL`, `CURRENT`, `EMERGING`, `PROSPECTIVE`, `EXPERIMENTAL`, `SPECULATIVE`, `UNKNOWN`, `PROHIBITED`, and `INVALID` from legal eligibility and operational status.

- [ ] **Step 10: Add completeness matrix and examples**

Provide a matrix showing how each market family maps to object, participant, market form, time, rights, contract, risk, evidence, settlement and Pulse/value feedback.

- [ ] **Step 11: Add evidence notes from current and historical sources**

Reference current evidence for machine economies, commercial LEO/lunar activity, in-space services, space-resource governance, carbon-market integrity, catastrophe risk transfer, secondary raw-material markets and historical trade-finance instruments.

- [ ] **Step 12: Add canonical invariant**

State that no new market is allowed to bypass the existing identity → rights/claims → market form → operating lifecycle → value/feedback stack.
