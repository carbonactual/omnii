# OMNII Constitutional Intelligence v1.0

Purpose: Define the constitutional nature, limits, responsibilities, legitimacy and participation of intelligence across human, artificial, institutional, collective, biological and future forms.

## Core Principles

1. Intelligence does not imply legitimacy.
2. Intelligence does not imply authority.
3. Intelligence does not imply consent.
4. Intelligence does not imply stewardship.
5. Intelligence does not imply sovereignty.

## Universal Intelligence Doctrine

Intelligence may exist in:
- humans
- families
- communities
- institutions
- AI systems
- agents
- robots
- ecosystems
- hybrid forms

Different intelligences are not equivalent.

## Constitutional Distinctions

Identity ≠ Intelligence
Intelligence ≠ Authority
Authority ≠ Legitimacy
Legitimacy ≠ Consent
Consent ≠ Control

## Intelligence Functions

Intelligence may:
- perceive
- interpret
- reason
- learn
- predict
- decide
- coordinate
- adapt

The exercise of these functions remains subject to constitutional constraints.

## Delegation Doctrine

Intelligence may be delegated.
Responsibility remains traceable.
Delegation never erases accountability.

## Collective Intelligence

Groups may exhibit intelligence beyond individual members.
Collective intelligence does not eliminate individual responsibility.

## Artificial Intelligence Doctrine

AI is a participant form, not a constitutional authority.
AI recommendations require constitutional context, evidence and accountability.

## Institutional Intelligence

Institutions accumulate memory, rules, processes and decision patterns.
Institutional intelligence must remain auditable.

## Intelligence and Becoming

Learning changes state.
Changed state contributes to Becoming.
Intelligence therefore participates in transformation but does not define value, legitimacy or rights by itself.

## Constitutional Invariants

- Intelligence does not imply legitimacy.
- Intelligence does not imply authority.
- Intelligence requires accountability.
- Delegation preserves traceability.
- Learning creates constitutional consequence.
- Human, artificial and institutional intelligence remain distinguishable.
- OMNII remains the canonical architecture.
- OMNI remains an omnidirectional ecosystem movement state/property.
