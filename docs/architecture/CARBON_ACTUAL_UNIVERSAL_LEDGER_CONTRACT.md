# OMNII Universal Ledger Contract

**Status: CANONICAL CONTRACT**

The ledger layer records governed value movements without collapsing distinct economic concepts into one.

## Distinct concepts

Keep separate:

- value
- money
- asset
- resource
- ownership
- contribution
- price
- cost
- obligation
- right
- transaction
- ledger entry
- settlement

## Ledger entry

A canonical entry identifies the economic subject, accounts/parties, amount and unit, direction, reason, authority, provenance, timestamp, correlation and lifecycle/status.

## Rules

- Ledger entries are attributable and auditable.
- No product invents a private universal value model.
- Economic policy is governed by the constitutional value model.
- Providers, currencies, payment rails and storage implementations are adapters beneath the contract.
- Corrections use explicit reversals/adjustments rather than silent mutation.

The ledger therefore becomes reusable infrastructure for BUNK, HAPI, banking, capital, marketplaces, services and future compositions.
