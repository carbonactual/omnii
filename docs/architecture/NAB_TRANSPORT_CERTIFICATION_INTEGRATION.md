# NAB — Transport Certification & Tokenization Integration

**Status:** Canonical runtime integration
**Role:** NAB remains the authoritative automobile biography/registry composition; the transport proof layer provides reusable verification, evidence, credential, passport and lawful tokenization structures.

## Canonical boundary

NAB owns the lifecycle biography and registry context of eligible automobile/mobility assets. It does not transfer that authority to CHARTER or the generic certification registry.

The certification layer records how a NAB claim is evidenced and verified. Tokenization remains a separate lifecycle and does not change NAB's legal authority.

Each stage may contribute evidence without rewriting the underlying biography.

## Verification envelope

A NAB-linked certification record may reference:

- authoritative registry checks;
- issuer-signed credentials;
- verifiable credentials;
- decentralized identifiers;
- inspection reports;
- maintenance records;
- conversion records;
- telemetry and sensor attestations;
- software and cybersecurity evidence;
- digital product/battery passport data;
- evidence hashes and provenance chains;
- human review and authority attestations.

## Tokenization envelope

A NAB asset may become **token-ready** only when the asset/right, authority, provenance, risk, transfer conditions, custody, settlement and jurisdictional treatment are defined.

`token-ready` is not the same as `issued` and does not itself create legal ownership, securities rights, custody or settlement authority.

Where tokenization is regulated, the registry requires the appropriate issuer, custodian, settlement route and legal-effect statement.

## Future vehicle state

For software-defined, connected or autonomous assets, NAB may reference:

- autonomy level;
- operational design domain;
- human/remote oversight;
- cybersecurity state;
- software version/update state;
- safety-case state;
- telemetry integrity;
- component/passport identity.
