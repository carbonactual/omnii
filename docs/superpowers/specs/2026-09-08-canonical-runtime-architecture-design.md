## Technical boundaries

The implementation has nine coordinated planes: constitutional control, universal objects, evidence/events/state, capabilities/adapters, intelligence/orchestration, execution, economics/flow, projections, and continuity/security.

Identity, authority, capability, evidence, state and execution remain separate concerns. Products compose shared capabilities and may not create competing universal primitives.

## Persistence

Use relational canonical state and transactional records, append-oriented event/evidence history, object/blob storage, search/index projections, optional graph projections, caches, and logical ledgers. Provider and infrastructure choices remain replaceable.

## Identity and authority

HASH is the identity relationship. SEAL is the human authority/approval mechanism. Credentials prove claims. Delegation transfers bounded authority. Policies constrain operations. Audit records what occurred. An agent identity never implies unbounded authority.

## Capability and adapter fabric

Capabilities expose stable contracts for identity, version, purpose, inputs, outputs, entities, authority, permissions, data classes, integrations, events, Pulse/Value metrics, security/privacy, health, dependencies, provider and replacement strategy. Providers remain adapters behind capability interfaces.

## Orchestration

ABBA resolves context and intent, discovers capabilities, evaluates Swarms, composes Teams, plans workflows, applies policy and authority gates, routes execution, interprets outcomes and feeds learning/curation. Team composition is contextual and may change with evidence.

## Economic runtime

IO, Pulse and Value remain distinct. Pulse is evidence/feedback and does not automatically become currency, authority or permission. Logical ledgers remain independent of any particular blockchain. Exact economic formulas remain empirical and jurisdiction-aware.

## Knowledge projections

Root, Index, Vault, Actual and Atlas are distinct access/projection roles over canonical records, not competing sources of truth.

## Continuity and security

Ash classifies residual, unknown, failed or security-relevant output that must not be silently discarded. Phoenix is the governed response mechanism for harmful, fraudulent, unauthorized or materially non-conforming activity.

## Non-regression

A machine-readable authority registry must preserve canonical identifiers, statuses, lineage, aliases and amendments. CI must reject lower-authority definitions that contradict frozen/canonical concepts. Later proposals must be classified as clarification, implementation change, capability extension, domain branch or constitutional amendment before affecting canonical structure.

## Product integration

NAIRE, NGIN, RITES, Charter, Bunk, Logistico, Sportist, TIP, Build Labs, Spare, HAPI, HAPI World, HAPI Bank, Marketplace, Capital Block and future branches remain compositions over the same fabric.

## Success criteria

- One canonical constitutional spine.
- One universal object model.
- One relationship/authority model.
- One common capability fabric.
- One governed orchestration path.
- Distinct Pulse/Value/IO semantics.
- Ledger and provider agnosticism.
- Rebuildable evidence/state where practical.
- Explicit product boundaries.
- Automated architectural non-regression.
