# SPOTIST — Standalone Product Boundary

**Status:** CANONICAL PRODUCT BOUNDARY
**Relationship:** implementation of the universal SPOTIST SEEK capability

## Product identity

**SPOTIST** is the standalone consumer-facing product expression of the canonical SPOTIST capability.

Its primary experience is:

> **What are you looking for?**

The product may accept natural language, voice, images, documents and links and translate them into governed SEEK objects.

## What the product sells

The product sells access to better discovery and resolution, not ownership of the things being discovered.

Potential commercial layers include:

- free/basic seeking;
- Pro/advanced seeking;
- persistent/standing seeks;
- concierge/complex mission assistance;
- Business/organizational seeking;
- API/service access.

Commercial packaging is product policy and implementation, not constitutional law.

## Product experience pillars

1. **State the seek** — user describes what they want.
2. **Tailor the seek** — authorized context improves relevance.
3. **Work the seek** — discover, trace, match, qualify and verify.
4. **Keep looking** — standing seeks continue monitoring.
5. **Show why** — provenance, evidence and match explanation.
6. **Connect** — provide appropriate handoffs.
7. **Remember the outcome** — history improves future discovery while remaining user-controllable.

## Product does not imply product category

The standalone product may support many use cases without becoming each of these categories:

- sourcing;
- marketplace;
- classifieds;
- jobs;
- real estate;
- genealogy;
- lost and found;
- services;
- events;
- deal discovery.

Those are projections/use cases of SEEK.

## Trust invariant

SPOTIST must never fabricate discovery results to create the appearance of network depth or commercial activity.

Empty, partial and unresolved states are valid product states.

## Commercial trust invariant

The product may monetize access, assistance, API usage or legitimate commercial relationships, but payment must not silently alter the meaning of evidence or masquerade as objective relevance.

## Ecosystem integrations

The product may use:

- ABBA for intelligence/orchestration;
- OMNII contracts for canonical semantics;
- HAPI for relevant identity/authority;
- OMNI for applicable economic participation;
- I/O for applicable value/settlement workflows;
- DESK as a future participant workspace surface.

No integration creates a competing primitive.

## Final invariant

> **SPOTIST is the product people can ask to find what they are looking for; the universal SEEK capability is the underlying ecosystem contract that makes the product extensible beyond any predefined category.**
