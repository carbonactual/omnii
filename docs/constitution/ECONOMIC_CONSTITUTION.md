# ECONOMIC CONSTITUTION

The Economic Constitution governs the creation, recognition, transfer, allocation, measurement, and preservation of value throughout OMNII.

## Purpose
Provide a common economic substrate across people, organizations, agents, products, resources, and institutions.

## Canonical Concerns
- Value creation
- Contribution
- Ownership and rights
- Exchange
- Pricing
- Compensation
- Allocation
- Treasury and reserves
- Tokenization
- Economic accountability

## Invariant
Economic mechanisms must represent and preserve legitimate value relationships rather than manufacture authority through accounting alone.
