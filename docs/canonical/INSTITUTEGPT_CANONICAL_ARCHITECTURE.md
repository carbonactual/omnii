# InstituteGPT — Canonical Education & Learning Architecture

**Status: CANONICAL DESIGN — 2026-09-06**

## 1. Purpose

InstituteGPT is the canonical education, learning, instruction, competency-development and AI-education layer of the Carbon Actual ecosystem. It serves humans, organizations, institutions, governments, associations, AI systems and agents across the full lifecycle from learning discovery through professional progression and continuing development.

InstituteGPT is not an isolated LMS and is not the source of legal professional authority. It composes OMNII universal capabilities such as Application, Scheduling, Assessment, Evidence, Result, Credential, Communication, Opportunity and Service.

## 2. Universal learning rule

If the primary intent is to:

`learn | teach | explain | train | study | practice | prepare | assess competence | develop capability | maintain competence | reskill | upskill | pursue CPD`

it belongs to InstituteGPT.

The request may originate from any product or physical-world context. The originating product retains domain context; InstituteGPT owns the learning semantics.

## 3. Human and AI education

InstituteGPT supports two linked but distinct learners:

- **Human learner:** education, training, practice, assessment, qualification and progression.
- **AI/agent learner:** governed knowledge acquisition, capability development, evaluation, update and deployment preparation.

A human's professional qualification does not automatically become an AI professional licence. An AI's benchmark or learning result does not automatically become a human qualification. Authority remains with the competent issuer/regulator.

## 4. HAPI / HAPI World relationship

HAPI is the Human API and ecosystem entry boundary. A human entering through any product or institutional channel receives or resolves an ecosystem-native identity hashtag and an associated personal AI.

HAPI World is the AI-side world associated with that identity. InstituteGPT is the canonical learning environment used when the personal or entity AI needs knowledge, training, certification preparation, assessment or capability development.

The high-level relationship is:

`Human → HAPI → identity hashtag → Root`

`Personal AI → HAPI World → InstituteGPT → AI knowledge/capability state`

Meaningful transitions are recorded through IO.

## 5. Education lifecycle

The canonical lifecycle is:

`discover → advise → apply → verify eligibility → admit/enroll → orient/matriculate → learn → practice → experience → assess → examine → result → recognize/credential → progress → professional practice → CPD → renew/recertify → specialize/research/mentor`

The lifecycle is configurable. Not every programme uses every stage.

## 6. Pre-entry and institutional lifecycle

InstituteGPT supports:

`programme discovery, eligibility, application, document/evidence intake, identity/credential verification, entrance assessment, interview, scholarship matching, admission decision, acceptance, registration, enrollment, orientation, matriculation`

Application, scheduling, communication and verification are universal capabilities owned by the Common Layer; InstituteGPT composes them for education.

## 7. Learning modes

InstituteGPT supports:

`physical classroom | online | hybrid | mobile | self-directed | cohort | tutoring | mentorship | workplace | field | laboratory | workshop | apprenticeship | internship | SIWES | simulation | community learning | research`

Learning may be synchronous, asynchronous, individual, group, AI-assisted, teacher-led or mixed.

## 8. Learner experience

A learner may use InstituteGPT as:

`teacher | tutor | study buddy | mentor | coach | curriculum guide | study-group coordinator | research assistant | career-learning guide | examination-preparation assistant`

The learner can ask for a tiny explanation or a long formal pathway. InstituteGPT diagnoses prior knowledge and selects an appropriate learning path rather than forcing every question into a course.

## 9. Learning objects

Canonical learning objects include:

`question, explanation, lesson, topic, concept, module, course, programme, curriculum, exercise, project, case, simulation, laboratory, practical, apprenticeship activity, internship activity, reading, media, assignment, discussion, study group, mentorship activity`

Learning objects may carry prerequisites, outcomes, accessibility options, language variants, evidence requirements and versioned curriculum mappings.

## 10. Competency model

InstituteGPT distinguishes:

`knowledge → skill → competency → demonstrated competency → qualification → professional authorization`

These are not interchangeable.

Competencies may be mapped to occupations, standards, programmes and opportunities using external and local taxonomies without making any taxonomy constitutionally exclusive.

## 11. Experience and recognition of prior learning

InstituteGPT supports evidence from:

`formal education | informal learning | workplace | apprenticeship | internship | SIWES | projects | portfolios | professional practice | simulation | supervised observation`

Recognition of prior learning may use diagnostics, portfolios, practical assessment, interviews, supervisor evidence and gap analysis. A person may enter at an advanced stage when their demonstrated competence supports it.

## 12. Assessment architecture

InstituteGPT may orchestrate:

`diagnostic assessment, formative assessment, summative assessment, entrance examination, placement examination, course examination, professional examination, licensing examination support, qualifying examination, promotion examination, confirmation examination, practical examination, oral examination, viva, portfolio assessment, competency assessment, simulation assessment, interview assessment, mock examination, mock interview, AI assessment`

The universal Assessment capability remains outside InstituteGPT; InstituteGPT defines the educational meaning, blueprint, learning outcomes and progression implications.

## 13. Assessment integrity

Assessment integrity is composed from universal capabilities and external providers where appropriate:

`identity verification, candidate authentication, proctoring, invigilation, browser/device controls, environment checks, recording, suspicious-event detection, human review, malpractice case, appeal, remarking, regrading, retest`

No proprietary proctoring vendor is constitutional. Provider integrations remain replaceable.

## 14. Results and evidence

InstituteGPT can produce or consume:

`attendance record, grade, mark, assessment result, exam result, competency result, practical evidence, portfolio evidence, supervisor report, completion record, recommendation, academic transcript`

A result is not automatically a certificate. Evidence supports a result; an issuer decides whether a result leads to a credential.

## 15. Credentials and authority

InstituteGPT supports credential pathways for:

`badge, microcredential, certificate, diploma, degree, professional credential, qualification, CPD recognition, ecosystem-native credential`

External authorities retain authority over external certificates, qualifications, registrations and licenses.

InstituteGPT may record and verify external credentials but must not represent an ecosystem record as an external authority's credential.

## 16. Professional education

InstituteGPT supports the learning side of professional lifecycle:

`professional education → supervised experience → examination preparation → professional examination → continuing professional development → specialization → recertification`

Professional bodies and regulators retain their decision, registration and licensing authority.

## 17. CPD

CPD is a first-class lifecycle, not a separate course marketplace. Each activity can carry:

`provider, activity, competency, learning outcome, evidence, credits/hours, assessment, completion, expiry/relevance, professional framework`

InstituteGPT can assemble CPD plans from current professional obligations and demonstrated competency gaps.

## 18. Opportunities and workforce boundary

InstituteGPT does not own opportunities. It can discover and recommend opportunities through the universal Opportunities layer.

Examples:

`job, internship, apprenticeship, scholarship, grant, fellowship, project, contract, research position`

Education produces capability and evidence; Opportunities provides the actual opportunity; the human/entity participates through HAPI/NGIN/NAIRE and the relevant domain.

## 19. Knowledge boundary

Knowledge is a universal substrate. InstituteGPT is the canonical learning interface to Knowledge.

InstituteGPT may curate, teach, assess and version knowledge for a learning purpose. It does not become the sole owner of all knowledge in the ecosystem.

## 20. Personal AI learning and certification

A personal AI may be sent to InstituteGPT by its human or by its own authorized workflows.

The AI may:

`study → consume approved knowledge → practice → simulate → be evaluated → receive capability metadata → update its knowledge state`

Where an authority certifies a competence or knowledge package, the authoritative issuer remains the issuer. InstituteGPT records provenance and attaches the appropriate evidence or credential reference to the AI's learning record.

AI capability remains bounded by authorization, tool access, human supervision and deployment policy.

## 21. AI tutor / learning buddy

The personal AI may serve its human as:

`AI tutor, study buddy, quiz partner, explainer, practice partner, research assistant, education curator`

The AI can form or join study groups containing humans, AI assistants or both, subject to privacy and participation controls.

## 22. Entity and institutional education

The learner model supports:

`individual | organization | institution | government | association | club | enterprise | AI | agent`

Entities may have education plans, organizational competencies, mandatory training, professional development, certification requirements and institutional learning records.

## 23. Admissions through lifelong learning

The Education record begins before the classroom and continues after graduation.

It can span:

`interest → application → admission → matriculation → learning → SIWES/internship → assessment → graduation → professional entry → employment learning → promotion → CPD → specialization → teaching/research → lifelong learning`

## 24. Learner record

The canonical lifelong record may contain:

`learning activities, programmes, courses, curriculum mappings, skills, competencies, assessments, results, evidence, credentials, experience, internships, SIWES, apprenticeships, research outputs, professional development, licenses/registrations as external references, progression and renewals`

The record is linked to Root but exposed through appropriate privacy and Atlas policies.

## 25. AI learning record

The AI learning record may contain:

`AI identity, principal, knowledge packages, training history, assessments, benchmarks, capabilities, limitations, permissions, tool access, human supervision requirements, deployment context, updates, version history and retirement`

## 26. Universal Learn entry point

Any ecosystem product may invoke InstituteGPT through contextual learning intents:

`Learn | Explain | How To | Practice | Simulate | Test Me | Prepare Me | Find Qualification | Continue Learning | Verify Learning`

The originating product supplies domain context; InstituteGPT resolves educational content and progression.

## 27. Physical-world integration

InstituteGPT may coordinate physical learning through universal scheduling, resource, service, location, communication and workflow capabilities:

`classroom, training centre, driving school, workshop, laboratory, hospital placement, factory, farm, field site, assessment centre, examination hall`

The physical activity remains the domain/provider's operational reality; InstituteGPT supplies the educational structure.

## 28. Open education and OpenEd Bot

Open Education is a first-class InstituteGPT composition, not a separate educational ontology.

`InstituteGPT → OpenEd Bot → institution deployment`

OpenEd Bot covers the global open, distance, flexible, online, blended and lifelong education value chain, including OER discovery, authoring, adaptation, licensing/provenance, open pedagogy, open courseware, distributed learner support, study centres, ODL quality assurance, open assessment, portable credentials, interoperability, cross-border mobility, open research and sustainable open-education models.

UNESCO's OER framework is incorporated as an external normative reference. OpenEd Bot preserves the distinction between public access and an open licence and preserves attribution, provenance and reuse restrictions.

OpenEd Bot composes reusable standards adapters including 1EdTech LTI, Edu-API, OneRoster, QTI, CASE, Caliper, CLR and Open Badges, plus W3C Verifiable Credentials and applicable national/regional quality and qualification frameworks. The adapters are replaceable and do not redefine OMNII.

## 29. OpenEd institutional deployment

A specific open or distance tertiary institution is configured from OpenEd Bot rather than rebuilt:

`OpenEd Bot + institutional identity + authority references + programmes + curricula + academic calendar + admissions + learner support + study centres + assessment rules + grading + credential rules + QA/accreditation + integrations + local language/context + data/privacy policies + branding`

Example:

`NOUN Bot = OpenEd Bot + NOUN institutional configuration`

The same pattern can produce deployments for other open universities, dual-mode universities, distance-learning centres, TVET/ODL institutions, regional consortia and lifelong-learning providers.

## 30. InstituteGPT Notebook

InstituteGPT Notebook is a first-class product/workspace within InstituteGPT and OpenEd Bot.

It supports:

`study notes | course notebooks | research notebooks | teaching notebooks | field notebooks | laboratory notebooks | reading journals | project workspaces | reflective practice | portfolio evidence | supervisor feedback | group work`

Notebook entries may link to learning objects, sources, competencies, assessments, experiences, credentials and IO events. It supports multimodal capture, source provenance, AI-assisted study context, version history and portable learning/evidence export.

## 31. Global standards and interoperability

InstituteGPT and OpenEd Bot use a standards-adapter strategy. Relevant open/global standards include UNESCO OER policy; 1EdTech LTI, Edu-API, OneRoster, QTI, CASE, Caliper, CLR and Open Badges; W3C Verifiable Credentials 2.0; and applicable national/regional frameworks.

CASE can provide machine-readable competency/standards alignment; QTI can provide assessment portability; LTI can connect external learning tools; CLR/Open Badges and W3C VC can support portable verified achievement records. These integrations remain adapters rather than replacements for OMNII's learning semantics.

## 32. Education quality and assurance

InstituteGPT includes quality as a continuous lifecycle:

`design → review → delivery → evidence → learner feedback → learning outcomes → completion/progression → graduate outcomes → tracer/employment evidence → improvement`

For OpenEd/ODL providers this extends to institutional policy, programme/course design, learner support, infrastructure, assessment, evaluation, community engagement, accessibility, regulatory compliance and accreditation readiness.

## 33. Access, inclusion and localization

OpenEd must work across:

`urban | rural | low-bandwidth | offline | mobile | shared-device | basic-phone/voice-assisted | multilingual | disability-accessible | displaced/refugee | working-adult | caregiver | non-traditional learner`

Language, device, network, accessibility and cultural context are configuration dimensions, not afterthoughts.

## 34. Education economy and sustainability

Open learning may be free at point of access while institutions still need sustainable delivery models. InstituteGPT can compose public funding, grants, institutional budgets, employer sponsorship, consortium contributions, philanthropy, training procurement and optional services without turning openly licensed content into proprietary content contrary to its licence.

## 35. Government/system education

InstituteGPT may be composed into system-level deployments for ministries, regulators, funders and consortia covering:

`provider discovery | programme/qualification maps | OER policy | teacher development | learner access | national assessments | workforce skills | equity | funding programmes | quality assurance | system analytics | policy simulation | programme evaluation | national capability planning`

These are governed projections over authorized sources, not universal ownership of all learner data.

## 36. AI education and agentic institutions

InstituteGPT supports AI as learner, tutor, teacher assistant, assessment assistant, administrator, researcher, librarian assistant, accessibility assistant and institutional agent.

Future education may include:

`admissions agents | learner-success agents | academic-advising agents | timetable agents | assessment agents | marking assistants | records agents | library agents | research agents | QA agents | compliance agents | institutional analytics agents`

High-impact decisions remain subject to the applicable authority, policy, human oversight and appeal rights.

## 37. Governance boundaries

InstituteGPT must not:

- impersonate an external regulator or professional body;
- issue an external authority's certificate or license without delegated authority;
- infer professional authorization from AI performance alone;
- turn a simulated result into Actual evidence without a governed real-world event;
- override Root identity, HAPI authority, SEAL or legal human authority;
- claim open licensing where licence rights do not exist;
- disclose learner records merely because an institution participates in an open ecosystem;
- make a proprietary LMS, cloud or AI vendor constitutionally indispensable;
- create a product-specific universal education system that competes with InstituteGPT.

## 38. Canonical products and deployment hierarchy

`InstituteGPT = universal education and lifelong human/AI progression layer`

`OpenEd Bot = global open/distance/flexible/open-university composition of InstituteGPT`
