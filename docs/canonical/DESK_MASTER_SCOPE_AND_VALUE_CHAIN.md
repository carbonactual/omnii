# Desk — Master Scope, Market Universe & End-to-End Value Chain

**Ecosystem:** Carbon Actual / OMNII  
**Status:** CANONICAL PRODUCT SCOPE — ecosystem-conformant composition  
**Depends on:** Carbon Actual constitutional layer, OMNII Core, ABBA, Actual, Atlas, TIP, registries, IO/Ledger, Pulse, Universal Movement/Charter, shared forms/workflows and policy infrastructure.

---

## 1. What the Desk is

The Desk is the ecosystem composition through which a participant can discover, offer, request, compare, arrange, exchange, finance, move, protect, receive, return, recover and revalue economic value.

It does not assume that value must be:

- a conventional product;
- money-denominated;
- bought from a corporation;
- new;
- physically local;
- formally listed;
- traded through an exchange;
- delivered immediately;
- paid in cash;
- moved by a conventional carrier;
- produced by a factory;
- owned by a company;
- available in large volume;
- or even already recognized as a market.

The Desk is designed around **economic need and available capacity**, not around pre-existing marketplace categories.

## 5. How something enters the Desk

### Existing formal market
API, catalogue, exchange feed, supplier portal, inventory feed, procurement system or partner integration.

### Existing marketplace without suitable API
Authorized data partnership, seller/partner intake, file upload, user-provided listing, approved connector or direct transaction handoff.

### Informal market
Field agent, voice, WhatsApp, SMS, USSD, photographed evidence, market association, community representative or assisted listing.

### Unlisted asset/capability
Participant creates an offer or request and the Desk structures it.

### Emerging market
Represent the object and relationship using existing primitives while the market remains marked emerging/experimental.

### Future market
Model the capability and boundary conditions without falsely claiming that the market or legal regime already exists.

- staggered production
- multiple suppliers
- local production
- substitute products
- refurbished alternatives
- used machinery
- trading companies
- group orders

The matching engine should show the break-even point, not merely say “MOQ not met.”

China is a major supply domain, not a special ontology.

Relevant routes include:

```text

trading company
wholesale market
```

The user-facing decision should compare:

```text
supplier price
+ FX
+ payment fee
+ sourcing fee
+ domestic China movement
+ export freight
+ insurance
+ customs/tax
+ local movement
+ financing cost
+ expected delay/risk
```

## 18. Customs optimization

The Desk may optimize legally by:

- correct HS classification
- origin determination
- preferential treatment where available
- accurate customs value
- lower-cost lawful freight mode
- consolidation
- timing
- documentation completeness
- pre-clearance where available
- risk-based workflow

It must never optimize through under-declaration, false description, false origin, concealment or prohibited routing.

WTO trade-facilitation rules specifically recognize risk management, single-window systems, post-clearance audit and technology-enabled trade procedures. citeturn739041search0turn739041search5

## 30. Existing solutions: integrate, replace, partner or observe

Each external solution should be assigned one relationship:

### INTEGRATE
Use APIs, feeds or supported connectors.

### DELEGATE
Send the regulated/physical execution to the provider and record the result.

### PARTNER
Use contractual collaboration when no suitable API exists.

### INGEST
Accept user-provided/listed data while respecting terms and rights.

### OBSERVE
Use as a market signal without pretending to be a transaction channel.

### SUBSTITUTE
Use another provider when the preferred rail fails.

### BUILD SHARED CAPABILITY
Only when an important capability is missing and should belong to the ecosystem rather than one product.

## 34. Fractionalization

Fractionalization is an execution mechanism, not the economic foundation.

Examples:

- one participant buys one unit from a pooled order
- ten buyers share a container
- multiple participants fund an eligible transaction
- a large export demand is assembled from many suppliers
- warehouse capacity is allocated in slices
- machine time is booked in small increments

A fractional share must always resolve to an actual underlying relationship and settlement rule.

## 57. Data freshness

Not all data decays at the same rate.

Examples:

```text

```

The Desk should expose stale-data warnings instead of pretending all information is current.

## 60. The Desk's economic optimization objective

The objective is:

> **maximize sustainable, lawful, evidence-backed value realization subject to user intent, quality, standards, risk, authority, jurisdiction, time and ecosystem constraints.**

This allows the Desk to choose a route that is:

- more expensive but safer;
- slower but much cheaper;
- local rather than imported;
- used rather than new;
- processed rather than raw;
- pooled rather than individual;
- service-based rather than product-based;
- barter rather than cash;
- a repair rather than a replacement.

Desk MUST:

2. use TIP object and market semantics;
3. use shared value and valuation primitives;
4. use shared registries rather than shadow registries;
5. use shared IO/Ledger records;
6. emit outcomes to Pulse;
7. use shared workflows/forms/document/evidence infrastructure;
8. use Universal Movement/Charter for movement relationships where applicable;
9. use reusable provider adapters;
10. remain replaceable and composable;
11. preserve provenance across handoffs;
12. distinguish constitutional semantics from product configuration.

> **Desk is the ecosystem's universal economic coordination interface: it finds and orchestrates legitimate ways for value, capability, goods, services, rights and resources to move between participants at the appropriate scale, through the appropriate rails, with transparent evidence, standards, risk and outcomes.**
