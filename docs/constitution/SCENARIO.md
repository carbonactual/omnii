# SCENARIO

**Status: CANONICAL CROSS-CUTTING CONSTITUTIONAL CONTRACT**

SCENARIO is the grammar for representing situations and chains without enumerating every possible scenario.

## Reality classes
`actual`, `planned`, `hypothetical`, `simulated`, `counterfactual`.

## Invariants
- Plans are not executions.
- Predictions are not observations.
- Simulations are not reality.
- Scenario graphs may branch, merge, loop, reverse, fail, recover and remain unresolved.
- Actual and non-actual paths remain explicitly distinguishable.
- Scenario composition uses existing constitutional objects and capabilities rather than inventing product-specific ontologies.

## Open-world rule
An unknown scenario may be represented immediately using generic constitutional primitives. Novel classification may be proposed later without invalidating the original record.
