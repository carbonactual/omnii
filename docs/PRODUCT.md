# BUNK Product Definition

BUNK is Carbon Actual / OMNII's **universal property product**. It is a product composition built from the OMNII ecosystem, not a standalone platform or foundation system.

## Canonical placement

```text
OMNII ecosystem
├── universal foundation / runtime
├── shared identity / authority / graph / registries / evidence / persistence
├── ABBA / agents / workflows / events / Pulse / reconciliation
├── foundation tools, including TIP
└── products, including BUNK
```

BUNK consumes the ecosystem's canonical semantics. It does not redefine them. See `docs/canonical/BUNK_OMNII_ECOSYSTEM_COMPOSITION.md` and `docs/canonical/BUNK_PRODUCT_MANIFEST.md`.

## Positioning

Property, properly connected.

BUNK connects property identity, human relationships, permissions, evidence, agreements, payments, operations, intelligence, markets and value across the property lifecycle.

## Product surfaces

1. Public/customer experience for discovery, listings, wanted requests, matching, inspections, offers, agreements, payments, tenancy, maintenance, property information and outcome feedback.
2. Partner/operator experience for owners, agents, agencies, managers, inspectors, lawyers, valuers, vendors, artisans, developers, financiers, insurers, facility operators and other authorized participants.
3. Investment/economic experience composed with TIP for property trade, leasing, finance, pooling, fractionalization, decimalization, collateral, secondary markets and tokenization where lawful and enabled.
4. Internal command center for verification, moderation, human SEAL review, risk, cases, reconciliation, audit, providers, ABBA routing, analytics, security and system health.

## Universal property scope

BUNK covers historical/customary, contemporary, emerging, futuristic, extraterrestrial and unknown property candidates. It supports land, parcels, buildings, units, infrastructure, property rights/interests, tenure, access and temporal interests, cemetery/burial/memorial rights, natural/ecological property, biological/material candidates, cultural/heritage property, intangible/digital property, financial property interests, spatial/capacity interests and frontier property.

BUNK also covers the human/service value chain: survey, title/evidence, due diligence, valuation, planning, acquisition, assembly, land banking, finance, design, engineering, procurement, construction, inspection, certification, brokerage, listing, transaction, tenancy, property/facility management, maintenance, insurance, tax/compliance, refinancing, redevelopment, recovery, reuse, preservation and archive.

## Economic boundary

TIP is the canonical OMNII economic foundation tool. BUNK owns property context and property product workflows; TIP owns canonical trade, investment, financing, market, collateral, tokenization and settlement semantics. BUNK must not create a parallel economic ledger or market ontology.

## Ecosystem composition rule

Every proposed BUNK capability must first be classified as an existing OMNII shared capability, existing TIP capability, genuinely BUNK-specific property capability, external integration/adapter, or prohibited duplication. Composition is preferred over reinvention.

## MVP and evolution

The existing production route covers authentication, multi-role onboarding, property/unit registry, listings, search, wanted requests, explainable matching, saves, lead CRM, inspections, offers, basic agreements, payment tracking, tenancy, maintenance, agent profiles, affiliate attribution, Proof, Pulse, human SEAL, moderation, ABBA routing, notifications, analytics and audit. Advanced investment pooling, mortgage orchestration, rent-to-own, fractional/decimalized participation and advanced tokenization remain policy/compliance controlled and are composed through TIP.

## North star

Verified successful property outcomes.

## Non-negotiable rules

- BUNK remains downstream of OMNII.
- BUNK does not establish a second universal identity, authority, graph, registry ontology, persistence boundary or canonical ledger.
- TIP remains the canonical economic foundation consumed by BUNK.
- Property records are separate from listings.
- Evidence is not verification.
- Verification is not public disclosure.
- AI never grants SEAL or makes final high-impact decisions.
- Every important claim retains ROOT lineage.
- Private records remain in VAULT.
- Public ATLAS publication requires valid human permission.
- Financial records are append-only after posting.
- No listing is represented as verified unless its verification route is complete and current.
- Unknown/frontier classification never implies ownership, personhood, sovereignty, consent, legal recognition or market eligibility.
