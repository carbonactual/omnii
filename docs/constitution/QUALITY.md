# QUALITY

**Status: CANONICAL CROSS-CUTTING CONSTITUTIONAL CONTRACT**

QUALITY governs whether an output or outcome is fit, complete, traceable and safe enough for its intended purpose.

## Dimensions
- structural
- evidence
- logical
- operational
- regulatory
- safety
- economic
- continuity

## Invariants
- Completeness is multidimensional.
- A missing requirement is a visible gap, not an invented answer.
- Correctness requires evidence and context appropriate to the domain.
- Quality scores do not replace human or legal authority.
- Standards, certifications and compliance findings remain distinct states.
