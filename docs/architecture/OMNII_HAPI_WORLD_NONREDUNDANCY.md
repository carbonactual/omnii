# OMNII Single-Object Nonredundancy Doctrine

Nonredundancy means **one canonical truth, many governed representations**.

It does not mean the system may not maintain indexes, caches, projections, materialized views, audit records, tokens, fractional interests, backups, or external representations. Those are derived or protective representations with explicit lineage and lifecycle; they are not independent authorities over the same fact.

## Canonical identity

Every admitted ecosystem object receives or resolves to one canonical identity. A consumer must resolve that identity before creating a new representation.

## Duplicate detection

A proposed object is a duplicate when its canonical identity, provenance, semantic scope, and underlying real-world referent match an existing object without a legitimate representation boundary.

Potential duplicates are reconciled before admission. Conflicts are surfaced as evidence and resolved through the constitutional control plane.

## Representation rule

A token, NFT, ledger entry, dashboard record, API object, registry projection, cached value, or product record may represent a canonical object. None may silently redefine its underlying truth.

## Resource accounting

Compute, time, energy, storage, network usage, human effort, physical movement, wear, depreciation, and other material resources are accounted for according to the applicable economic model. Unproductive residue is surfaced to Pulse/Ash/Phoenix rather than hidden as an untracked duplicate or loss.

## Exception rule

Redundancy introduced for resilience, security, availability, disaster recovery, or interoperability must declare its purpose, source identity, lifecycle, reconciliation rule, and authority. Protective redundancy is therefore controlled representation, not competing truth.
