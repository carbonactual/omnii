# CHARTER — Universal Transport & Movement Completeness

**Status:** Canonical Charter composition supplement  
**Architecture position:** Domain composition over existing OMNII primitives.  
**Economic boundary:** Value movement remains in IO. Charter does not create money, pricing, payment, settlement or ledger primitives.

## 1. Purpose

This document closes the scope boundary for Charter as OMNII's universal transport and movement domain. Charter is intentionally broad: it is not constrained to passenger vehicles, commercial transport, or any single mode, industry, class, technology, geography, era or economic segment.

The governing test is functional:

> **If an object, person, animal, system, infrastructure element, service, workflow or event materially performs, enables, controls, supports, observes, coordinates, recovers, regulates or records movement through space, Charter may compose it in a movement context.**

This does not transfer ownership of the underlying canonical object or capability to Charter. Charter owns the movement context and composes the canonical source.

- cranes, excavators, drilling rigs and tunnel-boring/boring systems where movement, displacement or transport is the relevant function;
- trains, metros, trams, monorails, funiculars and guided systems;
- elevators/lifts, escalators, moving walkways, platform lifts, gondolas, cable cars and ski lifts;
- conveyors, pipelines and other infrastructure-mediated movement;
- boats, fishing vessels, ferries, barges, cargo ships, cruise ships, yachts and superyachts;
- submarines, submersibles, underwater robots and other marine/submarine systems;

- satellites when movement, positioning or orbital transport is relevant;
- future, experimental, hypothetical or unknown movement systems with explicit epistemic status;
- biological migration and non-human movement where the movement itself is the relevant knowledge, transport or observation context.

## 7. Availability, capacity, capability, condition and position

These remain separate dimensions:

- **Capability:** what the object/person/system can do.
- **Capacity:** how much it can carry, support or perform.
- **Availability:** whether it can be used during a specified window.
- **Condition:** whether it is fit, restricted, damaged, maintained or otherwise situated in an operational state.
- **Position:** where it is, was, is expected to be or is planned to be.
- **Time:** when the position/state/availability applies.
- **Authority:** whether the intended operation is permitted.

A transport candidate is executable only when the applicable dimensions compose successfully.

## 10. Transport products remain distinct compositions

Products related to movement may coexist inside the broader OMNII ecosystem without duplicating Charter's transport primitives.

Examples include:

- **Charter:** universal transport/movement domain and execution context;

These products may reference and compose one another. Their existence does not justify duplicate vehicle, person, fleet, route, identity, authority, condition, availability, event, registry or economic primitives.

## 15. Future and unknown movement

Charter must remain extensible to movement systems not yet standardized or invented. Unknown, speculative, extraterrestrial or hypothetical entities may be represented only with explicit epistemic status and must never be promoted to verified fact merely because the schema can represent them.

This allows the same architecture to accommodate future drones, autonomous systems, commercial space transportation, novel marine/air/ground systems and other emerging modes without creating new constitutional primitives.

## 16. Economic boundary

Charter is not the source of economic truth.

Where movement creates or requires value activity, Charter emits/consumes the relevant canonical relationship and workflow context and **IO remains the source for value movement**.

Therefore Charter must not introduce:

- money primitives;
- payment primitives;
- pricing primitives;
- settlement primitives;
- financial ledgers;
- duplicate economic accounts.

The same rule applies to identity, authority, universal objects, lifecycle, evidence, registries and other existing OMNII sources.

## 17. Architectural invariant

> **Charter may be capability-abundant without becoming constitutionally overlapping.**

Its breadth comes from composition of canonical OMNII capabilities and objects, not from creating a separate primitive for every mode, vehicle, service, profession, infrastructure element or experience.

The source-of-truth test is mandatory:

1. Identify the canonical source.
2. Reuse its primitive/capability.
3. Add only the movement relationship/context required by Charter.
4. Preserve provenance, authority, lifecycle and evidence.
5. Do not duplicate the source capability in Charter.

## 18. Completion statement

Charter is therefore the universal movement composition for the ecosystem: from a person walking through a doorway, an escalator carrying passengers, a horse pulling a chariot, a tow truck recovering a broken car, a drilling machine advancing through earth, a cargo ship crossing an ocean, a private aircraft carrying passengers, an autonomous drone operating a route, to spacecraft transporting people or payloads beyond Earth.

The scale changes. The mode changes. The technology changes. The economic context changes. The authority changes. The object changes.

The canonical movement relationship remains composable through Charter and OMNII.
