# OMNII — Global Procurement Intelligence

**Status:** CANONICAL CAPABILITY SCOPE  
**Scope:** Global-by-default  
**Layer:** Shared ecosystem procurement intelligence

## Definition

Global Procurement Intelligence is the reusable capability for discovering, interpreting and coordinating purchasing demand and procurement pathways across public, institutional, corporate, community and network buyers.

## Procurement universe

```text
RFQ
RFP
tender
framework
catalogue demand
recurring procurement
spot procurement
project procurement
bulk procurement
institutional procurement
public procurement
```

## Procurement objects

Represent buyer, requirement, specification, quantity, budget/value basis, deadline, jurisdiction, eligibility, standards, delivery basis, evidence, procurement method and status.

## Discovery

Continuously identify authorized procurement notices, recurring demand, framework opportunities and institutional buying patterns from approved sources.

## Supplier side

Connect procurement demand to Sourcing, Matching and Verification/Trust. Support supplier eligibility signals, evidence packages and capability-gap discovery.

## Aggregation

Aggregate compatible procurement requirements across participants when authorized, preserving allocation, approval and settlement lineage.

## Bid support

Provide research, requirement decomposition, compliance checklists, sourcing alternatives, costing scenarios and evidence organization. It may assist bid preparation but never guarantees award or misrepresents eligibility.

## Timeliness

Deadlines, validity periods, clarifications, addenda and procurement calendar events are first-class temporal objects.

## Interfaces

Connects to Opportunity, Sourcing, Matching, Market Intelligence, Research, Trade/Regulatory Intelligence, Verification/Trust, Finance/Funding, TIP/IO and workflows/forms.

## Boundary

Not a procurement authority, bid winner, contracting authority or guarantee of public/private award.

## Canonical invariant

> **Procurement Intelligence makes purchasing demand and lawful procurement pathways discoverable and comparable while leaving procurement authority and award decisions with the authorized buyer.**
