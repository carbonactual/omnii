# Carbon Actual Ecosystem Relationship Model

**Status:** Canonical OMNII ecosystem architecture doctrine, harvested from `carbonactual/Carbon-Actual-`.

## Core model

Carbon Actual is not best understood as a collection of isolated modules. It is a living relationship system in which identities exist within Roots, manifest Actual states, interact through relationships, move through Becoming, and are represented through fitted Atlas/Plate experiences.

The practical core is:

**Identity (#) → Root → Actual → Becoming → Atlas**

with **Relationship** and **Interaction** operating across the entire continuum.

These are dimensions of a living system, not mandatory software layers or a rigid sequence.

## Identity (#)

Identity is the persistent referent of a person, organization, institution, agent, resource, territory, household, community, product, or other entity.

Identity provides continuity by which Root, Actual, Becoming, relationships, interactions, and Atlas expressions can remain connected over time.

## Root

Root is the grounding context of identity. It includes, as applicable:

- origin
- lineage
- territory
- family
- culture
- habitat
- relationships
- networks
- communities
- resources
- institutions
- dependencies
- history
- conditions
- opportunities

Root is not merely a location or parent record. It is the ecological context that participates in forming and sustaining identity.

## Actual

Actual is the current manifested state of the entity or reality at a point in time. It answers:

> **What is here now?**

Actual may include state, condition, position, availability, ownership, value, activity, status, capability, obligation, or other present manifestations appropriate to the entity.

Actual is time-bound without destroying identity continuity.

## Becoming

Becoming is the continuum through which Actual changes. It includes movement, development, interaction, learning, adaptation, transition, consequence, emergence, and future possibility.

It answers:

> **What is happening through time, and what can this become?**

Becoming is not a workflow stage that ends. It is the ongoing movement of reality.

## Relationship

Relationship is a first-class dimension of the ecosystem. It connects identities to other identities, Roots, resources, authorities, events, flows, obligations, agreements, outcomes, and contexts.

A relationship may carry its own:

- state
- history
- direction
- authority
- value
- permissions
- obligations
- provenance
- consequences
- temporal validity

The ecosystem should therefore model meaningful relationships rather than reducing them to incidental joins between records.

## Interaction

Interaction is what happens through relationships.

An interaction may change Actual state, create or alter a relationship, produce value, generate evidence, trigger an obligation, change authority, create an event, or move an entity through Becoming.

Conceptually:

**Relationship enables Interaction → Interaction can change Actual → Actual can alter Becoming → Becoming can create new relationships.**

This is a living feedback loop rather than a one-way transaction pipeline.

## Atlas

Atlas is the formed, navigable expression of relevant reality.

It brings the appropriate aspects of Identity, Root, Actual, Becoming, relationships, and interactions into a form that a particular person or entity can perceive, understand, navigate, and act upon.

Atlas is therefore not simply a dashboard or database view. It is the ecosystem's capacity to make a living context intelligible.

The same underlying reality may legitimately produce different Atlas expressions for different observers, purposes, permissions, or moments.

## Plate

A Plate is a naturally fitted curation within the Atlas continuum.

It is fitted to the person, encounter, purpose, entity, and habitat. It has no mandatory visual shape.

A Plate may be visual, spatial, relational, temporal, financial, geographic, operational, transactional, narrative, conversational, or another form appropriate to the reality.

The principle is:

> **Fit the form to the reality and the person; do not force the person or reality into the form.**

## The practical loop

Conceptually:

**Identity (#)**
→ grounded in **Root**
→ manifests **Actual**
→ enters **Relationship / Interaction**
→ moves through **Becoming**
→ is made intelligible through **Atlas / Plate**
→ produces understanding and action
→ changes relationships and/or Actual
→ continues Becoming.

This is not a required runtime sequence. It is a model for how the ecosystem should understand and design living systems.

## Architectural implications

### Data

Data models should preserve identity continuity while representing context, state, relationships, events, flows, provenance, and temporal change.

### APIs

APIs should expose meaningful capabilities and relationships rather than only CRUD representations of isolated objects.

### Agents

Agents should reason about entities in context: identity, Root, Actual state, relationships, permissions, interactions, history, and Becoming—not merely prompts or documents.

### Workflows

Workflows should be able to respond to events and state changes, create consequences, update relationships, and continue the continuum.

### UX

Interfaces should reveal the context needed for the current person and purpose. Complexity should be carried by the system where possible rather than imposed on the user.

### Products

Every product should identify the being it serves, its habitat, its important relationships, its current Actual state, its Becoming, and the Atlas forms required for useful interaction.

### Framer and web builds

A business website or digital experience is an individual Plate. It should reveal the business and the ecology that gives it meaning rather than presenting an isolated brand object.

## Ecological identity test

> **If the habitat and meaningful relationships were removed, would the entity still mean the same thing?**

If not, those relationships and context belong in the representation and, where appropriate, the underlying model.

## Thriving test

> **Does the system help the being live, navigate, connect, become, and thrive within its real habitat?**

The ecosystem should not become an artificial habitat that traps the user. Its purpose is to improve the user's ability to operate and thrive in the real world.

## Whole and individuality

Every individual build should be fully itself while remaining part of the Carbon Actual whole.

The whole is not created through forced visual uniformity. It emerges from a common underlying philosophy of identity, ecology, relationship, interaction, continuity, natural fit, and thriving.

**Many worlds. One living whole.**
