# OMNII — Global Finance & Funding Discovery

**Status:** CANONICAL CAPABILITY SCOPE  
**Scope:** Global-by-default  
**Layer:** Shared ecosystem finance/funding discovery

## Definition

Global Finance & Funding Discovery is the reusable capability for discovering, comparing and composing lawful funding paths for people, businesses, trade, assets, projects and institutional needs, then routing execution to appropriately authorized financial providers.

## Funding universe

```text
working capital
trade finance
equipment finance
asset finance
supplier credit
receivables finance
guarantees
grants
investment
project finance
community/group finance
insurance-linked risk capacity
```

## Finance need normalization

Represent purpose, amount, currency, timing, cash-flow pattern, collateral/guarantee context, eligibility signals, jurisdiction, risk, documentation, intended use and repayment/return structure where relevant.

## Discovery

Discover banks, fintechs, funds, grant programs, development institutions, suppliers offering credit and other lawful funding sources through replaceable adapters and evidence-backed records.

## Composition

Compare alternative funding stacks, for example:

```text
supplier credit + working capital
machine + equipment finance
purchase order + trade finance
project + grant + investment
```

## Financing-gap intelligence

Detect when an otherwise viable sourcing, opportunity or capability-development path fails primarily because of liquidity, tenor, guarantees, collateral or timing.

## Evidence and eligibility

Distinguish discovered products from confirmed eligibility. Preserve rates/terms, effective dates, eligibility evidence, fees and conditions.

## Risk boundary

The ecosystem may discover and explain financing options; regulated providers perform underwriting, lending, investment, deposit-taking, payments and other regulated acts.

## Interfaces

Connects to Intent, Opportunity, Matching, Market Intelligence, Sourcing, Verification/Trust, Value/VCE, TIP/IO, Trade/Regulatory Intelligence and Pulse.

## Boundary

Not a bank, lender, investment adviser, deposit taker, securities exchange or credit bureau by default.

## Canonical invariant

> **Funding Discovery finds lawful financial pathways that may unlock a requirement or opportunity, while regulated financial decisions remain with authorized providers.**
