# ECONOMIC COMPOSITION

**Status: CANONICAL CONSTITUTIONAL CONTRACT**

This contract defines how OMNII represents fractionalization, decimalization, democratization, tokenization, minting and blockchain without confusing them with value, ownership, authority or the constitutional substrate.

The complete definitions, entity model, capabilities, lifecycle, interoperability map, scenario coverage, invariants and standards reference are maintained in [`ECONOMIC_COMPOSITION_SCOPE.md`](./ECONOMIC_COMPOSITION_SCOPE.md).

## Core Distinctions

- **Fractionalization** = selecting or allocating a fraction of a larger capability, resource, entitlement, capacity or participation space.
- **Decimalization** = representing the selected quantity, level, intensity, precision or resolution at the granularity supported by the underlying domain.
- **Democratization** = governing equitable, accessible and contestable distribution of capabilities and opportunities; it is not synonymous with equal ownership or equal consumption.
- **Tokenization** = representing an underlying governed object, right, claim, entitlement, allocation, receipt, state or value exposure in a transferable or addressable digital form.
- **Minting** = controlled creation or issuance of a token or digital representation under explicit issuance authority, supply rules, provenance and lifecycle constraints.
- **Blockchain** = an optional external or internal distributed-ledger settlement/provenance infrastructure. Blockchain is not required for tokenization and tokenization does not imply blockchain.

## Constitutional Boundaries

`Value ≠ Token`

`Token ≠ Ownership`

`Token ≠ Authority`

`Fraction ≠ Decimal`

`Democratization ≠ Equalization`

`Minting ≠ Value Creation`

`Blockchain ≠ Truth`

`Blockchain ≠ Governance`

A token may represent an economic relationship, but the represented right remains defined by the underlying constitutional object, authority, contract, jurisdiction and provenance.

## Asset / Liability Inversion

For a defined period and context:

`Value Given = aggregate of independently valued resource/value dimensions actually committed or consumed.`

`Pulse Returned = aggregate of independently valued realized feedback/outcomes.`

```text
Pulse Returned > Value Given  → LIABILITY
Pulse Returned < Value Given  → ASSET
Pulse Returned = Value Given  → BALANCED
```

Asset/liability is a dynamic contextual state under the OMNII inverted-economic doctrine; conventional statutory accounting definitions remain separate interpretations where applicable.

## Ecosystem Value

Individual value vectors aggregate into group, territory, network and ecosystem vectors while preserving source-level dimensions.

OMNII may derive:

- individual vector;
- group/community vector;
- territory/network vector;
- general ecosystem vector;
- average capability benchmark;
- constitutional floor;
- protected Safe Haven reserve.

The **Floor** is the protected minimum capability/value condition required by applicable governance.

The **Average** is the contextual normal/benchmark capability available across a defined population or capacity base.

The **Safe Haven** is protected reserve capacity that should not be consumed merely to inflate short-term averages or outputs.

## No Loss / No Silent Dilution

Economic compositions preserve original quantity, fractional allocations, decimal precision, token supply changes, ownership/custody changes, transfers, redemptions, burns/retirements, reversals, expiries, fees, resource consumption, expected outcomes and actual outcomes.

Every transformation must be traceable from source value/resources to resulting representations and back.

## Open World

The economic grammar is open-ended. New forms of value, entitlement, fractional use, token representation, settlement, minting and distributed coordination may be introduced without redefining existing constitutional semantics.
