# NASC Statement of Work

## Objective

Configure, deploy, test and hand over a NASC institutional platform using OMNII reusable capabilities.

## In scope

Institution and authority model; actor/organization registries; crop/variety and seed-lot registries; facilities; licensing/permits; inspection; laboratories and evidence; certification; complaints/incidents/enforcement; public verification; forms; workflow/case management; notifications; fees/reconciliation; reporting/Pulse; audit/compliance; integrations; security and continuity; UAT; training; documentation; handoff/exit.

## Deliverables

1. Versioned NASC domain configuration.
2. Supabase institutional composition and migration.
3. Forms and workflow catalogs.
4. Evidence, controls, permissions and metrics definitions.
5. UAT/acceptance package.
6. Operations/admin/user/security/handoff documentation.
7. Deployment manifest and implementation roadmap.

## Acceptance

Acceptance occurs when the agreed UAT scenarios pass, material security/performance findings are resolved or accepted by the appropriate authority, and the deployment is documented sufficiently for institutional operation and handoff.

## Exclusions

Statutory decisions, regulated credentials, production integrations and production data remain subject to NASC authorization, applicable law, procurement and change control.
