# Carbon Actual Ecosystem Architecture

**Status:** Canonical architectural consolidation  
**Ecosystem:** Carbon Actual  
**Operating property:** Omni  
**Constitutional intelligence:** ABBA  

## 1. Architectural identity

Carbon Actual is the ecosystem. **Omni is not the name of the ecosystem** and is not an independent layer. Omni describes the ecosystem's omnidirectional, continuously recomposing operating behavior: movement, circulation, value, Pulse, mathematics, IO, relationships, and economic state can change the configuration of the ecosystem at every moment.

The architecture is deliberately compressed. It is not a stack of hundreds of permanent layers. Capabilities are represented once and composed repeatedly.

The fundamental rule is:

> **Build universal capabilities once; compose them into anything.**

Anything may be digital, physical, human, machine, organizational, governmental, financial, biological, autonomous, or a future entity class.

## 2. Constitutional ground

### Being

Being is the existential ground of the Carbon Actual ecosystem: what exists, participates, persists, changes, relates, contributes, receives, owns, owes, learns, or becomes.

### ABBA

ABBA is the master intelligence/orchestration intelligence of the ecosystem. ABBA does not replace a Being. It coordinates, evaluates, learns, curates, routes, enables, and balances intelligence across the ecosystem.

### Laws, principles and doctrines

The constitutional framework draws selectively from useful principles and source traditions rather than reproducing them as literal software layers. Relevant sources include:

- Hermetic principles, especially correspondence, polarity, rhythm/pendulum, cause and effect, mentalism, vibration and related balance concepts.
- Kabbalistic Tree/path concepts, adapted as a source for structured progression and curation; Carbon Actual uses **HASH/path identity concepts**, not a claim to reproduce a religious system.
- Masonic progression concepts, used only as a source for staged learning, responsibility and capability progression.
- Continuum, Union, Curation, Communication, Integration and other internally defined Carbon Actual laws.
- Mathematics, logic, probability, measurement, optimization, systems theory and evidence.

These sources inform design. They do not override evidence, safety, human rights, law, mathematical validation, or constitutional governance.

## 3. Core operating principles

The compressed operating fabric is:

**Being · ABBA · Value · Pulse · IO · Union · Communication · Continuum · Curation · Governance · Mathematics · Integration · Omni**

### Value

Value is the common measurement medium. Time, energy, labor, attention, computation, data, distance, direction, material, resource consumption, depreciation, risk, opportunity, service, contribution and outcome can be measured into a common economic model.

### Pulse

Pulse is observed feedback/evidence produced by activity. It records what actually happened and becomes an input to Value, learning, governance, curation and Becoming.

### IO

IO is the open movement and mobility fabric: how resources, information, value, people, capabilities, instructions, opportunities and physical/digital activity enter, leave and move through the ecosystem.

### Union

Union represents relationships, associations, networks, connections, memberships, collaborations and combinations between entities and capabilities.

### Communication

Communication is a first-class capability: human, machine, agent, institutional, physical and digital communication can be composed through the same ecosystem fabric.

### Continuum

Continuum represents lifecycle and temporal continuity: state changes, progression, maintenance, inheritance, migration, recurrence and future states.

### Curation

Curation transforms evidence and available capability into a contextually appropriate next action, opportunity, recommendation, team, workflow or Becoming path.

### Integration

Integration is a constitutional value. Carbon Actual is designed to integrate with free/open-source software, open alternatives, white-label systems, SDKs, APIs, AI models, agents, MCP/A2A-compatible systems, decentralized networks, blockchains, ledgers, hardware, institutions and other interoperable systems. Providers remain replaceable.

## 4. Economic inversion

Carbon Actual does not simply reproduce conventional economic assumptions.

The ecosystem uses an **inverted economic relationship** in which liabilities can represent opportunities/responsibilities for the ecosystem to absorb and assets can carry responsibilities for their owners. The exact mathematical and regulatory implementation must be validated rather than assumed.

A useful conceptual state function is:

```text
sent_value = resources contributed to an activity
pulse = measured consequential return/feedback
net_position = pulse - sent_value
```

Conceptually:

- `pulse > sent_value` → positive return / asset-side consequence.
- `pulse < sent_value` → liability-side consequence / ecosystem responsibility.
- the balanced reference is **Value**, representing the desired median/floor/boundary condition for sustainable operation.

This is an architectural hypothesis and must be subjected to mathematical, economic, behavioral, legal and empirical validation before monetary policy is frozen.

## 7. Foundation identity and routing fabric

The foundation is intentionally compressed.

### HASH

HASH is the ecosystem identity construct. For a human, it represents the balanced relationship between the human and their associated intelligence. It can also represent other entities according to the universal identity model.

HASH must support provenance, identity, capability, authority, relationships, consent, selective disclosure, delegation and continuity without exposing unnecessary private information.

### SEAL

SEAL is the human-authority/approval mechanism. It represents authoritative human consent, approval, attestation or intervention where required.

### Terminal

Terminal is the intake, normalization, minting, classification and routing point for ecosystem events and outputs. It receives minted activity and determines where the resulting information/value belongs.

### Mint

Minting turns an observed event, contribution, credential, capability, relationship, right, asset, outcome or other qualified object into an ecosystem-recognized record/representation.

### Ash

Ash represents residual/waste/non-value or unaccepted activity after useful value has been extracted and accounted for. It is also a security/context carrier for controlled interaction with external systems.

Carbon Actual has a **no-waste doctrine**: residuals must be classified, reused where possible, neutralized, archived, or safely discarded.

### Phoenix

Phoenix is the active security/recovery response pattern associated with Ash. A qualifying harmful, malicious, non-conforming or dangerous condition can escalate through controlled response, containment, revocation, quarantine, recovery, tracking and other authorized actions.

Phoenix must never be defined as unrestricted autonomous destruction. Its actions are governed, auditable, proportionate and safety-constrained.

### Root

Root contains highly authoritative or foundational entity-specific information and relationships.

### Index

Index is the general discovery/directory layer: broadly addressable knowledge, references, classifications and public/general ecosystem information.

### Vault

Vault contains protected value, sensitive records, reserves, custody information and other data/assets requiring stronger access controls.

### Actual

Actual is the operational/day-to-day view of a Being, organization, system or environment: what is happening now, what needs attention, what is active, and what actions are available.

### Atlas

Atlas is the public/profile/discovery representation: the ecosystem equivalent of a verified profile, professional identity, public record, social presence and discoverable capability map. Atlas may draw verified information from Root and other authorized sources.

### Becoming

Becoming is the curation/progression engine. It uses first-party information, Pulse, Value, capability, relationships, context and opportunity to determine what a Being or system can become/do next. It is not merely a screen or recommendation feed; it may orchestrate physical, social, digital and institutional activity.

## 8. HASH + intelligence + human model

A human HASH can be associated with an intelligence instance orchestrated from ABBA. The intelligence and human remain distinct participants while operating as a balanced, capable partnership.

External intelligences—such as models, assistants, agents and services—may be integrated into the person's intelligence environment rather than forcing the person to repeatedly create isolated identities and workflows.

The goal is **augmentation without domination or dependency**. Human authority remains explicit. AI capabilities are bounded by consent, governance, security and role permissions.

## 9. HAPI and HAPI World

### HAPI — Human API

HAPI is the human capability/execution interface: the physical human side that can receive curated work, perform real-world activity, communicate, socialize, operate equipment, deliver services, make decisions and provide human judgment.

### HAPI World

HAPI World is the intelligence/agent world where AI agents, tools, models, groups and autonomous experiments can operate, learn, collaborate and transact within controlled boundaries.

HAPI World is not the identity itself. It is an intelligence environment associated with the HASH ecosystem.

## 10. Swarms and Teams

### Swarm

A Swarm is the capability field for a function. It aggregates alternative implementations without prematurely selecting a winner.

Members may include humans, agents, models, tools, open-source projects, APIs, SDKs, services, organizations, machines, hardware, datasets, vendors, decentralized networks or future entity types.

Swarm intelligence evaluates capability, specialization, strengths, weaknesses, performance, cost, reliability, latency, security, privacy, openness, maturity, provenance, dependencies, licensing, resource requirements, failure modes, risks and context suitability.

### Team

A Team is a purposeful composition of capabilities selected from one or more Swarms for a specific outcome.

```text
Need
 → capability decomposition
 → Swarms
 → evaluate / benchmark / curate
 → select / combine / substitute
 → Team
 → workflow
 → execution
 → Pulse + Value
 → Swarm learning
```

Teams can contain humans + AI + agents + tools + services + organizations + machines + physical resources.

## 11. Universal Product Composer

Every Carbon Actual product, contract, partnership, government system, organizational system or physical deployment is a **branch/composition**, not an isolated architecture.

A build begins with an outcome, decomposes it into capabilities, queries Swarms, composes a Team, attaches identity/authority/security/economics, assembles workflows and integrations, executes, measures Pulse and Value, and feeds the result back into the ecosystem.

This makes products LEGO-like without making the ecosystem simplistic: the reusable bricks are constitutional capabilities, not arbitrary UI components.

## 15. Security, privacy and Ash/Phoenix

Security is defense-in-depth, not a claim that a system is literally unbreakable.

Required properties include:

- least privilege
- zero-trust principles
- encryption
- key management
- selective disclosure
- consent
- provenance
- isolation
- signed/attested actions where appropriate
- auditability
- revocation
- quarantine
- recovery
- post-quantum migration readiness
- abuse detection
- supply-chain security
- secure integration boundaries

For third-party interactions, Carbon Actual should expose the minimum required representation. Where an interaction produces residual risk or non-value context, Ash can carry the controlled residual representation. A security policy can escalate qualifying threats into Phoenix workflows.

## 16. No-waste architecture

Nothing should simply disappear because it is inconvenient.

Every output is classified as:

- useful value
- reusable resource
- evidence/knowledge
- liability/obligation
- residual/waste
- security context
- unknown/unclassified pending evaluation

Unknown is a valid temporary state. It must not be silently discarded.

## 17. Becoming and curation

The ecosystem continuously evaluates:

```text
Being
 + HASH
 + capabilities
 + relationships
 + environment
 + opportunities
 + Pulse
 + Value
 + commitments
 + constraints
 → Becoming
```

Becoming can produce recommendations, training, employment, social activity, physical tasks, investment opportunities, maintenance actions, organizational changes, team formation, new capabilities or other curated paths.

A core human principle is:

> **If a human can safely and meaningfully do something effectively, the ecosystem should prefer enabling the human to do it rather than unnecessarily replacing the human with automation.**

This keeps physical life, socialization, human agency and community participation central to the ecosystem.

## 18. Organizational intelligence

Organizations, governments, associations and other entities can have their own ecosystem identities and capability structures. Individual HASH identities can participate under explicit authority and roles.

Atlas, HAPI, Swarms, Teams, KPIs, Pulse, Value, Union and Communication can be used to identify capability gaps, strengthen teams, recruit, train, replace, redistribute or curate organizational capacity—subject to fairness, consent, law and governance.

## 19. Future extensibility

The ontology must not hard-code today's world as its boundary.

New humans, AI systems, robots, machines, organizations, biological entities, animals, autonomous systems, new computational paradigms, extraterrestrial/future entities or other unknown classes must be representable through the universal entity/capability contracts without rewriting constitutional invariants.

The architecture therefore distinguishes:

**invariants** → constitutional rules that should remain stable  
**capabilities** → reusable functions  
**adapters** → replaceable implementations  
**branches** → composed products/systems  
**instances** → actual deployed entities and activity

## 21. Architectural invariants

1. Carbon Actual is the ecosystem.
2. Omni is an operating property, not the ecosystem name.
3. Being is the existential ground.
4. ABBA is the master intelligence/orchestrator; it does not erase human agency.
5. Value and Pulse are central economic/measurement primitives.
6. Integration is a core value.
7. Universal capabilities are built once and reused.
8. Swarms preserve capability diversity; Teams compose capabilities for outcomes.
9. Products are branches/compositions, not isolated technological islands.
10. Human, digital, physical and machine execution are first-class.
11. Provenance and ownership must be traceable while privacy is preserved.
12. No-waste means residuals are classified and handled, not silently discarded.
13. Security is governed, layered and recoverable; no absolute unbreakability claim is made.
14. Providers and infrastructure implementations remain replaceable.
15. Unknown future entity/capability classes remain admissible.
16. Architecture is compressed; duplication belongs in implementations only when justified.
17. Constitutional invariants are frozen only after evidence, mathematical validation and appropriate legal/safety review.

## 22. Implementation boundary

This document defines the architecture. It does not prematurely dictate a single database, blockchain, AI provider, cloud, UI framework, payment provider, hardware protocol or vendor.

Codex and implementation teams should derive schemas, APIs, registries, kernels, adapters, services, user experiences and deployments from these invariants and contracts.

**The architecture is the reusable grammar. Products are sentences composed from it.**
