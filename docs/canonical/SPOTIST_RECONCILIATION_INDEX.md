## Interpretation rule

When an older product, roadmap, portfolio, or doctrine document uses SPOTIST to mean sourcing, procurement, supplier discovery, minerals, or resource discovery, interpret that material as a **historical or domain-specific application** unless it explicitly declares itself authoritative for canonical semantics.

The current canonical semantic meaning is the universal SEEK capability.

## Product rule

SPOTIST may have a standalone product surface while simultaneously remaining reusable as an ecosystem capability.

A standalone product is an implementation/composition of the capability, not the owner of its constitutional meaning.

## Boundary rule

SPOTIST:

- discovers, matches, traces, qualifies, verifies and monitors seeks;
- may connect or assemble candidates;
- does not independently grant authority;
- does not replace ABBA;
- does not replace OMNI;
- does not replace I/O;
- does not create a parallel identity, authority or economic constitution.

## Non-regression rule

New implementations must consume this reconciliation rather than re-deriving SPOTIST from historical sourcing-only material.
