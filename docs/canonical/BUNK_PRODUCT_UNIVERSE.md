# BUNK — Universal Property Product

**Product:** BUNK  
**Ecosystem:** Carbon Actual / OMNII  
**Status:** Canonical product boundary  
**Positioning:** Property, properly connected.

## 1. Definition

BUNK is OMNII's universal property product: the connected product for discovering, identifying, registering, understanding, proving, developing, financing, trading, investing in, using, operating, maintaining, transferring, preserving and recovering property, property interests and property-related opportunities.

BUNK spans historical, customary, contemporary, emerging, futuristic, extraterrestrial and unknown property candidates. Classification never implies ownership, personhood, sovereignty, legal title, tradeability or investment eligibility.

## 3. Property universe

BUNK recognizes at least these property families:

- land, parcels, plots, estates, farms, ranches, forests, wetlands, islands, urban/peri-urban/rural land, public/community/customary/institutional/religious land, development land, brownfield/greenfield sites and land banks;
- buildings and units: houses, apartments, estates, offices, retail, hospitality, industrial, logistics, storage, healthcare, education, cultural, religious, civic, sports and special-purpose property;
- infrastructure property: roads, bridges, rail, ports, airports, utilities, energy, telecom, fiber, charging, water/wastewater and data-center facilities;
- property rights/interests: ownership, co-ownership, beneficial interests, leasehold, tenancy, occupancy, possession, usufruct, easements, access, utility, development, extraction, conservation, concession, management, revenue, option, renewal, first-refusal, security and inheritance interests;
- temporal/spatial property: timeshare, reservations, parking/storage periods, room nights, capacity windows, use allocations, route/berth/slot positions where legally recognized;
- burial/cemetery property: cemetery land, grave plots, burial rights, mausoleum/crypt spaces, columbarium niches, memorial rights, reservation, transfer/succession and maintenance interests;
- natural/ecological property: forests, watersheds, aquifers, habitats, protected areas, conservation interests, carbon/biodiversity/water outcomes and environmental rights or market instruments where recognized;
- biological property candidates: seed, germplasm, plant/animal/microbial resources, tissue, specimens, genetic material and associated rights/benefit-sharing interests under applicable law;
- cultural/historical property: heritage sites, archaeological resources, historic buildings, sacred sites, traditional/community interests, artifacts and cultural objects subject to applicable law;
- intangible/digital property: IP, software, domains, datasets, licenses, digital assets, virtual spaces, digital twins and other contract/license-based interests;
- financial property interests: direct/co-owned property, pooled interests, funds, REIT exposure, debt, equity, revenue interests, collateral, receivables and other property-linked claims;
- frontier property: floating/offshore, underground, autonomous, orbital, lunar, extraterrestrial and other future property candidates;
- unknown property candidates: unknown-origin objects, artifacts, biological/material samples and other unresolved candidates modeled without automatic ownership or market status.

The list is extensible. New classes extend the property vocabulary without redefining OMNII's universal semantics.

## 5. Participants and capability economy

BUNK connects buyers, sellers, tenants, landlords, investors, developers, owners, communities, governments and institutions with agents, brokers, agencies, property managers, facility managers, inspectors, surveyors, valuers, lawyers, planners, architects, engineers, quantity surveyors, contractors, builders, artisans, vendors, suppliers, financiers, insurers, maintenance providers, security providers, cleaning providers and technology operators.

Each participant remains an OMNII identity/entity with capabilities, authority, evidence, availability and reputation. BUNK composes those capabilities into property workflows.

## 6. Listings and demand

Listings are representations of underlying property or property interests, not the property record itself. BUNK supports sale, rent, lease, short/long stay, land, development, off-plan, auction, tender, distressed/recovery, joint venture, land banking, co-investment, pooled/fractional exposure, financing, property services, maintenance and capacity/usage offers.

Demand/wanted flows cover buyers, tenants, investors, land, buildings, accommodation, development partners, contractors, artisans, property managers, financing and other property needs.

## 7. Investment and economic boundary

BUNK supplies property context. TIP supplies the canonical economic machinery for sale, leasing, financing, investment, pooling, syndication, fractionalization, decimalization, collateralization, insurance/risk markets, secondary markets, tokenization and settlement, subject to legal and policy controls.

Fractionalization or tokenization is never treated as automatic ownership. BUNK must preserve the relationship between physical property, legal/beneficial interest, economic exposure, digital representation and the authority/eligibility required to transact.

## 8. Intelligence

Property intelligence may cover location, title/tenure, evidence, ownership relationships, physical condition, development potential, market conditions, financial performance, environmental exposure, operations, maintenance, participants, reputation, risk and lifecycle state.

Intelligence informs decisions; it does not independently grant title, authority, SEAL, permission or regulated eligibility.

## 9. Frontier and unknown rule

BUNK may classify futuristic, extraterrestrial or unknown objects and environments as property candidates. Unknown is an epistemic state, not an ownership, personhood, citizenship, sovereignty, consent or market-eligibility assertion.

For space and extraterrestrial contexts, BUNK must distinguish object, resource, operational control, jurisdiction, treaty status and property rights. It must not infer terrestrial-style ownership where applicable law does not support it.

## 10. Trust and governance invariants

1. Property records are distinct from listings.
2. Evidence is distinct from verification.
3. Verification is distinct from public disclosure.
4. AI does not grant SEAL or make final high-impact authority decisions.
5. Important claims retain ROOT lineage.
6. Private information stays inside the applicable VAULT boundary.
7. Public ATLAS projection requires valid human authority.
8. Financial records remain append-only after posting.
9. Regulated property, finance and tokenization routes require jurisdictional eligibility and policy controls.
10. Product automation must honor authority, consent, privacy, security, risk and recovery controls.

## 11. Product north star

**Verified successful property outcomes.**

Success is not merely a listing, transaction or capital event; it is a trustworthy, measurable property outcome across the relevant lifecycle.
