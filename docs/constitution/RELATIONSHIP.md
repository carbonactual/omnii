# RELATIONSHIP

## Purpose
Relationship is the constitutional kernel that gives explicit semantic form to connections between every constitutional object: Beings, Identities, Knowledge, Motions, Values, Trust states, agents, organizations, resources, services, and systems.

## Canonical Objects
- Relationship
- Connection
- Association
- Membership
- Commitment
- Contract
- Ownership
- Delegation
- Trust relationship
- Dependency
- Communication channel
- Network

## Invariants
1. A relationship has a source, target, type, authority, and lifecycle.
2. Relationships do not erase the identity or authority boundary of participating objects.
3. Every consequential relationship is attributable and auditable.
4. Relationship type determines permitted semantics and operations.
5. Relationship termination must preserve required historical provenance.

## Inputs
Object identities, relationship type, authority, intent, contextual evidence, constraints, and effective-time information.

## Outputs
A typed relationship, relationship state, permissions or obligations derived from that relationship, and auditable provenance.

## Lifecycle
Proposed → Authorized → Active → Suspended/Constrained → Modified → Terminated → Archived.

## Relationships
Relationship connects Being and Identity to other objects; uses Knowledge and Trust as evidence; participates in Motion; enables Value exchange; and provides the connective substrate for Execution, Capability, Resource, and Composition.

## Dependencies
BEING → IDENTITY → KNOWLEDGE → MOTION → VALUE → TRUST → RELATIONSHIP.

## Composition Rules
Relationships are composable but must remain typed. Higher-level products may compose relationships but may not redefine constitutional relationship semantics.

## ABBA
ABBA may discover, evaluate, coordinate, propose, establish, monitor, or terminate relationships only within delegated authority. ABBA must preserve relationship provenance and escalate authority-sensitive decisions.
