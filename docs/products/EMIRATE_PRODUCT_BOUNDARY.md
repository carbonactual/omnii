# EMIRATE Product Boundary

**Status: CANONICAL PRODUCT COMPOSITION**

## Purpose

**EMIRATE** is a Carbon Actual ecosystem product for representing, operating, coordinating, preserving, and developing an emirate or comparable governed cultural-territorial institution.

EMIRATE is **not** a new constitutional architecture and does not create a competing ontology, graph, identity system, authority system, registry foundation, economic foundation, or intelligence foundation.

It is a composition of Carbon Actual/OMNII capabilities.

## Naming

The canonical product name is **EMIRATE**.

**Emirati** is not the product name; it denotes a person/adjective and is therefore not used as the canonical product identity.

**Kingdom**, **Royal**, and **Royalty** are not canonical substitutes because they imply different institutional/constitutional scopes.

A deployment may be named for its local institution, for example **Daura Emirate**.

## Architectural position

```text
CARBON ACTUAL
    ↓
OMNII constitutional + universal substrate
    ↓
shared composition/runtime capabilities
    ↓
EMIRATE product composition
    ↓
local institutional configuration
    ↓
Daura / other emirate deployments
```

The product must consume universal capabilities rather than duplicate them.

## Composed capabilities

EMIRATE may compose, among others:

- identity and human connection;
- authority, consent, governance and protocol;
- people, families, institutions and territories;
- registries and provenance;
- events, situations, forms, workflows and cases;
- time, calendars, schedules and recurrence;
- hospitality and accommodation;
- marketplaces, trade fairs and sponsorship;
- heritage and cultural knowledge;
- mobility and transport;
- horse/equestrian participation and related assets;
- security, health and emergency coordination;
- media, broadcasting and documentation;
- youth participation and skills;
- value, transactions, commitments and reporting;
- ABBA/intelligence and governed agents where authorized;
- Pulse, evidence, learning and continuity.

## Cross-product composition

EMIRATE does not absorb other Carbon Actual products. It composes them through governed contracts.

Examples:

- **Charter** provides transport/movement capabilities.
- **Hitch** provides an opportunistic/shared-movement composition where permitted and safe.
- **NAB** provides applicable vehicle/person/transport registry capabilities.
- **HAPI** provides human connection and participation capabilities.
- Marketplace capabilities provide buying, selling, renting, leasing and reservation.
- Other Carbon Actual products remain independently bounded and reusable.

The rule is:

> A product may use another product's capability; it must not copy or redefine that capability merely to serve its own domain.

## Universal movement composition

EMIRATE can invoke movement capabilities for people, goods, animals, equipment and specialized or delicate cargo.

A movement composition may connect:

`need → person/cargo/animal → route → vehicle/capacity → operator → permissions → handling requirements → host/destination → value → execution → evidence`

This supports cars, buses, motorcycles, large trucks, trains, aircraft, boats, animal transport, specialized freight, convoy movements and other authorized modes without creating a separate transport ontology inside EMIRATE.

Hitchhiking is represented as a movement composition with its own eligibility, safety, consent, route, capacity and authority constraints. It is not a special exception to the universal movement model.

## Event and situation neutrality

EMIRATE is not limited to festivals.

The same composition machinery must support, according to applicable local rules:

- Eid and Sallah activities;
- Maulud and durbar activities;
- weddings and marriage processes;
- funerals and condolence activities;
- trade fairs and cultural markets;
- councils and official gatherings;
- family homecomings and spontaneous visits;
- ordinary community activities;
- incidents, emergencies and exceptional situations.

Each deployment is a local configuration of people, authority, rules, protocol, time, resources, workflows, evidence, value and continuity.

## Data and forms

EMIRATE may define local schemas and forms for registration, applications, invitations, participation, hosting, accommodation, vehicles, horses, vendors, vendors' products, incidents, approvals, inspections, reports, receipts and other processes.

These are product/domain compositions and must use the universal schema/form and workflow capabilities rather than introducing product-specific foundational semantics.

## Governance boundary

Local institutional authority remains authoritative within its lawful/legitimate scope.

OMNII provides the universal substrate and contracts. EMIRATE does not manufacture authority merely through software.

ABBA, agents, automation, marketplace matching, analytics and recommendation must remain subordinate to explicit authority, policy, consent and jurisdictional constraints.

## Anti-silo rule

The EMIRATE codebase and product design must be reviewed for accidental duplication of Carbon Actual/OMNII capabilities.

When a capability is genuinely universal, strengthen the shared ecosystem once and let EMIRATE consume it.

When a capability is local to an emirate, encode it as a product/domain composition without promoting it to a universal constitutional primitive.

## Completion principle

EMIRATE is successful when the system can represent and govern the emirate as it actually exists—planned and spontaneous, ceremonial and ordinary, local and connected, peaceful and exceptional—while preserving authority, provenance, privacy, evidence, value, continuity and interoperability with the rest of Carbon Actual.
