# IDENTITY

**Status: CANONICAL CONSTITUTIONAL KERNEL**

Identity expresses Being through distinguishability, continuity, attributes, names, identifiers and representation.

## Boundaries

```text
Identity ≠ Name
Identity ≠ Address
Identity ≠ Namespace
Identity ≠ Authentication
Identity ≠ Authority
Identity ≠ Ownership
Identity ≠ Custody
Representation ≠ Reality
```

An identity may have many names, identifiers, addresses, credentials, representations and namespace bindings.

## Participants

Identity is species-neutral and open-world. A governed identity may represent a human, organization, animal, plant/fungal/microbial participant where applicable, AI, machine, robot, collective, institution, civilization, extraterrestrial, unknown entity, hypothetical entity, or future entity class.

Identity MUST preserve uncertainty where classification is provisional.

## Names and namespaces

Names are resolvable handles and may originate in DNS, ENS, Handshake/HNS, DIDs, enterprise namespaces, local namespaces or future naming systems. A resolution relationship does not by itself establish ownership, legal identity or authority.

## Continuity

Identity supports aliases, merges, splits, succession, delegation, representation, lifecycle and multiple embodiments without erasing provenance.

## Evidence

Identity assertions may be observed, claimed, inferred, verified, disputed or superseded. Every consequential identity assertion should preserve provenance, confidence and applicable time context.
