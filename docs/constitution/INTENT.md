# INTENT

## Purpose
Intent governs purpose, desired outcomes, decisions, priorities, constraints, and authorized direction so that action has a traceable reason and end state.

## Canonical Objects
- Intent
- Goal
- Objective
- Preference
- Constraint
- Decision
- Priority
- Desired outcome
- Authorization

## Invariants
1. Intent is attributable to an identity or legitimate system context.
2. Intent does not itself grant authority beyond the authority of its source.
3. Material intent changes are versioned and auditable.
4. Execution must be traceable to explicit or legitimately inferred intent or an authorized trigger.
5. Ambiguous or conflicting intent requires clarification, policy resolution, or escalation.

## Inputs
Identity, context, goals, preferences, constraints, evidence, prior commitments, governance rules, and authorization.

## Outputs
A typed intent, prioritized objectives, constraints, decision context, authorization request, or escalation.

## Lifecycle
Proposed → Interpreted → Authorized → Active → Modified/Superseded → Fulfilled/Abandoned → Archived.

## Relationships
Intent originates with Being/Identity, uses Knowledge and Trust, directs Motion and Execution, influences Value, establishes or modifies Relationships, and constrains Composition.

## Dependencies
BEING → IDENTITY → KNOWLEDGE → TRUST → INTENT → EXECUTION; GOVERNANCE constrains authority and conflict resolution.

## Composition Rules
Multiple intents may compose when compatible. Conflicting intents must remain distinguishable until resolved by authority, policy, negotiation, or explicit prioritization.

## ABBA
ABBA may infer, clarify, decompose, prioritize, reconcile, and route intent within delegated authority. It must preserve the distinction between user/system intent and ABBA's own planning or optimization choices.
