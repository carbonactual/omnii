# INTENT

Intent is the constitutional kernel governing purpose, desired outcomes, decisions, priorities, and authorized direction.

## Purpose
Give action and composition a traceable reason and desired end state.

## Canonical Concerns
- Goals
- Preferences
- Constraints
- Decisions
- Priorities
- Authorization
- Desired outcomes

## Invariant
Execution must be traceable to an explicit or legitimately inferred intent within its authority boundary.
