# ECONOMIC COMPOSITION

**Status: CANONICAL CONSTITUTIONAL CONTRACT**

## Purpose

This contract defines how OMNII represents fractionalization, decimalization, democratization, tokenization, minting and blockchain without confusing them with value, ownership, authority or the constitutional substrate.

## Core Distinctions

- **Fractionalization** = selecting or allocating a fraction of a larger capability, resource, entitlement, capacity or participation space.
- **Decimalization** = representing the selected quantity, level, intensity, precision or resolution at the granularity supported by the underlying domain.
- **Democratization** = governing equitable, accessible and contestable distribution of capabilities and opportunities; it is not synonymous with equal ownership or equal consumption.
- **Tokenization** = representing an underlying governed object, right, claim, entitlement, allocation, receipt, state or value exposure in a transferable or addressable digital form.
- **Minting** = controlled creation or issuance of a token or digital representation under explicit issuance authority, supply rules, provenance and lifecycle constraints.
- **Blockchain** = an optional external or internal distributed-ledger settlement/provenance infrastructure. Blockchain is not required for tokenization and tokenization does not imply blockchain.

## Constitutional Boundaries

`Value ≠ Token`

`Token ≠ Ownership`

`Token ≠ Authority`

`Fraction ≠ Decimal`

`Democratization ≠ Equalization`

`Minting ≠ Value Creation`

`Blockchain ≠ Truth`

`Blockchain ≠ Governance`

A token may represent an economic relationship, but the represented right remains defined by the underlying constitutional object, authority, contract, jurisdiction and provenance.

## Fractionalization

A fractionalized participation is a bounded share of an underlying governed object or capability.

The fraction must state:

- denominator or total reference capacity;
- numerator or allocated amount;
- scope of use;
- time/context validity;
- whether the fraction is consumptive, representative, ownership-bearing, voting-bearing or access-bearing;
- transfer/reassignment rules.

Fractions may apply to capacity, use, access, ownership, revenue, attention, resource supply, storage, compute, movement, time, or other constitutionally representable quantities.

## Decimalization

Decimalization specifies how precisely the fraction or capability is represented.

A capability may expose levels, tiers, intensities, qualities, resolutions or continuous quantities. Decimalization must preserve the domain's measurement semantics and must not fabricate precision unsupported by evidence or the underlying measurement system.

## Democratization

The ecosystem may make capabilities available through shared access rather than requiring each participant to own the underlying resource.

Democratization must preserve:

- identity and eligibility;
- access rules;
- scarcity conditions;
- floor protections;
- safe-haven reserves;
- priority/exception rules;
- consent and authority;
- auditability and redress.

## Tokenization

Tokenization creates a governed representation linked to an underlying object or state. The token must preserve provenance and the exact semantics of what it represents.

Examples include representations of:

- use rights;
- ownership interests;
- receipts;
- claims;
- access entitlements;
- fractional allocations;
- credentials;
- provenance proofs;
- tickets;
- settlement claims.

The token registry is never allowed to silently redefine the underlying object.

## Minting

Minting requires:

```text
issuer identity
issuance authority
issuance policy
supply/quantity
underlying reference
provenance
conditions
lifecycle
reconciliation path
```

Minting must support idempotency, rejection, revocation, burn/retirement where applicable, correction and audit.

Minting cannot manufacture value merely by increasing token supply.

## Blockchain / Distributed Ledger

Blockchain may be selected when the use case benefits from one or more of:

- shared settlement;
- externally verifiable ordering;
- multi-party state commitment;
- independent verification;
- programmable transfer rules;
- cross-organizational coordination.

Blockchain remains an infrastructure choice. Off-chain databases, signed records, append-only logs, Merkle structures, conventional settlement rails or other mechanisms may be constitutionally valid where they satisfy the required trust and provenance guarantees.

## Asset / Liability Inversion

For a defined period and context:

`Value Given = aggregate of independently valued resource/value dimensions actually committed or consumed.`

`Pulse Returned = aggregate of independently valued realized feedback/outcomes.`

The ecosystem classification rule is:

```text
Pulse Returned > Value Given  → LIABILITY
Pulse Returned < Value Given  → ASSET
Pulse Returned = Value Given  → BALANCED
```

Asset/liability is therefore a dynamic contextual state, not an eternal property of the object.

## Ecosystem Value

Individual value vectors aggregate into group, territory, network and ecosystem vectors while preserving source-level dimensions.

OMNII may derive:

- individual vector;
- group/community vector;
- territory/network vector;
- general ecosystem vector;
- average capability benchmark;
- constitutional floor;
- protected Safe Haven reserve.

The **Floor** is the protected minimum capability/value condition required by applicable governance.

The **Average** is the contextual normal/benchmark capability available across a defined population or capacity base.

The **Safe Haven** is protected reserve capacity that should not be consumed merely to inflate short-term averages or outputs.

## No Loss / No Silent Dilution

Economic compositions must preserve:

- original quantity;
- fractional allocations;
- decimal precision;
- token supply changes;
- ownership/custody changes;
- transfers;
- redemptions;
- burns/retirements;
- reversals;
- expiries;
- fees and resource consumption;
- expected and actual outcomes.

Every transformation must be traceable from source value/resources to resulting representations and back.

## Open World

The economic grammar is open-ended. New forms of value, entitlement, fractional use, token representation, settlement, minting and distributed coordination may be introduced without redefining existing constitutional semantics.
