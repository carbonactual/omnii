# OMNII Open-World Constitutional Audit — 2026-08-20

## Scope

Audit target: canonical OMNII architecture and the newly added open-world/meta-ontology, naming/resolution, multi-species, epistemic, movement and economic-composition layers.

## Audit result

**Direction: coherent, substantially strengthened, not yet runtime-verified.**

The architecture now has a consistent mechanism for representing unknowns and extending ontology without turning products, namespaces, tokens or external standards into constitutional authority.

## Strengths confirmed

- Canonical object identity and provenance are explicit.
- Authority is separated from capability, intelligence, orchestration and planning.
- Open-world extensions can begin unknown/provisional and evolve through evidence and governance.
- Namespaces and resolution are separated from identity and authority.
- Multiple mappings/classifications can coexist without silently overwriting one another.
- Multi-species, artificial, collective and unknown participants are supported without a human-only substrate.
- Evidence, claims, hypotheses, verification, disputes and supersession remain distinct.
- Economic value remains multidimensional and separate from money, price, cost, ownership and token representation.
- Fractionalization, decimalization, democratization, tokenization, minting and blockchain are distinct.
- Products remain compositions of capabilities rather than private constitutional systems.
- Historical semantics are preserved through versioning/supersession rather than silent rewriting.
