# CHARTER — Transport Product Handoff Catalog

Charter is the source movement layer. Products are extracted by composing bounded workflows over it.

## Handoff catalog

- **Ride / Door-to-Door:** passenger intent + HAPI operator + Fleet asset + Charter journey.
- **Charter:** premium/exclusive movement + specialist assets + qualified HAPI personnel + Charter execution.
- **Fleet:** organizational asset operations over Charter capabilities.
- **Logistico:** goods movement and custody workflow over Charter journeys.
- **Hitch:** hitchhiking discovery/matching experience over Charter movement availability.
- **Pilgrim:** pilgrimage journey experience over Charter routes, stops and services.
- **NAB:** automobile registry/biography and government handoff referencing canonical vehicle identity.
- **Recovery:** breakdown/tow/rescue workflow over Charter capabilities and HAPI specialists.
- **Public/Institutional Transport:** government, enterprise and public movement compositions.
- **Specialist Movement:** medical, industrial, agricultural, construction, marine, aviation and other regulated movement contexts.

## Handoff rule

Each product receives canonical references and contracts. It does not copy Charter's capability registry, journey model, identity model or execution primitives.

## Future expansion

New products and modes can be added through capability registration and composition rather than modifying the constitutional substrate.
