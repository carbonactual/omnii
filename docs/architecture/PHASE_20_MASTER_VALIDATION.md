## Gates
1. Every layer traces to constitutional contracts.
2. Infrastructure implements rather than redefines runtime semantics.
3. APIs and SDKs preserve universal identity, authority, provenance, and policy.
4. Agents and ABBA remain constitutionally bounded.
5. Knowledge and memory distinguish evidence from inference.
6. Economic, human, organizational, product, and territorial models use the same substrate.
7. Ecosystem composition preserves interoperability and autonomy.
8. Governance, security, audit, observability, compliance, and resilience span the complete stack.
9. Constitutional change remains governed and versioned.
10. End-to-end scenarios can trace intent through execution, outcome, value, continuity, and accountability.

## Final Exit Criterion
OMNII reaches Phase 20 only when the architecture can represent, execute, govern, observe, and compose people, agents, organizations, products, services, economies, communities, institutions, and territories without creating incompatible parallel operating systems.
