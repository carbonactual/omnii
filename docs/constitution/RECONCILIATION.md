# RECONCILIATION

**Status: CANONICAL CROSS-CUTTING CONSTITUTIONAL CONTRACT**

RECONCILIATION is the mechanism for detecting and accounting for divergence between expected and actual state, value, resource or outcome.

## Invariants
- No-silent-loss is required; physical loss cannot be made impossible by software.
- Consequential movements have attributable expected and actual outcomes where measurable.
- A mismatch creates an explicit exception.
- Recovery creates an explicit recovery record.
- Reconciliation does not overwrite the original state or evidence.
- Money reconciliation and broader value reconciliation remain distinct.
- Pulse may consume reconciliation feedback but does not erase the underlying ledger/evidence.
