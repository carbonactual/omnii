# OMNII Constitutional Becoming

**Status:** Constitutional Doctrine  
**Layer:** Becoming / Transformation  
**Canonical authority:** OMNII Canonical Architecture

## 1. Purpose

OMNII Constitutional Becoming defines how entities, relationships, capabilities, territories, organizations, and ecosystems transform through time while preserving sufficient continuity to understand what changes and what remains.

## 2. Core Definition

> **Becoming is the constitutionally coherent transformation of an entity or ecosystem from one state, capability, relationship, or condition toward another through time.**

Becoming is not merely change. It is change with identity, context, trajectory, constraints, and consequences.

## 3. Becoming Invariants

### 3.1 Continuity Invariant

A transformation must remain connected to the prior state where continuity is constitutionally material.

### 3.2 Direction Invariant

Where a trajectory or intended outcome exists, its direction must be distinguishable from incidental change.

### 3.3 Agency Invariant

Where an entity has legitimate agency over a transformation, that agency should remain distinguishable from external forces.

### 3.4 Constraint Invariant

Material constraints, dependencies, authority boundaries, and consent requirements must remain visible.

### 3.5 Capability Invariant

Changes in capability must remain distinguishable from changes in identity or authority.

### 3.6 Provenance Invariant

Material transformations must retain sufficient provenance to understand how the resulting state emerged.

### 3.7 Reversibility Invariant

Where reversal is possible, the system should distinguish reversible, irreversible, and uncertain transformations.

### 3.8 Uncertainty Invariant

Future states must not be represented as certain merely because they are intended, predicted, or desired.

## 4. Becoming Model

```text
Current Being
    ↓
Context / Intent / Constraint
    ↓
Transition
    ↓
New State
    ↓
Evidence / Feedback
    ↓
Updated Becoming Trajectory
```

The trajectory may branch rather than follow a single predetermined path.

## 5. Becoming Dimensions

Becoming may occur through:

- capability development;
- learning;
- economic transformation;
- organizational change;
- identity development;
- relationship formation;
- territorial change;
- ecological succession;
- technological evolution;
- institutional transformation;
- cultural change;
- lifecycle progression.

## 6. Becoming and Human Development

The ecosystem should treat human development as becoming rather than merely as a static profile.

A person may have:

```text
Current state
+ capabilities
+ aspirations
+ relationships
+ constraints
+ opportunities
+ trajectory
```

The ecosystem must not reduce the person's future to an algorithmic prediction.

## 7. Becoming and Economy

Economic becoming includes changes in:

- productive capability;
- resources;
- value;
- obligations;
- enterprise state;
- employment;
- investment;
- access.

Economic trajectory must remain distinguishable from guaranteed outcome.

## 8. Becoming and Intelligence

Intelligence assists becoming by sensing, learning, reasoning, forecasting, planning, and coordinating.

Intelligence does not own another entity's becoming merely because it can predict or recommend a path.

## 9. Becoming and Governance

Governance can coordinate collective transformation, but governance must remain within legitimate authority and consent boundaries.

## 10. Becoming and ABBA

ABBA may maintain becoming trajectories, detect transitions, coordinate workflows, identify constraints, and propose next actions.

ABBA must distinguish:

- observed state;
- inferred state;
- intended state;
- predicted state;
- authorized state transition.

These categories must not be silently conflated.

## 11. Becoming and OMNI

OMNI is the omnidirectional movement state through which an ecosystem can move among states and possibilities.

```text
OMNII = canonical architecture
Being  = present constitutional condition
Becoming = transformation
OMNI   = omnidirectional movement state
```

OMNI does not prescribe one destination. Becoming can branch, adapt, and emerge.

## 12. Failure Conditions

Becoming failure includes:

- treating predictions as facts;
- treating intentions as completed states;
- erasing prior state provenance;
- ignoring constraints;
- confusing capability with authority;
- forcing one trajectory where multiple legitimate trajectories exist;
- allowing automation to determine a person's becoming without legitimate authority or consent;
- treating optimization as the sole definition of development.

## 13. Constitutional Dependencies

Becoming depends on:

- Being
- Continuity
- Intelligence
- Relationship
- Economics
- Governance where collective coordination applies

Becoming provides the foundation for Civilization.

## 14. Final Principle

> **Becoming is not merely moving from what is to what will be; it is the accountable transformation between states while preserving the meaning of the journey.**
