# BUNK Property Intelligence

BUNK's intelligence layer turns property records into an evidence-backed, continuously updateable property picture without confusing inference with authority.

## Intelligence domains

- Location and geography
- Parcel/cadastral context
- Title, tenure and rights
- Evidence and provenance
- Physical condition and building systems
- Development potential, zoning and approvals
- Market supply, demand and comparables
- Financial performance, costs, yields and financing
- Environmental, climate, water, biodiversity and carbon context
- Operations, occupancy and maintenance
- Participants, capabilities and reputation
- Insurance, legal, safety and operational risk
- Lifecycle state, history and forecasts

## Observation contract

Material intelligence should retain the property reference, dimension, claim/observation, source/evidence reference, observation time, confidence and, when relevant, authority context and validity period.

## Separation rules

Intelligence can recommend a workflow, trigger review, identify anomalies or support valuation and matching. It cannot by itself establish title, ownership, consent, SEAL, authority, public disclosure, regulated eligibility or marketability.

## Sources of truth

Canonical OMNII objects, authoritative registries and validated evidence remain the source-of-truth boundaries. BUNK intelligence is a product projection/composition unless explicitly designated otherwise.

## ABBA and agents

ABBA and authorized agents may analyze BUNK intelligence, orchestrate approved workflows and surface opportunities. They remain subject to OMNII authority, consent, privacy, risk, jurisdiction and human-decision controls.
