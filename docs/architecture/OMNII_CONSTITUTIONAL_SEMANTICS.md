# OMNII Constitutional Semantics v1.0

Status: Constitutional Doctrine

Core principles:
- Existence implies participation.
- Existence implies relationship.
- Existence implies responsibility.
- Existence implies return.
- Existence implies consequence.
- Existence implies evidence.
- Existence implies becoming.

Universal Participation Contract:
Identity, Nature, Kind, Role, Purpose, Relationships, Capabilities, Resources, Authority, Boundaries, Responsibilities, Returns, Dependencies, Condition, Evidence, Lifecycle and Becoming.

Universal Questions:
- Who am I?
- What am I?
- Why do I exist?
- What may I do?
- What must I return?

Constitutional Invariants:
1. Everything that exists participates.
2. Nothing exists in complete isolation.
3. Participation creates relationship.
4. Relationship creates responsibility.
5. Responsibility exists before authority.
6. Capability does not imply permission.
7. Authority does not eliminate accountability.
8. Ownership does not imply stewardship.
9. Stewardship does not imply ownership.
10. Every consequential action produces evidence.
11. Every consequence contributes to becoming.
12. Interdependence is a constitutional reality.
13. Continuity matters.
14. Learning matters.
15. Becoming is continuous.

Highest-level definition:
OMNII Constitutional Semantics defines the meaning of participation, responsibility, contribution, consequence, evidence, continuity and becoming across physical, digital, biological, social and institutional reality.
