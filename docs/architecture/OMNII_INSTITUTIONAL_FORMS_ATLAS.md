# OMNII Institutional Forms Atlas

**Status: CANONICAL REUSABLE FORMS CATALOG**

Every institution-facing form is an executable interaction contract, not merely a UI page. Forms bind identity, authority, purpose, requirements, evidence, validation, workflow, decisions, payments where applicable, notifications, audit, lifecycle, appeal and continuity.

## Universal form families

- sign-up, invitation, onboarding, identity verification, recovery, consent, delegation
- organization/institution registration, profile, branch/unit creation, role assignment
- applications, registrations, expressions of interest, requests, declarations, nominations
- licences, permits, certifications, accreditation, renewal, suspension, revocation
- complaints, petitions, incidents, whistleblowing, disputes, appeals, reviews
- investigation intake, evidence submission, witness/statement, referral and disposition
- recruitment, vacancy, screening, interview, appointment, onboarding and separation
- promotion, transfer, posting, deployment, duty, leave and performance review
- training enrolment, attendance, assessment, credential issuance and renewal
- procurement requirement, RFQ/RFP, bid, evaluation, approval, award, acceptance and closeout
- contract creation, amendment, milestone, notice, breach, renewal and termination
- invoice, payment request, refund, grant, budget request, expense and reconciliation
- subscription, membership, dues, sponsorship, donation and cancellation
- booking, reservation, scheduling, allocation, waitlist, cancellation and no-show
- inspection, test, sampling, finding, corrective action, reinspection and certification
- asset registration, custody transfer, inventory movement, maintenance, disposal
- project charter, milestone update, change request, risk/issue and closure
- service request, status update, feedback, escalation and resolution
- election candidate, party, membership, polling agent, observer, material, result and dispute forms
- school admission, learner profile, guardian, attendance, assessment, examination, scholarship, transfer
- health appointment, referral, laboratory, treatment, discharge, insurance/claim and public-health reporting
- agriculture producer, variety, field, seed lot, inspection, certification, traceability and complaint
- digital infrastructure domain/subdomain, application, API, deployment, access, change, incident, finding and recovery

## Form execution contract

`draft → capture → validate → evidence → submit → acknowledge → route → review → decision → issue → renew/appeal/archive`

## Controls

Every consequential form should declare version, owner, authority, required evidence, data classification, retention, validation rules, payment rules, workflow, SLA, notification policy, audit requirements and migration strategy.

Forms support conditional fields, calculations, attachments, signatures, multilingual access, accessibility, offline capture, assisted submission, idempotency and duplicate detection.

## Reuse rule

Forms should reuse common field types, identity references, evidence types, policy rules and workflow templates. Institution-specific differences belong in configuration or domain packs.
