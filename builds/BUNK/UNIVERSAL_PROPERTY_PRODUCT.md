## Boundary

BUNK owns property-domain experience and workflows. OMNII owns shared identity, authority, graph, evidence, persistence, events, agents and governance. TIP owns canonical trade, investment, financing, market and settlement semantics.

This file is an index; it does not duplicate executable substrate or create a second property/economic ontology.
