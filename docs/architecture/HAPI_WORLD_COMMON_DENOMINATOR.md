# HAPI World — Common Denominator Architecture

**Status:** Canonical common-layer architecture
**Date:** 2026-09-05

## Purpose

HAPI World is the reusable participation environment for Carbon Actual. It is the common denominator shared by products that involve humans, AI, learning, work, interaction, capability discovery, progression, identity-linked relationships, value exchange, and world-facing representation.

It sits above the OMNII constitutional substrate and below specialized product experiences:

`OMNII Constitutional Substrate → Shared Capabilities → HAPI World → Domain/Product Context → Institution → Integration → Presentation`

## What HAPI World unifies

HAPI World provides one participation language for:

- human/HAPI identity and continuity;
- human ↔ personal AI relationships;
- AI/agent entities and their governed roles;
- learning, skills, credentials, practice, assessment and progression;
- work, service, contribution and opportunity;
- discovery, matching, context and availability;
- community, institution, marketplace and world interactions;
- relationships, reputation, evidence and history;
- value participation, exchange and settlement references;
- Atlas/world-facing representation and curated context.

The common denominator is not a single UI. It is a governed participation model.

A HAPI World participation record SHOULD contain:


The exact persistence representation remains implementation-specific; the semantics remain shared.

## Identity law

A human entering another Carbon Actual product is still the same human/HAPI participant. Product-specific accounts may exist as projections, credentials or institutional records, but they must not silently create a second constitutional identity.

A personal or organizational AI may be linked to the human/entity relationship route. The AI is not the human and the human does not become the AI.

## Crossings

Products are bounded contexts crossed through governed relationships. A crossing may be:

`discover → relate → consent/authority → enter context → interact → produce evidence/outcome → leave/update state`

A crossing does not automatically grant authority, ownership, membership, credential validity, employment, financial rights or legal standing.

## Learning and capability

HAPI World treats learning and skills as a continuous development surface:

`interest → learning → practice → assessment → credential → supervised application → continuing development`

A credential is evidence of achievement or qualification; it is not by itself constitutional or legal authority.

## Work and opportunity

Opportunity is modeled through intent, capability, context, availability, match, authority and outcome. A match is a discovery result; it is not authorization or a binding contract.

## Value and economics

HAPI World may expose value participation and economic context but does not replace OMNII economic/ledger semantics. Value, tokenization, rights, ownership, settlement and external legal/accounting status remain distinct.

## Atlas and world representation

HAPI World may project curated Atlas views of participants, places, communities, products and relationships. Atlas is a representation/discovery surface, never the canonical operational source of truth.

## Authority boundary

HAPI World never becomes an implicit authority issuer. It consumes OMNII authority and policy decisions.

`constitutional/human/legal authority → institutional delegation → product context → HAPI World participation → capability invocation`

ABBA can interpret and orchestrate within valid authority but cannot mint constitutional authority through HAPI World.

## HAPI World ↔ product doctrine

Every applicable product should expose a HAPI World crossing rather than independently rebuilding:

- identity;
- relationship entry;
- participant context;
- capability discovery;
- learning/progression;
- evidence/provenance;
- shared world navigation.

The product keeps its domain-specialized behavior.

Examples:

- RITES/NAIRE adds continuity/life-domain behavior.
- NGIN adds organizational/institutional behavior.
- NASC adds institutional forms, registries and workflows.
- NOUN Student Bot adds bounded institutional learning support.
- Cultural Atlas adds cultural knowledge and representation.
- Charter adds movement/fleet/logistics.
- Banking products add finance-specific authorization, transactions and settlement.
- Media products add content, rights, royalties and discovery.

## Non-goals

HAPI World is not:

- a replacement for the OMNII constitution;
- a legal identity registry by itself;
- a universal authority issuer;
- a duplicate ledger;
- a provider-specific AI framework;
- a mandatory identical visual shell for every product.

## Tests

**Common-denominator test:** Can the participant move between applicable products without creating duplicate identity semantics or losing provenance?

**Boundary test:** Can a product demonstrate exactly which authority and capability it consumed from OMNII?

**Continuity test:** Does participation update the shared participant relationship/history without erasing the product-specific domain state?

**Thriving test:** Does the environment help the being learn, work, connect, navigate and become within its real habitat without making the habitat decorative?
