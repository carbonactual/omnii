# OMNII Universal Institutional Fabric 2026

Status: Canonical Draft

Purpose: Define reusable institutional infrastructure for governments, regulators, schools, universities, clubs, NGOs, companies, security organizations and civic institutions.

Core chain:
Record -> Form -> Validation -> Case -> Workflow -> Task -> Authority -> Automation -> Execution -> Evidence -> Audit -> Finance -> Reporting -> Learning.

Reusable engines:
- Identity and Authority
- Sign-up and Onboarding
- Registries
- Forms and Schemas
- Documents and Evidence
- Cases and Workflows
- Tasks and SLA
- Rules and Policy
- Automation
- Approvals and Decisions
- Deployments and Promotions
- Procurement and Contracts
- Payments, Finance and Settlement
- Compliance and Audit
- Opportunity and Grants
- Knowledge and Search
- Analytics, Pulse and Value
- Continuity and Handoff
- Integrations and Interoperability

Institutional compositions:
Government, Agencies, Regulators, Elections, Education, Health, Agriculture, Justice, Security, NGOs, Cooperatives, Enterprises, Research and Community organizations.

Gap detection:
Duplicate identities, missing approvals, orphan records, stale workflows, unexplained financial movements, expired authority, segregation-of-duty violations, reconciliation failures, missing evidence and audit gaps.

Strategic principle:
Build once. Reuse everywhere. Configure locally. Govern explicitly. Handoff cleanly.
