# InstituteGPT — Learning, Skills, Certification & Advancement Services

**Status: CANONICAL DESIGN — 2026-09-06**

## 1. Product boundary

`InstituteGPT` is the first-class education GPT and owns the universal learning experience. There is no separate `Advance` product.

InstituteGPT owns learning, courses, programmes, learning paths, skills, competencies, practice, laboratories, projects, CPD, professional learning, badges, certificates, credential pathways and lifelong advancement.

OpenEd Bot, Notebook and Exams are first-class compositions/products within the education ecosystem and interoperate with InstituteGPT. Other education functions remain reusable capabilities inside the InstituteGPT education layer.

## 2. Curation at application and onboarding

Application and onboarding are not only account creation. InstituteGPT builds a governed learner context and continuously curates learning around the learner.

Curation may use:

`interests | goals | education stage | prior knowledge | skills | experience | career direction | subjects | preferred formats | pace | available time | language | accessibility | device/connectivity | location context | budget | provider preferences | credential goals | professional-body requirements | CPD requirements | learning-style preferences | support preferences | lab/project preferences | assessment preferences`

The learner can revise these preferences at any time. Curation is adaptive, explainable and reversible. Preferences are not a license to make hidden high-impact decisions.

For children, guardian/educator controls, age suitability, safeguarding, privacy and retention rules apply. Childhood exploration must not silently become an adult employment profile.

## 3. Universal learning value chain

`discover → apply/onboard → understand learner context → diagnose → advise → curate → set goal → build path → select learning experience → learn → practice → lab/simulation → project → experience → demonstrate → assess → recognize → badge/certificate/credential → portfolio/record → opportunity → work/practice → CPD → renew/recertify → reskill/upskill → lifelong learning`

This is a graph: branches, loops, stacking, recognition of prior learning and direct entry are supported.

## 4. Learning experiences

The universal learning unit is a **Learning Experience**, which can contain:

`lesson | reading | media | AI dialogue | teacher instruction | tutoring | exercise | quiz | discussion | lab | simulation | project | fieldwork | mentorship | apprenticeship | internship | assessment`

Courses and programmes are compositions of learning experiences rather than the only possible form of learning.

## 5. Labs

Labs are a major InstituteGPT capability connecting knowledge to demonstrated capability.

They may be:

`coding | cloud | AI | data | cybersecurity | science | engineering | robotics | electronics | medicine | agriculture | manufacturing | finance | GIS | media | design | language | vocational | field | simulation | VR/AR | digital twin | hardware-connected | remote`

Environments may include sandboxes, browser runtimes, notebooks, containers, virtual machines, cloud workspaces, remote hardware, simulation engines and physical facilities.

Lab outputs may become governed evidence, artifacts or assessment signals.

## 6. Skills and competency progression

`knowledge → skill → competency → practice → evidence → demonstrated competency → achievement → qualification → professional authorization`

Skill states may include:

`self_declared | learning | practiced | assessed | demonstrated | externally_verified | expired | superseded`

Learning never automatically creates regulated professional authorization.

## 7. Credentials, badges and certification

InstituteGPT supports:

`participation | completion | open badge | microcredential | certificate | professional certificate | diploma | degree reference | qualification | professional certification | CPD recognition`

InstituteGPT may issue configured ecosystem-native credentials and may reference/verify external credentials. External issuers and regulators retain authoritative control over their credentials and licences.

Portable targets include Open Badges, CLR and W3C Verifiable Credentials, plus applicable national, institutional and professional frameworks.

## 8. Learning Wallet and Skills Passport

**Learning Wallet** provides a portable collection/reference of learning records, achievements, badges, certificates, credentials, assessments, projects and evidence.

**Skills Passport** provides a provenance-aware view of declared, learned, practiced, assessed, demonstrated and externally verified skills.

Neither replaces the authority of an external issuer.

## 9. Pathway Builder

InstituteGPT can construct and revise paths from:

`goal + learner context + diagnostics + prerequisites + competencies + learning resources + labs + projects + assessments + credentials + opportunities + renewal requirements`

Paths may be native, partner, institutional, employer, professional-body, government, learner-authored or AI-generated. AI-generated paths are recommendations where human or legal authority is required.

## 10. Provider ecosystem

InstituteGPT uses replaceable adapters/profiles for:

`GitHub Education | OpenAI Education/Academy | Anthropic Education | Microsoft Learn | AWS Educate | NVIDIA DLI | Google | IBM SkillsBuild | Cisco Networking Academy | Salesforce Trailhead | Coursera | Udemy | Alison | Coursiv | edX | FutureLearn | LinkedIn Learning | Khan Academy | DataCamp | Pluralsight | Codecademy | freeCodeCamp | professional bodies | universities | TVET providers | employer academies | governments | future providers`

Provider integrations may expose catalogs, deep links, progress references, labs, assessments, achievements and credential references according to each provider's actual permissions and interfaces.

## 11. Child-to-lifelong coverage

InstituteGPT supports:

`early childhood | primary | secondary | youth | vocational/technical | tertiary | postgraduate | doctoral | professional entry | workplace | career transition | entrepreneurship | professional development | community learning | lifelong learning | AI/agent learning`

The semantic substrate is shared; governance, content, permissions and safety are age/context dependent.

## 12. Education capabilities kept inside InstituteGPT

These are capabilities, not additional products by default:

`discovery | counselling | admissions | enrollment | curriculum | teaching | tutoring | mentoring | coaching | accessibility | learner support | assessment | marking | moderation | proctoring | invigilation | learner success | wellbeing | safeguarding | research | libraries | internships | apprenticeships | SIWES | work-integrated learning | administration | interoperability | funding | government education | AI governance | marketplace | pathway building | learning wallet | skills passport`

A capability may become a separate first-class product only when it acquires an independently meaningful boundary and ecosystem role.

## 13. First-class InstituteGPT composition

`InstituteGPT = universal education, learning, skills, certification and lifelong advancement GPT`

`OpenEd Bot = InstituteGPT + open/distance/flexible education composition`

`Notebook = InstituteGPT study/research/teaching/evidence workspace`

`Exams = InstituteGPT formal examination/testing product`

The three compositions share the InstituteGPT education layer and do not duplicate or redefine its semantics.

## 14. Opportunity connection

Learning evidence may inform the universal Opportunities layer for jobs, internships, apprenticeships, scholarships, projects, research, contracts, entrepreneurship and promotion. InstituteGPT does not make the external opportunity decision.

## 15. AI learners

AI/agents may learn, practice, simulate, benchmark and receive governed capability state. AI learning is distinct from human qualification and does not automatically grant human or regulated authority.

## 16. Governance

`Human authority remains authoritative.`

`External issuers retain their credential authority.`

`Provider adapters are replaceable.`

`AI recommendations are not automatically authoritative decisions.`

`Simulation and sandbox activity do not silently become real-world Actual evidence.`

`Learner and child data remain subject to authorization, privacy, safeguarding and retention policy.`
