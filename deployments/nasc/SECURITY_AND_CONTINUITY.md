# NASC Security & Continuity

## Controls

Use least privilege, separation of duties, active-authority checks, evidence provenance, audit logging and restricted/public data boundaries. Integration credentials belong to the institution and are never committed to source control.

## Access lifecycle

Create access only for a defined role and scope; review privileged access; expire/revoke access when authority ends; retain audit evidence.

## Data continuity

Maintain institution-approved backups and recovery procedures. Test restoration periodically. Preserve audit/history; do not replace historical records with corrected values without recording the correction event.

## Public verification

Expose only the minimum data needed to verify status. Protected identity, contact, commercial and enforcement information remains governed and non-public unless lawful disclosure applies.

## AI and automation

Automation may route, notify, validate and surface findings. It does not independently confer authority or approve licences, certificates or enforcement actions.
