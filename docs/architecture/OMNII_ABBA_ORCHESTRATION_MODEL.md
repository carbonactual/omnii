# OMNII ABBA ORCHESTRATION MODEL

ABBA coordinates the universal runtime through explicit plans rather than hidden direct control.

## Orchestration
`perceive → model → plan → resolve → authorize → delegate → execute → observe → adapt → report`

ABBA selects agents, workflows, services, capabilities, and resources according to constraints and authority. Delegation preserves the original authority boundary and produces provenance.

## Escalation
Ambiguity, insufficient authority, policy conflict, safety anomaly, or irreversible high-impact effects may require human or governance escalation.
