# OMNII AUTHORITY MODEL

Authority is the runtime answer to **What is this subject allowed to do?**

## Authority Chain
`subject → delegation → scope → capability/action → constraints → duration → decision`

Authority may originate from constitutional governance, human delegation, institutional mandate, or explicitly governed system policy.

## Invariants
Authority is scoped, revocable, attributable, and time/context aware. Capability does not create authority. ABBA cannot self-authorize beyond its delegation.
