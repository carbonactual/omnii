# OMNII Universal Composition & Productization Engine — Canonical Scope

**Status:** CANONICAL CAPABILITY EXTENSION  
**Family:** Composition  
**Scope:** Global / open-world  
**Purpose:** Turn any valid human or machine-originated idea, request, object, signal, product, service, project, institution, workflow or half-formed thought into a governed, executable composition without requiring a new architecture.

## 1. Core Principle

> **No idea is too random to enter the ecosystem.**
>
> The ecosystem interprets the input, preserves uncertainty, discovers reusable capabilities, identifies genuine gaps, composes the result, validates it, and returns an executable next state.

The engine MUST NOT force an unknown idea into an incorrect category merely to achieve classification. `Unknown`, `Emerging`, `Ambiguous`, and `Unclassified` are valid states.

## 2. Universal Intake

Accepted inputs include:

- text, voice, image, video and mixed-media prompts;
- documents, spreadsheets, datasets and URLs;
- sketches, mockups, prototypes and specifications;
- products, services, workflows and institutional concepts;
- observed problems, opportunities, surplus, idle capacity and disruptions;
- physical objects, facilities, machinery and human capabilities;
- existing software, APIs, agents, models and external systems;
- incomplete or contradictory ideas.

Every intake produces an **Intent Object** with provenance, source, context, confidence, ambiguity, requested outcome and authority context.

## 4. Composition Units

The engine composes, rather than hard-coding product architectures, from:

- capabilities and sub-capabilities;
- workflow recipes and reusable steps;
- registries, forms and records;
- agents, skills and model providers;
- human roles and HAPI actions;
- physical resources and machine capacity;
- data, memory and knowledge sources;
- UI/UX templates and generated experience surfaces;
- integrations, APIs, events and external providers;
- policies, standards, evidence and validation rules;
- value, finance, contracts, procurement and exchange capabilities;
- deployment environments and lifecycle controls.

## 6. Gap Discipline

A missing component becomes a **Capability Gap** only after:

1. existing ecosystem capabilities have been searched;
2. external integrations have been considered;
3. configuration, composition or adaptation have been considered;
4. reuse or substitution is insufficient; and
5. the gap is confirmed as reusable or materially necessary.

A product-specific implementation MUST NOT be promoted into the shared fabric solely because one composition is missing an ingredient.

## 7. Reverse Composition

Composition works in both directions.

```text
IDEA → COMPOSITION → PRODUCT / SERVICE / INSTITUTION / WORLD

REALITY → SIGNAL → DISCOVERY → COMPOSITION → NEW VALUE / PRODUCT
```

Examples include existing factories, machinery, unused buildings, human skills, transport capacity, datasets or relationships becoming a new service or enterprise when matched with demand, finance, logistics and governance.

## 8. Productization

Productization converts a validated composition into one or more deployable forms:

- product;
- service;
- project;
- program;
- institution;
- workflow;
- internal tool;
- public experience;
- marketplace/exchange surface;
- physical-world operating model;
- agent or AI service;
- HAPI/HAPI World experience.

One composition may yield multiple products or experiences without duplicating the underlying capability graph.

## 9. Variant and Fork Model

Compositions MUST support:

- versioning;
- branching;
- localization;
- customer/organization variants;
- jurisdiction variants;
- interface variants;
- provider substitutions;
- rollback;
- experiment variants;
- archival and resurrection.

Variants inherit canonical semantics while changing configuration.

## 10. Human Authority

ABBA may interpret, recommend, simulate and orchestrate. It does not silently assume human authority where authority is required.

SEAL remains the mechanism for consent, authorization, attestation or approval where the governance contract requires a human decision.

## 11. Global-by-Default

> **Build globally. Configure locally. Federate jurisdictions. Never make a local implementation the definition of the capability.**

Local law, language, culture, currency, tax, infrastructure, payment rails, accessibility, connectivity and operating norms are configuration and evidence context.

## 12. Completeness Contract

A composition is not complete merely because a screen, document, prototype or code artifact exists.

Completion requires resolution or explicit status for:

- required capabilities;
- data and knowledge dependencies;
- authority and permissions;
- workflows;
- integrations;
- evidence and standards;
- security and safety controls;
- experience states;
- deployment environment;
- monitoring and recovery;
- value/outcome measurement;
- lifecycle ownership.

## 13. Boundaries

This capability is not:

- a replacement for every domain product;
- a single UI framework;
- a single AI model;
- a mandatory cloud provider;
- a marketplace;
- an ERP/CRM replacement;
- a universal programming language.

It is the **composition contract** that lets these things participate without owning the constitution.
