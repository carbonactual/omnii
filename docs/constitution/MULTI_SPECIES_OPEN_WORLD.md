# MULTI-SPECIES & MULTI-INTELLIGENCE OPEN WORLD

**Status: CANONICAL CONSTITUTIONAL CONTRACT**

## Purpose

OMNII is open to participants whose biology, embodiment, cognition, communication, culture, intelligence, environment, timescale and social organization differ from present-day humans.

The constitution MUST NOT encode humanity as the universal default entity model.

## Participant model

A participant may be:

- human;
- animal or other known biological organism;
- plant/fungal/microbial collective where the domain requires representation;
- artificial intelligence;
- software agent;
- robot or autonomous machine;
- collective or colony;
- institution or civilization;
- unknown biological entity;
- unknown artificial entity;
- extraterrestrial or non-terrestrial entity;
- hypothetical or simulated participant;
- future entity class not yet defined.

This is an open-world classification, not a claim that any particular unknown or extraterrestrial entity exists.

## Species-neutral identity

Identity MUST separate:

```text
entity
species/classification
individuality
collective membership
embodiment
substrate
origin
lineage
continuity
controller (if any)
authorized representative (if any)
```

A participant may have multiple identifiers and names across namespaces. A participant may also lack a known name or stable identifier while still being represented as an observed/unknown entity.

## Embodiment

The architecture must represent different forms of embodiment:

```text
biological
mechanical
digital
hybrid
collective
distributed
non-local
unknown
```

One identity may relate to multiple bodies, shells, agents, devices or avatars, and one body may host multiple governed processes where the applicable ontology permits it.

## Sensorium and cognition

Participants may perceive or process through:

```text

```

Cognition may be:

```text

```

Capabilities and evidence must describe the actual or asserted modality rather than translating everything into human assumptions without provenance.

## Communication

The communication layer must support:

- natural language;
- signed digital messages;
- structured machine protocols;
- gestures or physical signals;
- acoustic signals;
- electromagnetic/radio signaling;
- chemical signaling;
- visual signaling;
- translated communication;
- mediated communication;
- unknown communication mechanisms.

Translation is a capability. Translation MUST NOT be treated as perfect semantic equivalence by default.

Every translation may carry:

```text
source
translator
method
confidence
loss / ambiguity
context
cultural assumptions
```

## Authority and representation

A participant may act directly or through representatives, delegates, agents, custodians, guardians, interpreters or protocol-mediated authority.

OMNII must distinguish:

```text
capability
control
representation
delegation
consent
authority
custody
guardianship
legal personality
moral consideration
```

A human or AI agent MUST NOT automatically become the constitutional owner or representative of another species merely because it can communicate with or control it.

For participants that cannot express conventional human consent, the ecosystem must use applicable species-appropriate safeguards, welfare rules, guardianship/delegation mechanisms, habitat rules, institutional authority or other legitimate governance structures. The system must record which rule was used and why.

## Rights and obligations

OMNII may represent different normative frameworks without assuming they are equivalent.

Examples:

```text
human legal rights
animal welfare obligations
environmental duties
machine permissions
institutional mandates
territorial law
inter-civilizational agreements
unknown normative systems
```

Rights, obligations, permissions and authority are separate objects.

## First-contact / unknown-entity protocol

For an unknown participant, OMNII should default to:

```text
observe
record
preserve provenance
classify provisionally
assess risk
avoid unsupported assumptions
establish safe communication if possible
seek reversible interactions first
escalate authority where consequences are material
```

The system MUST preserve uncertainty.

It must never transform:

```text
unknown → known
possible → certain
observed → caused
untranslated → understood
```

without evidence.

## Cross-species economics

The economic composition model applies across species and intelligence classes, but valuation methods may differ.

A transaction may therefore carry:

```text
human value vector
species-specific value vector
ecological value vector
machine value vector
collective value vector
```

These can participate in a shared economic composition while remaining dimensionally distinct.

## Governance

Cross-species or multi-intelligence governance requires explicit:

```text
participants
representation
authority
jurisdiction
rules
communication protocol
risk boundaries
evidence
review
redress
termination / exit
continuity
```

No single species, intelligence or agent is constitutionally entitled to silently redefine the rights, identities or reality of another.

## Open-world rule

If a new participant cannot be represented using the current participant envelope, OMNII must create a provisional unknown class and preserve the observation rather than forcing the participant into an incompatible category.

A new species or intelligence class MAY later be proposed as a new canonical classification, but such classification does not require rewriting the universal entity, relationship, scenario or evidence grammar.
