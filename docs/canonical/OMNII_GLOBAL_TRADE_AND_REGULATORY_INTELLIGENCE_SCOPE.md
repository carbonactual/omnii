# OMNII — Global Trade & Regulatory Intelligence

**Status:** CANONICAL CAPABILITY SCOPE  
**Scope:** Global-by-default  
**Layer:** Shared trade/compliance intelligence

## Definition

Global Trade & Regulatory Intelligence is the reusable capability for understanding jurisdictional rules and changing requirements that affect trade, market access, products, services, people, assets and cross-border execution.

## Scope

Covers imports, exports, tariffs, duties, taxes, customs processes, classification, origin, valuation, permits, licences, product restrictions, standards, certification, documentation, sanctions/restrictions where applicable, market-entry conditions and regulatory change.

## Jurisdiction model

Every rule carries jurisdiction, authority/source, effective date, expiry where applicable, scope, affected objects and evidence. Jurisdictions may be compared, but legal treatment remains context-specific.

## Intelligence lifecycle

```text
source → capture → normalize → cross-check → interpret → monitor change → notify → regulated handoff
```

Contradictions and uncertainty remain visible.

## Compliance pathway

For a proposed route, identify required classifications, documents, approvals, testing, certifications, taxes/duties and responsible authorities without impersonating those authorities.

## Change intelligence

Detect changes in rules, rates, restrictions, standards, licensing and procedures and identify affected sourcing, opportunity, market and logistics paths.

## Market-entry comparison

Compare possible jurisdictions and routes using market access, compliance burden, trade friction, total value, timing and risk.

## Interfaces

Connects to Research, Market Intelligence, Sourcing, Opportunity, Matching, Verification/Trust, Standards/Conformance, Network/Corridor, Finance/Funding, TIP/IO and regulated handoffs.

## Boundary

It is not a regulator, customs authority, tax authority, legal authority or licensed compliance professional. It provides evidence-backed intelligence and routing into the correct authority/provider.

## Canonical invariant

> **Trade & Regulatory Intelligence makes jurisdictional requirements intelligible and actionable without replacing the authorities that create or enforce them.**
