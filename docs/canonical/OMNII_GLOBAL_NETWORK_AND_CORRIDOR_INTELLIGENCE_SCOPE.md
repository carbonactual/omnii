# OMNII — Global Network & Corridor Intelligence

**Status:** CANONICAL CAPABILITY SCOPE  
**Scope:** Global-by-default  
**Layer:** Shared ecosystem network intelligence

## Definition

Global Network & Corridor Intelligence is the reusable capability for understanding how goods, people, services, information and value can move through physical and digital networks, and for comparing, monitoring and improving those pathways.

## Scope

Covers roads, rail, ports, airports, maritime lanes, air freight, waterways, warehouses, hubs, border crossings, transfer points, digital corridors, consolidation/deconsolidation nodes, carriers, modes and route dependencies.

## Intelligence

Track observed and inferred cost, time, capacity, congestion, reliability, disruption, failed routes, seasonal effects, backhaul availability and route concentration.

## Route optimization

Compare local, regional, national, continental and global paths against declared objectives including cost, speed, resilience, risk, quality, carbon/environmental factors where measured and total value.

## Disruption

Detect closures, congestion, delays, capacity loss, border friction and dependency failures; produce alternatives without silently executing regulated or contractual changes.

## Backhaul and utilization

Identify empty or underused return capacity and compatible demand while preserving authorization, item lineage and safety constraints.

## Evidence

Every dynamic condition carries source, observation time, validity/freshness, geographic scope, confidence and provenance. Unknown and stale are explicit states.

## Interfaces

Consumes and contributes to Global Sourcing, Global Opportunity, Matching/Discovery, Market Intelligence, Resource/Capacity Intelligence, Trade/Regulatory Intelligence, Charter, TIP/IO, fulfillment, evidence and Pulse.

## Boundary

It is not a carrier, freight forwarder, customs authority, transport regulator or universal routing oracle. Regulated execution and contractual commitments remain with authorized parties.

## Canonical invariant

> **Network Intelligence explains and optimizes possible paths through the world's physical and digital networks without becoming the network operator or regulator.**
