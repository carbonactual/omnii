# CANON.md — HAPi World Supreme Law

**Status:** Canonical  
**Authority:** Supreme constitutional law of HAPi World  
**Scope:** Every object, actor, agent, organization, capability, transaction, value movement, representation, and process operating inside HAPi World

## 1. HAPi World

HAPi World is the whole operating universe of the Carbon Actual ecosystem: a 1:1 inspected mirror of Earth, using Earth's clock and geography as its temporal and spatial reference frame.

The ecosystem does not exist as a second speculative universe detached from reality. It is a governed, inspectable representation of the real world and its authorized digital activity.

## 2. Know Before Use

Nothing enters HAPi World for operational use without first being known and verified to the degree required by its risk and purpose.

Knowledge precedes use. Verification precedes trust. Provenance accompanies use.

Unknown, unverifiable, contradictory, stale, revoked, or unauthorized objects must not be silently promoted to trusted operational state.

## 3. Human Authority

The human is the sole administrator of the ecosystem.

AI entities, agents, organizations, services, and automated processes operate only within authority explicitly granted to them. SealGrant is the governing mechanism for delegated authority.

AI may perceive, reason, recommend, compose, simulate, plan, and execute permitted work, but it does not create constitutional authority for itself and does not independently move human value without the required human consent and authorization.

## 4. Single Canonical Object

There is no intentional redundancy in HAPi World.

A real-world or ecosystem object exists once as its canonical identity and state. Products, views, indexes, tokens, fractions, ledgers, registries, dashboards, APIs, and other representations reference the canonical object rather than silently creating a competing copy with independent truth.

Representations may differ in purpose, precision, accessibility, or rights, but they do not create a second underlying reality.

Duplicate facts must reconcile to one canonical source. Conflicting representations are evidence requiring resolution, not permission to fork truth.

## 5. Value and Economic Integrity

HAPi Bank is the financial spine of HAPi World. Financial state is governed through one coherent value model spanning banking infrastructure, value infrastructure including the IOLedger, and economic intelligence.

Value movement must be recorded, attributable, reconcilable, and governed. A representation of value does not by itself create authority to transfer that value.

Full-cost accounting considers the resources consumed or committed by an action, including time, energy, distance, wear, depreciation, capacity, and other material cost appropriate to the object or activity.

## 6. No Waste

HAPi World operates under the Pulse-Ash-Phoenix discipline.

- **Pulse** records the value signal emitted by activity.
- **Asset formation** occurs when activity produces recognized value under the governing economic model.
- **Ash** records value that cannot be productively retained and represents recoverable waste/security intelligence where applicable.
- **Phoenix** is the destructive security response for malicious, prohibited, or irrecoverably unsafe input.

Waste is never treated as a hidden duplicate resource. Recovery, reuse, conversion, or destruction must remain attributable to the original canonical object or event lineage.

## 7. External Boundary

Movement of value or other consequential state between HAPi World and the real world must pass through an explicitly governed boundary.

Internal ecosystem value, real-world money, physical goods, services, rights, and obligations must not be conflated merely because they are convertible.

## 8. Execution Law

Consequential execution follows the ordered principle:

`know → verify → establish context → assess capability → establish authority → assess mission readiness → obtain required human consent → execute → observe → record → reconcile`

Readiness is not authority. Recommendation is not authorization. A plan is not execution. A token or representation is not the underlying right, asset, liability, obligation, or authority.

Blocked or incomplete work must not execute by bypassing a higher-level gate through a lower-level interface.

## 9. Time and Place

HAPi World uses Earth's authoritative clock and geography as its reference frame. Events, state transitions, value movements, and other temporal or spatial claims must carry appropriate time/place provenance.

## 10. Contradictions and Change

This Canon outranks repository implementation, product conventions, agent preferences, generated output, and informal discussion.

A newer implementation does not override Canon merely because it is newer. Contradictions must be classified, evidenced, resolved according to constitutional authority, and recorded so that the system can reconstruct how the decision was made.

## 11. Implementation Boundary

Repositories, databases, services, agents, models, plugins, providers, and infrastructure are implementation reality. They implement Canon; they do not redefine it.

When implementation reality conflicts with Canon, the implementation is non-conformant until corrected, constrained, or explicitly governed through a constitutional amendment process.

## 12. Universal Ecosystem Principle

Every capability should be reused through the canonical ecosystem substrate before a new specialist implementation is introduced.

Specialization is permitted at the boundary where genuine domain semantics require it. It must not be used to recreate an existing universal or ecosystem capability under a different name merely to duplicate functionality.

## 13. Canonical Test

For any proposed object, capability, workflow, or integration, ask:

1. What is the canonical object or fact?
2. Is it known and verified before use?
3. Who has authority over it?
4. What existing capability or representation can be reused?
5. Does the change create a duplicate truth, ledger, registry, or state?
6. What value, cost, liability, waste, or risk does it introduce?
7. How is it observed, evidenced, reconciled, and recoverable?

Failure to answer these questions is a governance failure, not merely a product-design issue.
