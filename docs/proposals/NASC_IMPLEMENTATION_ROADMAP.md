# NASC Implementation Roadmap

## 1. Composition baseline

Confirm institutional authority model, domain registry map, forms/workflows, permissions and controlled reference-data ownership.

## 2. Pilot journeys

Activate producer/operator registration and the licence-to-renewal lifecycle first. This validates registry, forms, evidence, inspection, workflow, authority, audit, notifications and reporting.

## 3. Regulatory expansion

Add variety/seed submission, laboratory/testing, certification, lot traceability and public verification.

## 4. Compliance and intelligence

Add complaints/incidents/enforcement, reconciliation, KPI/Pulse dashboards, market intelligence and cross-service controls.

## 5. Integrations

Connect institution-approved identity, payments, notifications, laboratories, public verification and other systems through named contracts.

## 6. Adoption and handoff

Train administrators/operators, execute UAT, remediate findings, establish continuity procedures, complete institutional acceptance and hand over the versioned package.

## Reuse loop

Validated NASC patterns become reusable OMNII modules where they have value beyond NASC. Future institutions consume the same modules with their own domain configuration and authority boundaries.
