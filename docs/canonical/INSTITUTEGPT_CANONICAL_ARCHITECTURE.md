# Education Layer → InstituteGPT — Canonical Education Architecture

**Status: CANONICAL DESIGN — 2026-09-06**

## 0. Domain placement

The Education Layer is a **domain boundary of OMNII**, not a competing constitutional or operating architecture.

```text
OMNII constitutional foundation
  ↓
shared canonical services / universal composition / runtime
  ↓
Education Layer
  ├─ InstituteGPT
  ├─ OpenEd Bot
  ├─ Notebook
  └─ Exams
  ↓
education capabilities
  ↓
providers / institutions / deployments / integrations
  ↓
Education Floor / operational reality
```

Education composes existing OMNII identity, authority, knowledge, motion, value, trust, relationship, intent, execution, capability, resource, composition, continuity, integration, event, state, evidence, workflow, policy and audit semantics. It MUST NOT create a competing universal graph, identity system, authority model, runtime, value model or constitutional kernel.

HAPI, Root, Index, Vault, Terminal, Actual, Atlas, IO, Evidence, Pulse and Opportunities remain **shared OMNII architecture**. InstituteGPT consumes and integrates with those functions; it does not own or redefine them.

## 1. Architectural position

`Education Layer → InstituteGPT`

InstituteGPT is a **product of the Education Layer** and is the canonical education GPT/service of the Carbon Actual ecosystem.

The Education Layer is the broader domain boundary. InstituteGPT is its universal product for education, learning, instruction, competency development, certification pathways and lifelong progression. It composes OMNII common capabilities without redefining constitutional primitives.

## 2. Universal education rule

Primary intents such as:

`learn | teach | explain | train | study | practice | prepare | assess competence | develop capability | maintain competence | reskill | upskill | pursue CPD`

route to InstituteGPT.

The originating product, institution, provider or physical-world context remains visible; InstituteGPT owns the **educational interpretation and learner experience**, not the authority of the originating institution or provider.

## 3. Application and onboarding curation

InstituteGPT begins personalization during application/onboarding. It creates a governed learner context and uses it to continuously curate education around what the learner likes, wants, needs and is trying to become.

Relevant context can include:

`interests | goals | education stage | prior knowledge | skills | experience | career direction | subjects | preferred formats | pace | available time | language | accessibility | device/connectivity | location context | budget | provider preferences | credential goals | professional-body requirements | CPD requirements | learning-style preferences | support preferences | lab preferences | project preferences | assessment preferences`

Curation is adaptive, explainable and reversible. Learners may revise their preferences. Preferences are not a license for hidden high-impact decisions.

For children, guardian/educator controls, age suitability, safeguarding, privacy, communications and retention policies apply. Childhood exploration does not silently become an adult employment profile.

## 4. InstituteGPT universal learning and advancement services

InstituteGPT includes:

`learning | courses | programmes | lessons | modules | learning paths | skills | competencies | labs | practice | projects | simulations | experiential learning | CPD | professional learning | badges | certificates | microcredentials | credential pathways | learning wallet | skills passport | pathway builder | education marketplace | provider integrations`

There is **no separate Advance product**. These are native InstituteGPT services/capabilities.

## 5. Universal learning value chain

`discover → apply → onboard → understand learner context → diagnose → advise → curate → set goal → build/revise path → select learning experience → learn → practice → lab/simulation → project → experience → demonstrate → assess → recognize → badge/certificate/credential → portfolio/record → opportunity → work/practice → CPD → renew/recertify → reskill/upskill → lifelong learning`

The path is a graph: branches, loops, stacking, direct entry, recognition of prior learning and re-entry are supported.

## 6. Learning Experience

The fundamental learning unit is a **Learning Experience**, which may contain:

`lesson | reading | media | AI dialogue | teacher instruction | tutoring | exercise | quiz | discussion | lab | simulation | project | fieldwork | mentorship | apprenticeship | internship | assessment`

Courses and programmes are compositions of learning experiences rather than the only form of learning.

## 7. Labs

Labs are a major InstituteGPT capability connecting knowledge to demonstrated capability.

Lab classes include:

`coding | cloud | AI | data | cybersecurity | science | engineering | robotics | electronics | medicine | agriculture | manufacturing | finance | GIS | media | design | language | vocational | field | simulation | VR/AR | digital twin | hardware-connected | remote`

Environments may include sandboxes, browser runtimes, notebooks, containers, virtual machines, cloud workspaces, remote hardware, simulation engines and physical facilities.

Lab outputs may become governed artifacts, evidence, assessment signals or competency demonstrations. Sandbox and simulation outputs do not silently become Actual professional practice or authorization.

## 8. Skills and competency model

`knowledge → skill → competency → practice → evidence → demonstrated competency → achievement → qualification → professional authorization`

Skill states include:

`self_declared | learning | practiced | assessed | demonstrated | externally_verified | expired | superseded`

A course does not automatically prove mastery. A badge does not automatically grant regulated professional authority. Evidence is not authority.

Recognition of prior learning may incorporate formal, informal, workplace, apprenticeship, internship, SIWES, portfolio, project, simulation and supervised-practice evidence where the responsible authority permits it.

## 9. Certification, badges and credentials

InstituteGPT supports:

`participation | completion | open badge | microcredential | certificate | professional certificate | diploma | degree reference | qualification | professional certification | CPD recognition`

InstituteGPT may issue configured ecosystem-native achievements and reference or verify external credentials. External issuers, educational institutions, professional bodies and regulators remain authoritative for their credentials and licences.

Interoperability targets include Open Badges, CLR and W3C Verifiable Credentials, plus applicable national, institutional and professional frameworks.

The authority chain is:

`learning → evidence/demonstration → recognition/credential → professional/legal authorization`

A downstream state MUST NOT be inferred merely because an upstream state exists.

## 10. Learning Wallet and Skills Passport

**Learning Wallet** provides a portable collection/reference of learning activities, assessments, projects, achievements, badges, certificates, credentials and evidence.

**Skills Passport** provides a provenance-aware view of declared, learned, practiced, assessed, demonstrated and externally verified skills/competencies.

Both are InstituteGPT capabilities and may become independent products only if a future architecture establishes a genuinely separate ecosystem boundary.

## 11. Pathway Builder and marketplace

InstituteGPT can construct and continuously revise learning paths from:

`learner context + goals + diagnostics + prerequisites + competencies + learning resources + labs + projects + assessments + credentials + opportunities + renewal requirements`

Marketplace capability discovers and compares native, partner and external learning while retaining provider ownership, source provenance and issuer authority.

The marketplace is a capability inside InstituteGPT, not a replacement for the universal Trade/Value architecture.

Providers are **external participants/integrations**, not Education Layer constitutional objects. No provider, LMS, cloud vendor, AI model or proprietary service is constitutionally indispensable.

## 15. Education product hierarchy

The canonical Education Layer product set is:

```text
Education Layer
├─ InstituteGPT
├─ OpenEd Bot
├─ Notebook
└─ Exams
```

Institutional deployments such as **NOUN Bot** are configurations/deployments of OpenEd Bot, not new universal education products or competing ontologies.

## 16. AI learners and AI education

AI/agents may learn, practice, simulate, benchmark and receive governed capability state through InstituteGPT. AI learning remains distinct from human qualification and does not automatically confer regulated authority.

The AI learning continuum can include:

`AI awareness → AI literacy → prompting → workflows → agents → tool use → MCP → retrieval → evaluation → safety/security → AI product development → AI engineering → research → governance → leadership`

An AI agent's learned state is an AI capability state; it is not automatically a human credential, professional authorization or legal identity claim.

## 17. Opportunity connection

`jobs | internships | apprenticeships | scholarships | fellowships | projects | contracts | research | entrepreneurship | promotion`

InstituteGPT supplies learning/competency context; it does not make the external opportunity decision.

## 20. Governance and authority

`provider creates learning`

`trainer teaches`

`lab provides practice`

`assessment measures`

`exam tests where required`

`issuer recognizes achievement`

`regulator authorizes practice`

`employer decides employment`

InstituteGPT must not impersonate external credential issuers/regulators, convert simulation into authoritative real-world practice without a governed event, expose protected learner/child data without authorization, or make any provider/vendor/cloud/AI model/LMS constitutionally indispensable.

Human authority, institutional mandate and lawful regulatory authority remain authoritative. AI recommendation, optimization, scoring or curation is not itself authority.
