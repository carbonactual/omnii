# Carbon Actual Build Constitution

**Status:** Canonical implementation boundary

## Rule 1 — Build the ecosystem once

Universal capabilities belong to Carbon Actual. Products are branches/compositions.

## Rule 2 — Compose before creating

Before implementing a new capability, search the universal registry and Swarms for an existing capability, adapter or implementation.

## Rule 3 — Integrate before replacing

Existing open-source, free, open-alternative, white-label, SDK, API, agent, hardware and decentralized capabilities should be integrated where they satisfy the contract. Replacement is justified by evidence, not preference.

## Rule 4 — Interface before vendor

Products depend on capability interfaces. Provider-specific implementations live behind adapters whenever practical.

## Rule 5 — Human + intelligence

Human and AI capabilities are complementary. HASH expresses their governed relationship; neither is constitutionally entitled to dominate the other.

## Rule 6 — Measure without surveillance

Value measurement must be purposeful, consent-aware, privacy-preserving and minimized.

## Rule 7 — Every meaningful action produces evidence

Actions generate events and Pulse where measurement is appropriate. Pulse informs Value, governance and learning.

## Rule 8 — No waste

Every output is classified. Unknown is preserved as unknown rather than silently discarded. Residuals become Ash where appropriate.

## Rule 9 — Security is compositional

Every branch inherits identity, authority, privacy, provenance, least privilege, audit, recovery and integration-boundary controls.

## Rule 10 — Physical reality is first-class

A branch may require people, machines, buildings, vehicles, materials, locations, energy or other physical resources. The architecture must model them rather than treating them as external to software.

## Rule 11 — Swarms precede teams

The ecosystem evaluates the capability field before composing a Team. Teams are selected for context and may change as evidence changes.

## Rule 12 — Pulse improves the ecosystem

Execution results feed capability evaluation, Swarm intelligence, curation and future Team composition.

## Rule 13 — Products inherit the universal grammar

Institute GPT, Noun Student Bot, NGIN/RITES, Bunk, Charter, Logistico, Sportist, TIP, Build Labs, Spare, HAPI, HAPI World, HAPI Bank, NAIRE, Marketplace, Capital Block and future branches use the same universal contracts.

## Rule 14 — Unknown is allowed

The architecture must represent things it does not yet understand without corrupting the constitutional model.

## Rule 15 — Freeze invariants, not adapters

Constitutional principles and stable contracts may be frozen. Vendors, chains, models, SDKs, implementation languages and infrastructure may change when evidence supports change.

## Rule 16 — Never claim what has not been proven

Security, economic, mathematical, AI and governance claims must be stated according to evidence. "Quantum-proof", "unbreakable", "optimal" and similar claims require proof or must be treated as goals rather than facts.

## Rule 17 — Every branch must leave the ecosystem stronger

A product is successful not only when it works, but when it creates reusable capabilities, evidence, integrations, knowledge, revenue/value, relationships or improved Swarm intelligence that benefit future composition.

## Rule 18 — Becoming is the forward loop

The ecosystem does not merely record what exists. It uses evidence and capability to curate what can happen next.

## Canonical build formula

```text
CONSTITUTION
 → OBJECTS
 → CAPABILITIES
 → SWARMS
 → TEAMS
 → WORKFLOWS
 → INTEGRATIONS
 → EXECUTION
 → PULSE
 → VALUE
 → LEDGERS / SETTLEMENT
 → LEARNING
 → BECOMING
```

This constitution governs implementation planning while allowing domain-specific engineering decisions beneath it.
