# OMNII — Economic Product Boundaries

## Purpose

Ensure the inverted economic doctrine is shared across every ecosystem product while keeping one canonical economic/value-movement boundary.

| Domain / Product | Primary value contribution | Economic role | Canonical value movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| HAPI | human capability, learning, work, teaching, becoming | creates/enables value | IO |
| Becoming | curation, development, capability matching, potential stewardship | converts unrealized potential into capability/opportunity | IO |
| Atlas | discoverability, reputation, evidence-backed presentation | surfaces value and opportunity | IO |
| Charter | movement, access, transport capability | enables physical movement and continuity | IO |
| Fleet | asset organization, readiness, utilization | increases asset productivity | IO |
| Logistico | goods-flow orchestration | increases goods movement efficiency | IO |
| NAB | registry, biography, provenance, evidence, real-time state | preserves/organizes economic context and reduces uncertainty | IO |
| ABBA | intelligence, coordination, optimization | improves ecosystem decision quality | IO |
| Other products | domain-specific contribution | create/enable/preserve value | IO |

## Shared principles

1. Value is multidimensional.
2. Money is a representation/routing mechanism, not the definition of value.
3. Every product can create Pulse.
4. Pulse is feedback/evidence, not automatically monetary value.
5. Curation is value-creating work.
6. Teaching and enabling others can produce multiplicative value.
7. Assets create stewardship responsibilities.
8. Unresolved liabilities and unmet needs can expose ecosystem opportunity.
9. Potential should not be lost because it is initially immature.
10. Economic settlement remains centralized at the architectural boundary of IO even where products have domain-specific workflows.

## Product economics flow

```text
PRODUCT ACTIVITY
      ↓
CONTRIBUTION
      ↓
EVIDENCE / PULSE
      ↓
VALUE EVALUATION
      ↓
PROGRESSION / ACCESS / SUPPORT
      ↓
VALUE CIRCULATION
      ↓
IO VALUE MOVEMENT
```

## No duplication rule

No product may create its own independent:

- money ledger;
- settlement engine;
- universal value unit;
- progression score;
- economic ontology.

Products may define domain-specific measurements and obligations and pass them into the shared economic model.

## Ecosystem optimization

The economic system should prefer actions that:

- increase capability;
- reduce unnecessary waste;
- preserve continuity;
- convert unused capacity into useful activity;
- resolve unmet needs;
- improve quality and reliability;
- reduce avoidable harm;
- increase learning and reuse;
- strengthen future capability.

## Final boundary

> **Every product creates, enables, preserves, routes or learns from value. IO remains the canonical domain for moving that value.**
