# OMNII Architectural Invariants

These statements are intended to become automated contract tests.

1. Every canonical object has a stable identity and canonical type.
2. Every consequential object has provenance sufficient for audit.
3. Every consequential relationship identifies source, target and relationship type.
4. Authority is scoped, attributable, time/context aware where required, and revocable.
5. Capability never implies authority.
6. Intelligence never implies authority.
7. Orchestration never implies ownership.
8. Planning never implies execution.
9. An action/command does not prove execution occurred.
10. An event records an occurrence; it does not substitute for state.
11. State transitions are attributable and observable.
12. Queries do not mutate canonical state.
13. Every consequential execution is attributable to an actor and authority context.
14. Cross-boundary execution requires explicit compatible authority/agreement.
15. Constitutional kernels do not depend on products.
16. Constitutional kernels do not depend on specific agents.
17. ABBA cannot self-create constitutional authority.
18. ABBA cannot exceed delegated authority.
19. Products cannot redefine canonical object semantics.
20. Phase 21–40 extensions cannot redefine the canonical graph.
21. Dependencies are semantically distinct from ordinary relationships.
22. Registry sharding cannot create competing semantic definitions.
23. Economic transactions are auditable and attributable.
24. Value, money, resource, asset, ownership, contribution, price and cost remain semantically distinct.
25. Historical architecture may remain for provenance but cannot silently compete with canonical sources.
26. A future/horizon component is not implementation-ready solely because source code exists.
27. A missing phase implementation is reported as a gap rather than inferred into existence.
28. Unknown things are representable without forced classification.
29. A name, namespace, address, DID, ENS/HNS resolution, credential or token does not become the underlying identity merely by resolving to it.
30. Resolution evidence does not itself grant authority, ownership, custody or consent.
31. Multiple classifications and mappings may coexist; conflict must be explicit rather than silently collapsed.
32. Ontology extension does not silently rewrite historical records; supersession preserves historical semantics.
33. A newly introduced capability, resource or value dimension must remain independently measurable and traceable before aggregation.
34. Fractionalization, decimalization, democratization, tokenization and minting remain distinct operations.
35. Minting, token transfer, settlement or blockchain confirmation does not constitute value creation or truth.
36. Blockchain/DLT is an optional infrastructure/settlement mechanism and cannot become a constitutional prerequisite.
37. Multi-species, artificial, collective and unknown participants use the same open-world substrate without human-default semantics being silently imposed.
38. Representation, delegation, agency, rights, welfare and authority remain distinct dimensions.
39. Observation, evidence, claim, inference, hypothesis, verification and truth remain distinct epistemic states.
40. Emergent patterns may become provisional objects without requiring prior design-time enumeration.
41. An ontology extension must declare provenance, lifecycle and compatibility boundaries.
42. A new external standard may be mapped into OMNII without becoming OMNII's semantic authority.
43. Reversal, compensation, irreversibility, failure and recovery are distinct outcome semantics.
44. Every consequential economic composition records its value-given and returned/Pulse vectors independently where applicable.
45. Asset/liability classification is an outcome of the defined value comparison, not a synonym for the underlying resource or ownership state.
46. Ecosystem aggregate value does not erase the individual vectors from which it is derived.
47. Safe Haven, Floor and Average are benchmark states, not substitutes for individual valuation.
48. Products are selectable compositions of constitutional capabilities; a product may not require users to adopt its private ontology.
49. The ecosystem must be able to represent a novel entity, relation, resource, capability, event, phenomenon, namespace or economic primitive without constitutional-core rewrite.
50. No extension may silently grant legal personhood, rights, authority, consent, ownership or economic value merely by being registered.
