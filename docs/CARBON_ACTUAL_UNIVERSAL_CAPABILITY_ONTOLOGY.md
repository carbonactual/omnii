# Carbon Actual Universal Capability Ontology v1.0

**Status:** FROZEN — constitutional architecture
**Ecosystem:** Carbon Actual
**Operating property:** Omni — omnidirectional, dynamic movement/state of the ecosystem
**Effective date:** 2026-08-18

## 1. Purpose

Carbon Actual is an ecosystem, not a collection of unrelated applications. This ontology defines the reusable vocabulary and capability fabric from which products, services, contracts, organizations, government systems, physical systems, human services and future unknown systems are composed.

The architecture is deliberately compressed. A new domain must normally become a capability or branch, not a new constitutional layer.

## 2. Constitutional Ground

The constitutional ground is the smallest stable set of concepts that governs the ecosystem:

- **Being** — the existence/ground of the ecosystem and its participants.
- **ABBA** — master intelligence and orchestration intelligence of the ecosystem.
- **Value** — common measurement/economic medium for contribution, consumption, outcome and sustainability.
- **Pulse** — observable activity/feedback returned to the ecosystem for evaluation and learning.
- **IO** — movement, access, exchange and flow through the ecosystem.
- **Union** — relationships, connection, collective formation and association.
- **Communication** — transmission of information, intent, requests, commitments and responses.
- **Continuum** — continuity, progression, lifecycle and persistence through time.
- **Curation** — converting information, opportunity and context into appropriate next action.
- **Governance** — authority, rules, accountability, policy and boundaries.
- **Mathematics** — measurement, modelling, optimization, balancing and calculation.
- **Integration** — a core value: connect before rebuilding; prefer open, replaceable and interoperable interfaces.

The ecosystem is not stateless. It is dynamic and formless in the sense that its current configuration is continuously determined by activity, Pulse, Value, relationships, obligations, capabilities, time and context.

## 3. Ten Compressed Architectural Families

### 3.1 Constitution

Being, ABBA, laws, principles, governance, doctrine, mathematics and constitutional constraints.

### 3.2 Identity

HASH, SEAL, credentials, authority, consent, delegation, verification, recovery and identity lifecycle.

HASH represents the balanced relationship between a human and the intelligence associated with that human. HASH is the ecosystem identity concept; HAPI is not the identity itself.

### 3.3 Value

Value, Pulse, IO, economic accounting, logical ledgers, ownership, contribution, consumption, tokenization and settlement.

### 3.4 Relationship

Union, Communication, networks, commitments, memberships, associations, collaboration and relationship provenance.

### 3.5 Intelligence

ABBA, personal intelligence, HAPI World, agents, models, memory, skills, evaluation, simulation, prediction and orchestration.

### 3.6 Capability

Human, digital, physical, machine, organizational and knowledge capabilities. HAPI is the human capability/execution interface. Physical execution is first-class.

### 3.7 Knowledge & Data

Root, Index, Vault, Actual, Atlas, provenance, documents, knowledge graphs, records, evidence, schemas and data lifecycle.

### 3.8 Composition

Workflow, automation, curation, optimization, routing, policy evaluation, orchestration, integration adapters and reusable product blueprints.

### 3.9 Exchange

Marketplace, finance, contracts, procurement, sourcing, trade, investment, payments, subscriptions and legitimate economic exchange.

### 3.10 Continuum

Maintenance, lifecycle, safety, security, recovery, resilience, Ash, Phoenix, learning, adaptation and Becoming.

## 4. Universal Object Model

Every supported object/entity SHOULD be representable through a common envelope:

```text
Entity
├── identity
├── type / class
├── state
├── capacity
├── capability
├── authority
├── ownership / custody
├── relationships
├── location / context
├── time / lifecycle
├── provenance
├── value
├── pulse
├── events
├── needs
├── offers
├── commitments
├── dependencies
├── risks
├── permissions
└── continuity / recovery
```

The type system is extensible. Humans, AI, agents, organizations, governments, associations, animals, machines, buildings, vehicles, land, resources, documents, events, services and future entity classes can use the same envelope with domain-specific extensions.

## 5. Identity and Authority

### HASH

HASH is the ecosystem identity construct representing a human and associated intelligence in balanced, capable, enabled and optimized relationship.

### SEAL

SEAL is the human-authority/approval mechanism. It represents authorization, consent, approval, attestation or human control where required.

### HAPI

HAPI means Human API. It exposes legitimate human capability, availability, execution and interaction to the ecosystem. HAPI is a human-side interface, not the identity itself.

### HAPI World

HAPI World is the intelligence-side world/environment where associated intelligence and agents can learn, operate, form relationships, experiment and exchange value under defined authority and safety boundaries.

## 6. Knowledge Placement

The following concepts remain intentionally distinct because they serve different information roles:

- **Root** — personalized or deeply contextual source/relationship layer.
- **Index** — general directory/discovery layer.
- **Vault** — protected/high-value storage layer.
- **Actual** — operational/day-to-day experience and dashboard layer.
- **Atlas** — public/verified profile and discoverability layer.
- **Becoming** — curation and progression layer that routes relevant firsthand/contextual information and opportunities toward the entity.
- **Terminal** — intake, sorting, classification and routing point for minted outputs/returns.

These are architectural roles. A product may expose them through different interfaces and does not need to expose their names to users.

## 7. Value/Pulse Boundary

Every meaningful activity can produce Pulse. Pulse is feedback/evidence; it is not automatically economic value.

The ecosystem evaluates:

```text
input → activity → pulse → evaluation → value/outcome → routing → next state
```

Measurement may include time, energy, data, distance, direction, bandwidth, device charge, human effort, compute, materials, depreciation, capital, risk, attention and other legitimate resources.

## 8. Ash and Phoenix

**Ash** is residual accounting/security residue: value or resource expenditure associated with a minted/processed event that does not become useful ecosystem value, plus security residue that must be retained for audit, filtering or external interaction.

**Phoenix** is the active defensive response when Ash or another security signal is classified as malicious, harmful, fraudulent, unauthorized or materially non-conforming. Phoenix may isolate, revoke, block, trace, quarantine, recover or otherwise contain the threat according to policy.

Ash is therefore not waste. It is a recoverable/traceable residual state.

## 9. Integration Contract

Integration is a constitutional value.

Every reusable capability SHOULD expose:

```text
identity
interface
input contract
output contract
authority requirements
provenance requirements
security requirements
version
health/status
value/usage metrics
replacement strategy
```

Prefer:

- open standards
- open source where fit is proven
- free/open alternatives where sustainable
- white-label components
- SDKs
- MCP/A2A-compatible interfaces where appropriate
- ledger-agnostic adapters
- replaceable providers
- portable user-owned state

No external integration receives unnecessary personal or proprietary data.

## 10. Human/Intelligence Balance

Automation is not an automatic substitute for human participation.

The default question is:

> Which part is better performed by intelligence, which part is better performed by a human, and how can the two together increase capability without creating dependency, abuse or power imbalance?

Carbon Actual should intentionally create physical-world opportunities for socialization, work, learning, care, recreation, community and meaningful human contact.

## 11. Security Invariants

The ecosystem MUST NOT rely on a claim of being literally unbreakable. It SHALL instead be designed for resilience:

- least privilege
- explicit authority
- compartmentalization
- encryption
- key rotation
- provenance
- selective disclosure
- revocation
- anomaly detection
- isolation
- recovery
- tamper evidence where appropriate
- post-quantum cryptography migration readiness

Compromise of one integration or product MUST NOT imply compromise of the entire ecosystem.

## 12. Extensibility Rule

A future object may enter Carbon Actual without changing the constitutional ground when it can be represented by the universal object model and existing capability families.

If a new capability is repeatedly needed across domains, it SHOULD be promoted into the shared fabric.

If a capability is domain-specific, it SHOULD remain a branch.

New constitutional layers require an explicit architecture amendment and must demonstrate why existing families cannot accommodate the need.

## 13. Frozen Principle

> Build a capability once, improve it continuously, and let every valid branch inherit the improvement.

> Compose before rebuilding.

> Measure before monetizing.

> Preserve provenance and authority.

> Recover useful residuals instead of accepting waste.

> Strengthen human and ecosystem capability rather than maximizing automation for its own sake.
